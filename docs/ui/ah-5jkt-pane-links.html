Clickable things in the panes — how much they should announce themselves

The second half of ah-5jkt. A skill or item named in the unit pane, and a product, market good or structure named in the region pane, opens the game-data dictionary (ah-5jkt.1) already on that entry.

The question is how visible the links should be. These panes are dense — a unit's skills and items, a region's products and both market lists — and almost every noun in them becomes clickable. Colour them all and the pane turns blue; colour none and nobody discovers the feature.

The item marked with a dashed outline in each is shown as it looks under the pointer.
