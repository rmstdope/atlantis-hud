Question A — the footer sentence

The foot is one line of standing explanation, like the merged-reports panel's "What these factions saw is on your map. Their units are shown, and cannot be ordered."

CandidateSentenceAgainst it
A1 (mine)A newer sheet from the same faction replaces the one you hold. Says nothing about what a sheet is for — but it is the only thing that is true on the day this ships.
A2A sheet is used by the study planner. The wording from the mockup you chose — but the study planner is ah-lyg6.2 and is not built. This sentence would be false for at least one release.
A3These mages will be planned alongside your own once the study planner lands. Honest about both, and the longest; it also dates itself and has to be changed by ah-lyg6.2.

Question B — Escape, while the confirm is showing

Every other header popover closes on Escape (usePopoverDismiss). The confirm adds a second thing Escape could mean.

CandidateWhat Escape doesAgainst it
B1 (mine)First Escape cancels the confirm and leaves the popover open; a second Escape closes the popover. Escape means two things depending on state — but it always means "back out of the innermost thing", which is what it means everywhere else in this app.
B2Escape always closes the whole popover; the confirm is abandoned with it. One rule, no state to reason about — but a keyboard user who armed the wrong row has to reopen the popover and find their place again.

Forget buttonLabelled for screen readers forget Creeping Death (17)'s mage sheet. Arming a second row while one is armed moves the confirm to that row.
Forgetting the last oneThe popover and the chip both go; focus returns to the faction chip beside where it was.
FocusOpening focuses the popover frame; Cancel takes focus when the confirm appears; closing returns focus to the chip. All of it is what ChipPopover already does.
PersistenceForget is a real deletion in the game's store, so it survives a reload. The chip follows the game you switch to and is empty in a game with no sheets.
No shortcut keyThe chip earns no keyboard shortcut of its own; it is reached the way the merged chip is.
