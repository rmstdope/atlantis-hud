Magic study tree — B: by branch

The same 70 skills, but grouped by which first-step skill they descend from. Each card is one branch of study: take WEAT and the weather spells open up; take NECR and the undead do. Indentation inside a card is depth, so a chain reads top to bottom.

The grouping is derived from the data, not hand-written — a skill's branch is the first-step skill on its own prerequisite path. Where a skill also needs something from another branch, that shows on the right of its row in violet, e.g. +INVI 3, instead of drawing a line across the page. Scroll inside the frame below.

  • Reads at a glance. Every branch fits a card; nothing needs panning to be understood.
  • Reflows: three columns on a wide window, one on a narrow one. No layout engine, no edge routing.
  • The grouping is computed, so a ruleset refresh that adds a skill places it without anyone editing a table.
  • A skill appears once, in one branch, even when it genuinely belongs to two. The violet chip states the other requirement but does not draw it.
  • Distance from a foundation is only indentation within a card — harder to compare across branches than in A.
