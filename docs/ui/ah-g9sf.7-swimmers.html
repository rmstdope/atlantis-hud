Units that can swim — the agreed shape

In New Age: Trident a unit may swim. A lizardman wades into the coastal sea beside it; a unit riding sea creatures strong enough to bear its whole weight rides out into the deep. Atlantis HUD knew none of it: every unit that was not flying and was not a fleet was stopped at the water's edge and told it needed a ship. Every panel below is what the player sees after the change. Nothing changes in New Origins, which has no swimming rule, and nothing changes for fleets, which the rule exempts.

Agreed, and this is the record. A unit's detail gains a fourth capacity tile, Swim, and the sentence beneath it names Swim alongside Walk. In a game without swimming the tile is absent altogether, not zero. A refusal at the water's edge says which of three reasons it is, and each reason has two sentences — one for water in the way, one for water the player clicked on. A wet step is marked · swimming. Where the map cannot tell whether water is shallow, the planner refuses and says the doubt out loud. A report silent about swimming still plans dry routes.

1 The unit knows it can swim, and says so

Swimming is not a speed — a lizardman still walks two hexes — so the movement badge in the unit list is untouched. It is a fourth capacity, and it sits with the other three.

4 What is not known is refused, and said

Two things can be unknown: whether a sea hex is shallow, which depends on hexes the faction may never have seen, and what the unit can carry while swimming, which an older report may not state. Neither is guessed at.

What is left alone. Fleets, which the rule exempts. New Origins, in every respect. The unit list and its movement badge. The map, which paints nothing new and hides nothing. The region's problem list, which has never remarked on a hand-typed march into water. The panel's fields, their order, its buttons, and where focus lands. Rules this rests on, looked up rather than recalled: Trident rules/movement_normal — “Swimming units are restricted to coastal ocean regions and lakes. Ships are not affected by this restriction.” Trident data/lizardman — weight 10, walking capacity 5, swimming capacity 5. Trident data/giant turtle — weight 50, swimming capacity 20.
