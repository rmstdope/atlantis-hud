ah-g7ts — a foreign guard can block your TAX, and one word decides everything

“Units which have the Guard flag set (using the GUARD order) will block TAX orders issued by other factions in the same region, unless you have declared the faction in question Friendly.”

Observed in neworigins-3.0.0-g3-f42-t1.rep, mountain (36,4): a foreign City Guard (14) of The Guardsmen, on guard, 120 leaders. Our unit ordering TAX or PILLAGE there is credited in full with no hint of the guard.

The data is all there — ReportUnit.on_guard, .own, .faction_id, and the header's DeclaredAttitudes with its default and per-level faction lists. So the bead's guess that this “is almost certainly a doubt” may be too pessimistic. It turns entirely on who “you” is in that sentence.

The two readings

 What it means, and what we can compute
R1 — “you” is us, the taxer The guard blocks unless we have declared them Friendly. Our own report prints exactly that, in Declared Attitudes. So in most hexes we can say certainly whether we are blocked — a real zero, like ah-cxxa's pillaged hex — and only fall back to a doubt when the guard conceals its faction or the report has no attitudes block.
R2 — “you” is them, the guard's owner The rules are addressed to the reader, and the guarding faction is the one doing the declaring. Their attitude toward us is not in our report and never will be. Then every foreign guard in a hex we tax is an unknowable, and the only honest answer is a doubt, every time.
The readings are not close: R1 gives an exact figure in the common case, R2 gives ? on every hex containing a foreign guard — and city hexes almost always contain one, because City Guards are on guard by definition. This is a rules question, not a preference, and you play the game.
