ah-jo6b — eight orders the ITEMS ledger refuses while the SILVER column prices them

Two walks answer for one unit’s month. The ITEMS ledger (orders/semantics.rs) follows what a unit holds; the SILVER column (orders/silver.rs::forecast_unit) follows what it is worth. Measured on 9dfae80f, eight orders make one of them stop and the other carry on — so the two cells beside each other in one row describe different months.

What each surface does when it cannot follow a line. The ITEMS cell keeps its figure and adds a dim + ?, and the hover says “and more that cannot be counted: …”. The SILVER cell does the opposite: any doubt, of any kind, blanks the whole month to a dim ? — “This month cannot be added up, so what moved this unit’s silver is not listed.” That all-or-nothing rule is a shipped decision (silverFigure in UnitTableDock.tsx: “Never a number that might be wrong”), and it is what makes the question below worth your minute.
