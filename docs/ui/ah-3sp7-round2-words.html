Round two — the words as they will ship, and the states the happy path hides

You chose all twelve classes, a warning only where the game refuses, and a hover that names the class. Everything below is the wording of those two, written out exactly, with the cases each has to survive. Whatever you pick here is what the implementer writes; it will not be asked again.

1 — the refusal finding, in all four of its states

Unit 900 holds twenty lions, three wolves and one skeleton. Lions and wolves say This item cannot be given to other units.; the skeleton does not.

WORDING A — name what is refused, and what is left
unit 900
GIVE 901 ALL MONSTERS
line 2 — 20 lions and 3 wolves cannot be given away, so this order moves only the skeleton.
state: nothing at all can move (no skeleton)
line 2 — 20 lions and 3 wolves cannot be given away, so this order moves nothing.
state: the same order written as a TAKE
unit 901
TAKE FROM 900 ALL MONSTERS
line 2 — 20 lions and 3 wolves cannot be taken from another unit, so this order moves only the skeleton.
state: the unit holds none of that class at all

Nothing is said. Nothing was refused; there was simply nothing there.

  • Says what was refused and what survives, so the row below it is explained rather than merely odd.
  • Two shapes of the sentence ("only the skeleton" / "nothing"), and a list that grows with the holding.
WORDING B — one sentence, no list
line 2 — some of this unit's monsters cannot be given away, so this order moves only part of them.
state: nothing at all can move
line 2 — this unit's monsters cannot be given away, so this order moves nothing.
  • Short, and never grows with the holding.
  • "some" and "part of them" leave the player to work out which, which is the whole thing they wanted to know.
WORDING C — name the rule, not the items
line 2 — lions and wolves cannot be given to another unit. This order moves only the skeleton.
state: nothing at all can move
line 2 — lions and wolves cannot be given to another unit. This order moves nothing.
  • Names the items without their counts, so the sentence is stable as the stack changes.
  • Two short sentences rather than one long one; reads well when the list is long.
  • Loses the counts, which are what tell a player how much they were about to lose.

2 — is it a warning, or an error?

The order is valid: the game accepts it and executes it, just with less effect than the player probably meant. It is not a syntax mistake and the server will not reject it.

4 — giving away centaurs

A centaur is people and a mount, so GIVE 901 ALL MOUNTS moves men: the MEN cell falls, the receiver's rises, and the receiver's skills are diluted by the arrivals exactly as a gift of men already does. Unit 900 holds 10 centaurs, 4 horses and knows lumberjack 3.
