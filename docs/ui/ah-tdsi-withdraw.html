ah-tdsi — WITHDRAW spends the faction's fund, not the unit's silver

The rules are explicit: “WITHDRAW [item] — Use unclaimed funds to acquire basic items that you need. If you do not have sufficient unclaimed, or if you try withdraw any other than a basic item, an error will be given.”

Both surfaces charge the unit instead. forecast_unit adds the cost to the same expense accumulator as BUY and GIVE; the ledger's apply charges the unit's silver. Nothing anywhere debits the fund.

Two visible consequences. A withdrawing unit is shown with less silver than it will really have, and can be warned not-enough-silver for money it was never going to spend — a shipped test pins a unit warned it is “one silver short” of a $369 withdrawal. And a faction that empties its fund through WITHDRAW is never warned, because total_claimed counts Intent::Claim alone.

The shipped claims-exceed-unclaimed check sums Intent::Claim alone, so today this is silent. Three ways to fix it:

 What the player reads
2a — widen the shipped check, reword your units claim and withdraw $670 between them and the faction has $500
One check for one fund. The Settings row's description widens with it.
2b — a second, separate code claims-exceed-unclaimed stays exactly as it is, and a new withdrawals-exceed-unclaimed covers the rest. Two rows in Settings, and a faction doing both gets two findings about one fund.
2c — widen it, keep the wording your units claim $670 between them and the faction has $500 — says “claim” of money that was withdrawn. Smallest change, and it tells a small lie.

Who gets named

The shipped check names every claiming unit, each on its own CLAIM line, for a stated reason: “Every claiming unit is named because none of them is more at fault than the others — the message says the total, so a unit reading it is not being blamed for the overrun on its own.”

Unit 101, on its CLAIM lineyour units claim and withdraw $670 between them and the faction has $500
Unit 102, on its WITHDRAW lineyour units claim and withdraw $670 between them and the faction has $500
Extending that to withdrawing units is the consistent reading. The alternative is to name only the claimers, which would leave the withdrawal — often the larger sum — unmarked in the Problems panel, where a player goes to fix it.

“The ruleset does not say what withdrawing costs.” Doubted the unit's expense. The unit has no withdrawal expense any more — so a withdrawal the ruleset cannot price no longer makes the unit unpriceable. That is a strict improvement for the column.

But the fact does not go away: an unpriced withdrawal means the faction's total is unknown, so the fund check cannot be trusted.

4a — stay silentThe check simply does not fire, exactly as it declines a report whose header states no fund. Absence of a number is not evidence of an overrun.
4b — say soA finding on the withdrawing unit: the ruleset does not price this withdrawal, so your unclaimed fund cannot be checked. Honest about a check that has silently switched off.

Out of scope, and worth saying

The ledger charges the silver but never credits the withdrawn item — a unit that withdraws 10 grain does not gain 10 grain in the model. That is a real second gap and it is not this bead; fixing the payment side does not make it worse.
