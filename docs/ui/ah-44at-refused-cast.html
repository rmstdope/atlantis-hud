A cast that cannot be paid for — chosen design

A refused cast reads like a refused study: the missing material ends at a red minus figure with one red sentence, the materials the caster holds stay where they are, and what the cast would have made is struck through with the reason. Agreed 2026-10-16: Q1 A, Q2 A, Q3 B, Q4 A, Q5 A, Q6 A, Q7 A.

The case. In desert (0,48), Ivanhoe (683) knows summon wind 3 and holds 75 ironwood. He orders CAST Summon_Wind, which needs 75 floater hides and 75 ironwood. The only hides in the hex belong to Brian (1159), who is not sharing — so the game refuses the cast and takes nothing.
