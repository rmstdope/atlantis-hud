ah-z27v — B1 · Own row, with arrow

BThe tag moves off the FORM line onto a row of its own directly beneath it, starting at the left margin. It is not a line of orders — it has no line number, cannot be typed into, and the caret steps over it. Same rule always, whether the line is long or short, like the side notes in the unit popups.

When the tag first appears (or goes away) the lines below move down (or up) by one row.

The tag is beneath the FORM line, even when there would be room beside it.
