Question 1 — where a battle-derived skill shows

The fact this bead rests on. A region sighting never discloses another faction's skills — 0 of 2915 foreign unit blocks in the committed reports carry a Skills: section. A battle roster does, explicitly, and only these five: combat, riding, tactics, longbow, crossbow — for both sides of any battle you could see, including battles you were not in.

How much it recovers, counted over the committed reports. In a battle-heavy turn, a lot: turn 72 of g7-f95 has 252 foreign units on the map and battle rosters carrying skills for 143 of them. In a quiet turn, nothing at all — 14 of the 26 reports have no battle section. Across one game's stored turns, 522 distinct units picked up skills this way.

And it is already retrievable. Every imported turn keeps its raw report text (imported_turns.raw_report), and all 26 committed reports re-parse in 0.20 s. So a scan back over the turns you already have costs a fraction of a second and needs no new storage — that part is settled and is not what this page asks.

What this page asks: a foreign unit's skills can now be known. Where should that show? The three options are cumulative — each contains the one before it.

A — in the export only

Nothing on screen changes. The units table still says not disclosed for every foreign unit. The battle file simply goes out with skills in it, and the export dialog's notice changes to say so.

B — in the export, and in the units table's Skills column   recommended

The Skills cell fills in for any foreign unit a battle has told us about, marked so it cannot be mistaken for a skill list we were actually shown. not disclosed stays for the rest. Everything in A as well.
