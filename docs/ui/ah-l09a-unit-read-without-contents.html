ah-l09a — a unit read without its contents

Decided 2026-09-09, over three rounds. Atlantis HUD reads a turn report line by line. If a unit's line is laid out even slightly differently from what the game engine normally produces — a report trimmed by hand, one from another client, one wrapped at a different width — it can read the unit's name perfectly and quietly lose everything the unit was carrying. The unit appears on the map with no men and no equipment and nothing anywhere says so, which makes every number built on it confidently wrong: what it can carry, what it can study, what it pays in taxes, what it is worth in a fight.

* Drones (9498), Borg (21), revealing faction, 100 gnolls [GNOL], 170 swords [SWOR].

  read as →  Drones (9498) — 0 men, nothing carried.        <-- everything lost, silently
  or as   →  Drones (9498) — 100 gnolls.                    <-- the swords alone lost, silently

The decisions: the unit stays on the map and says its contents could not be read rather than showing zero; any shortfall counts, not only a total loss; it is counted in the existing ⚠ N to check chip and listed on the panel's Not read tab, whose closing sentence widens; the unit refuses to show a figure it does not have, while keeping anything that genuinely was read; every total containing such a unit becomes a floor; and Atlantis HUD stops advising about that unit altogether, saying why once.

Keyboard, focus and persistence unchanged

  • The chip, the tabs and the panel behave exactly as they do today. No new shortcut, no new focus rule.
  • A marked unit is selected, opened and moved through like any other unit. Nothing about it is disabled.
  • Nothing is dismissible and nothing is remembered: it is rebuilt from the loaded report, follows a turn switch, and comes back on reload. Load a correctly wrapped report of the same turn and every mark disappears.

Rejected and why, so nobody re-opens them

ConsideredRejected because
Drop the unit entirely and list its line like any other unreadable lineA unit you own simply is not there, which you only notice if you already know it should be. Its region, its structure and its orders lose their reference to it too.
Reuse the existing “about N” / ~ estimated-people marker“about 0” is a strange thing to read, it says nothing at all about lost equipment, and you would only find out by opening that unit.
Report only a unit that lost everythingThe dangerous case is the partial one: 100 gnolls and no swords looks like a perfectly ordinary scout company. That is exactly the failure that was reported.
Split the panel into “Lost entirely” and “Read in part” groupsRows would stop being in plain file order, which was the one rule that needs no explaining when you go and check the report against the panel.
Drop the panel footer and repeat the consequence on every rowA long list then repeats “None of this reached the map.” on nearly every row.
Show a figure marked as a floor on the unit itself — “0 — at least”Easy to skim past, and indistinguishable at a glance from a unit that really does hold nothing. A blank cannot be mistaken for zero.
Let totals silently add up what they haveThat is the reported failure one level up: a confident total that is quietly wrong.
Let totals refuse a figure entirelyOne badly read unit would blank a number you were relying on for the other twenty.
Keep advising, marked unreliableWarnings that are certainly meaningless — “no men” on a unit whose men were simply not read — would appear every turn and have to be dismissed by hand.
Withhold only the advice that depends on the unit's contentsWhich advice depends on contents is a judgement made warning by warning, and nothing on screen would tell you which rule you were looking at.
Hide the part-read unit's known items tooThe two horses were genuinely read, and the “Not read” panel would become the only place to see them.

Round 4–6 the money, the total, and the withheld advice

Decided 2026-09-09. The break takes a tail, so a garbled unit loses more than its items: silver, skills, weight, capacity, movement and flags all go with it. Atlantis HUD reads a missing silver figure as a confident 0, which is the very failure this piece of work exists to stop, one level down and in the money. These four screens settle where all of that lands.

The withheld advice — a banner on the unit's own pane

There is no per-unit advice area in Atlantis HUD: advice is per hex, in the Problems tab. The unit's pane does have a banner slot, which a stale unit already uses — so both facts go there in one sentence, and nothing is added to the Problems list. An unread unit is not a fault in your orders, and a badly wrapped report would fill that list.

One word, everywhere

not known is the single phrase for a figure that is missing, in every place it appears: the unit's pane, the units table, the hover card and the silver ledger. not read was considered and dropped — two words for one fact in the same pane, and in a Silver column it could be misread as “you have not read it”. The banner carries the reason once, so the figures do not have to.
