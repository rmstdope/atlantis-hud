Cross-faction teaching: the forecast says what will happen

When a mage plans to teach someone from another faction, the Planner puts the result in the student's schedule cell. A confirmed teaching bonus, a study that will proceed normally, and a result that cannot be forecast are distinct at a glance. The detail is available by hovering or focusing the highlighted cell.
