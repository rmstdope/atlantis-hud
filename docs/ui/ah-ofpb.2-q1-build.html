Q1 — what a BUILD puts in the ITEMS column

A unit ordered BUILD spends material on the structure it is working on, and its ITEMS cell does not move today. Unlike a PRODUCE, nothing arrives — a structure is not an item — so the whole of the change is stock leaving.

How much leaves is knowable. One unit of work needs one man-month by a character with the building skill and one unit of the material; a higher level works faster in proportion. So ten men with building 3 do 30 units of work and spend 30 wood — unless the unit’s own stock, or the structure’s remaining need, stops it sooner.

The three rows below are the three shapes that takes. A Stockade costs 60 wood and wants building 1.

unit 5541 ; Builders, 10 humans, 120 wood, building 3, in Stockade [4] (needs 45) build ; 10 men x level 3 = 30 work; 30 wood leaves unit 5542 ; Hodmen, 10 humans, 15 wood, building 3, same Stockade [4] build ; men could do 30; only 15 wood to do it with unit 5543 ; Finishers, 10 humans, 120 wood, building 3, in Stockade [7] (needs 6) build ; men could do 30; the structure only wants 6 more

  • The column keeps one meaning: everything in it is what the unit ends the month holding.
  • The two caps are the whole reason a player would distrust the figure, and both are said out loud, in the cell the figure is in.
  • Same shape as a PRODUCE, which already says “Includes 10 WOOD this unit will produce…” and repeats its cap.
  • Three new sentences to word and keep in step.
  • Unit 5542 — all its wood gone — drops WOOD from the cell entirely, which is a large visual change for a routine order.

B2 — the material leaves, and nothing is said

Exactly what a GIVE, a BUY or a SELL does today: the cell moves, the hover carries only the was: line.

hover on unit 5543's ITEMS cellwas: 10 HUMN, 120 WOOD
  • Nothing new to word at all, and the ITEMS hover stays a single simple idea.
  • Consistent with the four orders the column has shown since it was built.
  • Unit 5543 spends 6 wood where its men could do 30, and nowhere in the application says why. The player is left to work out for themselves that the Stockade is nearly finished.
  • Unlike a BUY, the figure here is derived — from a skill level, a headcount and a remaining need. A derived figure with no explanation is a figure the player has to either trust blindly or recompute by hand.

B3 — nothing moves, and the cell is marked

A BUILD is treated as something the column declines to count: the cell keeps the report’s list, upright, with the + ? mark it already uses for a month it could not fully account for.

IDNAMEITEMS
*5541Builders10 HUMN, 120 WOOD + ?
*5542Hodmen10 HUMN, 15 WOOD + ?
*5543Finishers10 HUMN, 120 WOOD + ?
hover on unit 5541's ITEMS cellwas: 10 HUMN, 120 WOOD and more that cannot be counted: build
  • Never wrong, and nothing new to word — the mark and its sentence already exist.
  • Honest about the one case the app really cannot settle: several units helping one structure between them may spend more than it still needs, and each is counted alone.
  • The commonest building unit in the game — alone, inside its own structure, with plenty of stone — is one the app knows the answer for exactly. Marking it unknown makes the mark mean “deliberately withheld”.
  • Leaves the bead with almost nothing in it: the column would still show the pre-turn figure for every builder.
Rules: rules/economy_buildings — “each unit of work on a building requires a unit of the required resource and a man-month of work by a character with the appropriate skill and level; higher skill levels allow work to proceed faster (still using one unit of the required resource per unit of work done)”; rules/skills — “a unit with level 2 in a skill is twice as good as a unit with level 1”; data/Stockade — 60 wood, building 1. The hovers above name the structure as Building 4 because the report gives these no name of their own (+ Building [4] : Stockade, needs 45.) — the exact wording is a later question, not this one. The ITEMS column does not track silver, and a BUILD costs none. The builds this column cannot settle at all — a structure built from wood or stone, BUILD HELP, an unfinished ship — are Q2, on its own page.
