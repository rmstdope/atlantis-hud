Shipping when one end moves — measured once the moves are made

The agreed behaviour. Every unit moves before any goods are shipped, so a shipment is measured from where the sender and the quartermaster stand once the month’s moves are made, not from where the report found them.

Agreed: the distance is measured from where both units stand once the month’s moves are made, and the refusal keeps the sentence already agreed, word for word (1). When the forecast cannot name where a moving unit ends the month, the distance is measured from where the report shows it, and nothing extra is said (2). A refused shipment is listed in the hex the sender is listed in on the report, even when it moves away (3).

This is a drawing, not a running screen. Trident’s rules/sequenceofevents moves every unit (MOVE, ADVANCE, SAIL) before any TRANSPORT is processed, so goods are sent from, and received in, the hex a unit is standing in once the month’s moves are made. Reaches are from rules/economy_transport: any unit may send to a quartermaster within 2 hexes; sending within 2 hexes is free. Sentences are quoted exactly as they would ship.
