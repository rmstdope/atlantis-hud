ah-dpvh — A: the popup carries it alone

A unit already at its skill ceiling still writes STUDY — usually because the report's own orders template wrote @study comb for it. The forecast today projects 5 (480) in the next-turn blue, which reads as a month well spent; it is a month that buys nothing. This page is variant A of three — open all three before choosing.

The scene. Five own units in one hex, all writing a month-long STUDY. Six of Two (13402) is a leader at COMB 5 (450) whose report template writes @study comb — and data/LEAD says a leader stops at level 5. This is the bead's unit: the month buys nothing. Braves (1487) studies combat from level 2 and will reach 3 — an ordinary forecast, and nothing here may change it. Ore Gang (1774) are hill dwarves, capped at combat 2 and already there. Hunters (1901) is a leader at MAGI 5 studying magic, capped and short of the fee — two warnings at once. Watch (1620) studies nothing.

A — the row is untouched; the popup gains one amber sentence

Nothing on the table changes. The Skills cell of every studying unit stays italic brass, exactly as ah-qig3 left it, and the chain still draws next turn's figure in the selection blue. The only new thing is a warning line at the foot of the popup, in the same amber the fee-short and headcount doubts already use.
