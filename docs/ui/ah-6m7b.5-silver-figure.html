The old mockup drew today's line as silver  1000 → 1216 — a figure that already moves with the tax and the wage. It does not. Today the ITEMS column moves a unit's silver only for a GIVE, a TAKE and a TRANSPORT. A tax, a wage, a purchase, a study fee and a cast fee leave it untouched, on purpose: orders/effects.rs:5292 pins it in as many words — "the ITEMS column shows silver for every other unit that BUYs — the spend belongs to the SILVER column" (ah-agbm).

So the line has two halves and they are governed by different rules. Naming the seven causes fixes the sentence. What should happen to the figure beside it is the question.
