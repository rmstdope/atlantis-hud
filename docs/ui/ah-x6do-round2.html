ah-x6do — round 2: the edges of the new advisory

Agreed last round: the ITEMS column stops inventing the goods, and a new advisory of its own — its own Settings row, on by default — says why. Two edges of it are still open, and both are things a person sees.

The sentence it will ship with, unless you say otherwise: “only basic items can be withdrawn, and silver is not one” — the item named the way the player wrote it is named everywhere else in these messages, in lower case.

Question 1 a WITHDRAW naming something the catalogue has never heard of

WITHDRAW 5 SLIVER — a typo, or an item from another world's catalogue. Today the preview is silent about it everywhere: the order resolves to nothing, no goods arrive, no warning is raised. It is a different case from silver: silver is a real item the game refuses to withdraw, whereas this is a word nothing recognises.
