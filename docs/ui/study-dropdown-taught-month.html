What this choice costs

  • Green never lies. Choosing a green row really does buy the doubled month, this turn, with nothing else to change.
  • It hides opportunities. Sable is sitting idle beside Ereb and could double his pattern for nothing — the dropdown will not say so, and the player must notice it himself.
  • Order of work matters. The player must plan the teacher before the pupil, or see no green at all.
