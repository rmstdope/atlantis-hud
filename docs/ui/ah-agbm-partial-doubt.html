Q2 — when one of a unit's orders cannot be counted

Some orders cannot be projected at all: BUY ALL HORS (how many depends on every other thing the month spends), a BUY of goods this market does not sell, GIVE 5512 ALL ITEMS (a whole class), a TAKE FROM 999 ALL SILV where 999 is not in this hex. The question is what that does to the rest of the unit's orders.

The situation on this page. Traders (5530) holds 300 SILV, 40 FUR, 6 HERB. It is given three orders. Two can be counted exactly — the market wants fur at $32, and the gift is a plain quantity to a unit standing right here. One cannot be counted at all.

unit 5530
  sell 40 FUR
  buy all HORS
  give 5512 2 HERB

Option A — per order: count what can be counted, say nothing about the rest

This is exactly what GIVE does today: an order that cannot be read changes nothing, and the unit's other orders still apply.

IDNAMEITEMS
*5530Traders1580 SILV, 4 HERB
hoverwas: 300 SILV, 40 FUR, 6 HERB
  • The two orders that can be checked are checked. The sale and the gift both read correctly.
  • It is the rule the column already follows, so nothing about GIVE changes.
  • Nothing on the row says a horse is missing. The player sees a confident italic figure that silently omits one of their three orders.

Option B — per unit: one unreadable order silences the whole cell

The cell falls back to the report, upright. This is the rule the silver ledger already uses internally: a unit whose orders touch something unpriceable is not judged at all.

  • Never shows a projection with a hole in it. What is italic is always complete.
  • The unit vanishes from the column's usefulness entirely: the sale of 40 fur is perfectly knowable and is not shown.
  • One BUY ALL anywhere in a unit's block blanks everything else it does — and the row looks identical to a unit with no orders at all.

Option C — per order, and the cell admits the gap

Everything countable is counted, and a trailing marker says part of the month could not be. The hover names the order.

IDNAMEITEMS
*5530Traders1580 SILV, 4 HERB + ?
hoverwas: 300 SILV, 40 FUR, 6 HERB
and more that cannot be counted: buy all HORS
  • Keeps everything Option A gets right, and stops the figure claiming to be the whole month.
  • The + ? matches the Silver column's existing ? for a figure it will not guess at.
  • A new mark in a dense column, on a row that is already italic. It has to be quiet enough not to shout and visible enough to be seen.
  • The exact wording of that hover line is Q5, next round, if this option wins.
