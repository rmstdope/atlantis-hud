ah-qkhk — The unit pane's Events section

Chosen: Variant C3 — the unit's own event lines are shown in the unit pane itself, uncapped, as the last section of the panel, with one link into the turn report's Events tab. Variant A (open the report filtered to that unit) and Variant B (open the report scrolled to that unit's group and outlined) were both rejected: they move the player somewhere to read three lines that fit where they were already looking. Within C, capping the list at three and putting the section above Movement were rejected too — the panel body already scrolls, so putting the section last costs a long list nothing and never displaces Movement, Flags, Skills or Items. The interview is recorded in bead ah-qkhk's design field.

Before this, reaching the events the engine printed for the unit you were looking at meant opening the turn report, switching to Events, and reading down a list of every event in the report. The reverse direction already worked: a unit named in the events list is a link back to the unit.
