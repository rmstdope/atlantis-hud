Round 2 — what the rules really say a CAST creates

You were right, and my Q3 page was wrong on the facts. The data page states the output of every creating spell, with its tag and its quantity, in the skill's own level paragraph. The scraper reads only the cost clause and throws the rest away (packages/ruleset/src/data.ts: one regex for via magic at a cost of …, one for the transmutation pairs, and nothing else). So the app's ruleset genuinely cannot name an output today — but the source it was built from can.

Reading all 25 priced spells, they fall into two families that want opposite treatment, and no amount of scraping merges them.

Family A — certain, and scaled by the mage's level

Seven spells. The mage makes an exact number, every time, and pays per item made.

  • Every figure is knowable: the mage's level is in the report and the holdings cap the rest.
  • The app charges these wrongly today. cast() charges the stated cost once — 200 silver, one sword — however high the level. A level-3 amulet maker really spends 600 silver and the SILVER column says 200. A level-3 sword enchanter really spends 15 swords and the app charges 1.
  • Transmutation is under-counted too: an unnumbered CAST Transmutation mithril makes 2 × level, and the code counts 1 by explicit admission.

What it costs to get there

Family A needs three things that do not exist:

  • The scraper must read the creation sentence: the output tag, the multiplier (5 times their level, their level, 2 … times the skill level) and whether it is certain or a chance. Today readCastCost reads the cost clause and the transmutation pairs and nothing else.
  • The ruleset type must carry it, in Rust and in the generated TypeScript.
  • The ledger must charge the level-scaled cost rather than the flat one — which changes the SILVER column and which units the shipped not-enough-silver and not-enough-items warnings fire on. That is a correctness fix worth having, and it is not a change the ITEMS column should smuggle in.
Every quotation is from config/public/ruleset.json, scraped from the NewOrigins data page on 2026-08-25. Family membership was read off all 25 spells that state a casting cost; EARL, PHEN and every other costless spell states no item and is untouched either way. The ITEMS column does not track silver (ah-agbm): the 600 silver a ring costs shows in the SILVER column, and unit 8803's SILV entry stays where the report left it. That is why its cell is upright with only the mark.
