Practice from a planned cast

The study planner counts the 5 days of practice a planned cast gives. Only the exceptions show in the cell: an arrow when the days go to a prerequisite instead of the spell, a dim struck-through tag when they go nowhere. Numbers read the month's end, practice included, and every later turn starts from them. A battle spell is counted as if he fights; only the words say so.

1 · The grid

Ereb readies fire for battle on turn 26 while force is still 1, so the 5 days go to force (65). His force study on turn 27 therefore reaches level 2 (95) a turn earlier. On turn 28 force is 2, so fire practises itself: studied and practised, fire ends the month at 1(65). Sable's farsight is at 5, the highest: no gain, dim. Her bird lore cast on turn 27 is amber as agreed before — she won't know it.
