ah-z73s.3 — what the crew warning says when men arrive

One sentence in the Problems panel changes shape. Today it has two forms, and a comment in the code says a third can never occur — because men arriving silenced the whole check. Once they no longer do, it can.

The sentence today

Problems
⚠ 9508 Crew — Raft [218] is short of sailors: 2 sailing levels aboard less 1 given away this month, it needs 2, so it will not sail.
⚠ 11125 Crew — Longship [329] is short of sailors: 2 sailing levels aboard, it needs 4, so it will not sail.
The first is a unit that gave a sailor away this month; the second is a crew that simply is not big enough and moved nobody. Nothing about either of those two cases changes in this bead, whichever option you pick below — unless you pick S.

The three cases this bead makes reachable

A Raft states Sailors: 2/2; unit 9508 aboard it is two gnolls who each know sailing 1 (30 points each). A Longship states Sailors: 4/4; unit 11125 aboard it is one man at sailing 1.

Why the “drop” case is the one that matters. Today it is silent, and after this bead it warns — about a fleet whose report says it has exactly the crew it needs. The player is looking at a number the report never printed, so whatever the sentence says has to be enough to make sense of that on its own.

Open all four and compare the middle row — that is where they differ

P — say nothing new: the plain sentence covers everything that is not a give-away
Problems
drop Raft [218] is short of sailors: 0 sailing levels aboard, it needs 2, so it will not sail.
rise Longship [329] is short of sailors: 2 sailing levels aboard, it needs 4, so it will not sail.
both Raft [218] is short of sailors: 0 sailing levels aboard, it needs 2, so it will not sail.
gave away … 2 sailing levels aboard less 1 given away this month, it needs 2 … (unchanged)
  • Not one string is added or changed anywhere. The smallest possible change to the panel.
  • The “rise” row reads perfectly well as it is — the fleet is short, and by how much is what it says.
  • The “drop” row says 0 about a raft the report says has 2 of the 2 it needs, and offers nothing to explain the gap. That is the case this bead exists to start warning about.
Q — one new clause for every arrival: “once this month's transfers of men have run”
Problems
drop Raft [218] is short of sailors: 2 sailing levels aboard, 0 once this month's transfers of men have run, it needs 2, so it will not sail.
rise Longship [329] is short of sailors: 1 sailing levels aboard, 2 once this month's transfers of men have run, it needs 4, so it will not sail.
both Raft [218] is short of sailors: 2 sailing levels aboard, 0 once this month's transfers of men have run, it needs 2, so it will not sail.
gave away … 2 sailing levels aboard less 1 given away this month, it needs 2 … (unchanged)
  • One new string, used for all three cases. Always shows the report's figure beside the one being judged, so the contradiction is on the screen rather than left to the player to notice.
  • The clause is true of the “both” case without having to describe two movements at once.
  • “1 sailing levels” — the sentence has never had a plural rule and this option does not add one. It is already like that today in less 1 given away.
  • Does not say why the number fell. A player who gave two peasants aboard may not connect “transfers of men” with a skill being diluted.
R — name the men, in two shapes: joining leaves fewer, or joining brings more
Problems
drop Raft [218] is short of sailors: 2 sailing levels aboard, but the 2 men joining it this month leave 0, it needs 2, so it will not sail.
rise Longship [329] is short of sailors: 2 sailing levels aboard with the man joining it this month, it needs 4, so it will not sail.
both Raft [218] is short of sailors: 2 sailing levels aboard, but the 2 men joining it this month leave 0, it needs 2, so it will not sail.
gave away … 2 sailing levels aboard less 1 given away this month, it needs 2 … (unchanged)
  • The only option that explains the surprise: unskilled men joining a skilled crew make it worse, which is not obvious and is exactly what rules/give says happens.
  • Two new strings, and the count of men joining is a number the sentence did not carry before.
  • In the “both” case it says only half of what moved — a gnoll also left, and the sentence does not mention it.
  • Four shapes in one message now, which is the most any finding in the panel has.
S — one shape for every changed crew, replacing the give-away sentence too
Problems
drop Raft [218] is short of sailors: 2 sailing levels aboard become 0 once this month's orders have run, it needs 2, so it will not sail.
rise Longship [329] is short of sailors: 1 sailing levels aboard become 2 once this month's orders have run, it needs 4, so it will not sail.
both Raft [218] is short of sailors: 2 sailing levels aboard become 0 once this month's orders have run, it needs 2, so it will not sail.
gave away Raft [218] is short of sailors: 2 sailing levels aboard become 1 once this month's orders have run, it needs 2, so it will not sail. (changed)
  • Two shapes in total instead of four: “nothing moved” and “this is what it becomes”. The simplest rule to hold in your head and the simplest to test.
  • It rewrites a sentence that has already shipped and been verified (ah-j0e, two verification failures paid for that wording), and it loses “given away”, which named the cause.

Things I have already decided, so you do not have to

  • No new warning and no new switch. This is the existing fleet-undercrewed finding, on the existing toggle, pointing at the same SAIL line.
  • One unit aboard that cannot be judged still silences the whole fleet's crew count. A fleet is crewed by everyone in the hull, so a hole in one unit is a hole in the total.
  • The overload half is untouched and goes on warning even when the crew half is silent.
