When the month is uncertain the popup draws no lines and shows a sentence instead. Today: “A faction-mate's silver in this hex pays for this unit's orders.” In this option, for a loan across a sailing:

Several lenders

If two sharers where the fleet lands pay the fee between them, the passenger's line names both: “by Drones (9508) and Drones (9579), where its fleet lands in ocean (38,44)”.
