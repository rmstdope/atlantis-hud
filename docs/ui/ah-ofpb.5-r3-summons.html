Q10 — the control caps

Two of the four state a cap on how many the mage may control at once: wolves, level² × 4; eagles, (level−2)² × 2. SUSK and RAIS state no cap — they state an 8% chance of decay per turn instead.

A month’s summon never breaches its own cap — I checked the arithmetic at every level; 2n is always at or below the cap. It only bites on a mage that has been summoning for a while: a level 3 wolf master already holding 30 wolves (cap 36) would show 31-42.

What I cannot look up: whether the engine refuses to summon past the cap, or summons and lets the excess break free. Neither page says, and it is not in the arithmetic you gave me.

S1 — one sentence for everything. Includes 1-12 WOLF this unit will create by casting.
  • One string, one code path, nothing to choose between.
  • “create by casting” for a wolf.

  • Reads the way the rules read.
  • A second string, and a rule for which skills are summons — the ruleset does not mark them, so it would be a list of nine tags in the code.

  • One string, and true of a wolf and a runesword alike.
  • Vaguer than either, and diverges from the PRODUCE sentence’s shape.
