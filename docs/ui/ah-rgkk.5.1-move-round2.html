L — what are the three capacity lines called?

Same panel, same figures, same sentences. Woodsmen (15189): one wood elf and one horse, Weight: 60. Capacity: 0/70/85/0, given 6 grain by Farmers (1502) and so 90 — over both. The brass label is the capacity that decides: the mode it uses, or, when it is overloaded, the largest of the three, which is the figure its load has to beat before it can move at all.

  • The report’s own three words, in the report’s own order.
  • fly 0 reads as a mode, not as a capacity — it looks like it says the unit is flying zero.
  • Read aloud it is close to meaningless: “fly 0. ride 70. walk 85.”
