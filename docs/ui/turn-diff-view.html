The turn diff view — reading what changed between 70 and 71

Bead ah-jg6.4, the surface of the diff epic. A comparison is already held and signalled in the Turn chip (ah-jg6.3, chosen: 71 ⇄ 70); the differences are already computed (ah-jg6.2: units added/removed/moved/changed with fields named, region changes with "not seen" kept apart from "changed", orders diffs that ignore reflow). This bead is where the player reads them. Two drawn questions — where the view lives and how the three categories are laid out — plus two behavioural ones asked alongside.

Chosen. A dialog in the battles-view frame, its categories behind Units / Regions / Orders tabs with counts in the tab labels (the navigator preferred tabs over the recommended stacked sections). It opens from a Changes chip that appears in the header only while a comparison is on, and clicking a unit or region named in the diff selects it on the map and closes the dialog.
