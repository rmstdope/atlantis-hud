ah-7cdt — faction food pays a unit's upkeep

From GitHub issue #556, reported by a player. Most of that report is already answered by ah-1wcw.4, which charges 10 silver a character and 50 a leader, pays it from the unit's own food first where the unit is set to consume, and values food at 50 — the very rate the reporter said was wrong at 30.

This bead is the one piece left: CONSUME FACTION means a unit eats faction food held by other units in the region — step 2 of the rules' payment order, which ah-1wcw.4 scoped out because ah-1wcw.1 settled that the Silver column counts each unit alone.

1. Food the unit owns  ·  2. Food from faction units in the region  ·  3. Silver the unit holds  ·  4–9. regional and allied

So it is a deliberate exception to a settled rule, for one term, with precedent: the shipped not-enough-silver already pools silver across a hex's sharing units for the same reason.

The pool what is in it, and when

Two passes, in the rules' own order. Every consuming unit spends its own food first (step 1). Only what is left over, across all your units in the hex, forms the pool that step 2 draws on — so the same grain can never feed two units.

Only units flagged consuming faction's food draw on the pool. A unit flagged consuming unit's food eats its own and stops there; a unit consuming nothing pays silver, because step 3 comes before step 5.

When the pool is short the figure is ?, and here is why

Same hex, but the quartermaster holds only 3 grain. Four are needed. The rules waste food — each unit needs whole items, and a partial one is consumed whole — so the total genuinely depends on who eats:

Scouts owe 60 -> needs 2 items      Guards owe 80 -> needs 2 items      pool = 3

  Scouts fed first    Scouts   0    Guards  30    total 30
  Guards fed first    Scouts  10    Guards   0    total 10

Two different, equally defensible answers, and nothing in the report says which the engine will pick. So neither unit gets a number:

{n} is the silver that faction food covered, not the unit's own and not a count of items — the panel is in silver throughout. Note that a unit the pool feeds always ends at an Upkeep of 0: the pool is all-or-nothing, so if it can cover a unit at all it covers the whole remainder. That is precisely why the note earns its place — a zero needs explaining. Both lines join the shared "one note, first match wins" table settled in ah-1wcw.1; the short-pool line is a doubt and therefore sorts with the doubts, above the informational lines.

Optimistic — every consuming unit may draw the whole poolConsistent with TAX, WORK and entertaining, but it says everybody ate when four were needed and three held. Understating a shortfall is the dangerous direction for a column whose negatives you act on.
First come, first fed (report order)The order is ours, not the engine's, so one unit would be shown starving for a reason that exists only inside this app — and reordering the report would change who.
Feed the neediest firstA stated rule rather than an accident of order, but still our invention rather than the engine's.
Pool in silver and ignore the whole-item wasteAlways a number and no ? — but optimistic exactly when the hex is short of food, which is the moment you would want to know.
Share the shortfall across the contending unitsChosen first, then withdrawn: there is no single correct total to share, because the waste is per unit and the total differs by who eats. The arithmetic above is the demonstration.
