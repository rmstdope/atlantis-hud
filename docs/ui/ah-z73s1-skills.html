ah-z73s.1 — skills arriving with given men

A report prints a skill as lumberjack [LUMB] 5 (450), and the figure in brackets is study points per man — I checked that against every skill reading in the fixtures (4022 of them): a level is the largest n with points ≥ 15·n·(n+1), i.e. 30, 90, 180, 300, 450. Because it is per man, and the rules divide months "as evenly as possible among the people in the unit", the giver's cell never changes — it keeps its points per man and therefore its level. Only the receiver merges.

The three cases below are what that arithmetic produces. The question is only about the third one.

Case 1 — a formed unit, the one ah-jw85 named

GIVE NEW 1 5 HUMN from a unit at LUMB 1 (30). The formed unit starts with no men and no skills.

The knowledge is real but spread thin, so the cell gains a skill at level 0. That is a state the report itself prints — the fixtures carry (0, 5), (0, 17) and (0, 25) — so nothing new is being invented, and the existing cell already renders TAG level (points).

Case 3 — the one that needs your decision: the level goes down

GIVE 3100 5 HUMN from a unit at LUMB 1 (30), into 5 men who are LUMB 3 (180).

(5 × 180) + (5 × 30) = 1050 over 10 men = 105 points → level 2. The receiving unit drops from 3 to 2.
Nothing is lost — the unit knows more in total than it did — but it can no longer do what level 3 could, and a player who was reinforcing a skilled unit will not expect that. It is also invisible in the cell itself: LUMB 2 (105) looks like an ordinary predicted value.
