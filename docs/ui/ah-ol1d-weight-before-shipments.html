Shipping to a quartermaster that walks away — which load decides the walk?

Every unit moves before any goods are shipped. So goods shipped to a quartermaster this month cannot stop it walking this month — they arrive after it has gone. Today the map and the unit’s Movement box count those goods as if they were already on its back, decide it is too heavy to move, and so forecast the shipment as delivered from where it stands. Nothing is said.

Agreed: A — the map, the Movement box and the shipment all weigh a unit as it steps off, before any goods shipped this month reach it; the refusal keeps the sentence already agreed. C1 — when the goods that arrive after the walk make the quartermaster too heavy for next month, nothing is said.
