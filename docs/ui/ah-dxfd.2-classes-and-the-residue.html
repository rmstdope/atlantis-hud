ah-dxfd.2 — you were right about the classes

I went and checked instead of assuming. Most of the item classes are classifiable from the data page the app already scrapes — the page states them in its own sentences, one per item, and the scraper simply does not read them. Four are not stated anywhere and cannot be derived at all.

So: ten or eleven of fifteen, and it is worth its own bead. The change runs right through the ruleset contract — the scraper (packages/ruleset/src/data.ts) learns the sentences, config/public/ruleset.json is regenerated, and the reading side (crates/core/src/movement/rules.rs) grows the field to match, since both ends are deny_unknown_fields and must move together. It then pays off in three places at once: the units table's preview, the ledger, and this bead's projection — and it retires SilverDoubt::GivesAWholeClass for ten classes instead of the two ah-dxfd.1 is doing now. I would file it as a bead of its own, at P4 for you to rank, rather than fold it into this one.
