PILLAGE — what it actually requires, and who gets the money

Your verification of ah-dxfd.2 found no pillage warning at all, and you filed ah-q6bt for the overhaul, noting one tension for planning to settle with you. This page is that tension, on your own reproduction hex.

rules/economy_taxingpillaging, verbatim: “Martial factions may also pillage a region. To do this requires the faction to have enough combat ready men in the region to tax half of the available money in the region. The total amount of money that can be pillaged will then be shared out between every combat ready unit that issues the PILLAGE order. The amount of money collected is equal to twice the available tax money.”
Your words on the bead: “Only units that actually issue the PILLAGE order will participate, so those units need to have enough combat ready men between them to carry it out. Units not issuing PILLAGE will not count. The pillaged silver will be divided proportionally between the combat ready men participating.”
Looked up, not recalled — and it decides the predicate. data/sword: “No skill is needed to wield this weapon.” (weapon.needs: null in ruleset.json). So a man holding a sword is combat ready with no Combat skill — which is why City Guards, printed with Skills: none, is legitimately flagged taxing and on guard. Requiring COMB ≥ 1 would be wrong.
