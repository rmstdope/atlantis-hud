Other factions' units in the palette

Chosen. A unit from another faction, standing in a hex the player can see this turn, is found in the command palette by its name or unit number. Its row names the faction beside it (or "Faction not shown"); choosing it goes to its hex and selects it. Faction names are not searched. Order among units: yours, then this turn's others, then remembered. When space runs short the faction shortens first.

Choosing one — chosen A

A — recommended The hex and the unit

Exactly what choosing one of your own units does: the hex is selected and the unit is selected in it, so its details show straight away.

  • The standard way palettes behave: the main text keeps priority, the secondary text gives way.
  • A long faction name can lose its number — "(10)" — at the narrowest widths.

Considered and rejected

Row: a "foreign unit" kind label (says not yours, not whose); colour only (easy to miss, lost on colour-blind players). Search: faction name also finding its units (a large neighbour floods the list; finding a faction is its own piece of work). Choosing: hex only (lands you on a different unit from the one you searched for). Order: remembered before this turn's others. Long row: unit name shortening first (its number is often what you typed).
