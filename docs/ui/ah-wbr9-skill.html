ah-wbr9 — a PRODUCE the unit cannot carry out

Split out of ah-19l2.2, which settled that a unit ordered to produce something it cannot make simply shows expense 0. That is right about the silver and silent about the wasted month, which is what this bead is for.

There is an exact precedent: build-without-skill already says the same thing about BUILD, and its message reads

cannot build a Mine: needs building 1, has no building

So the shape is settled by the house. What is not settled is the wording for an item rather than a structure, what happens when nothing makes the thing, and how far the bead reaches.

Question 1 the message

A unit with carpenter 2 orders @produce catapult. Catapults need carpenter 4.

OptionThe message
A — mirror BUILD cannot produce catapults: needs carpenter 4, has carpenter 2
and with no skill at all: … needs carpenter 4, has no carpenter		 The same sentence the reader already meets for BUILD, word for word in its structure. Nothing new to learn.
B — name the order's own word cannot produce catapult: needs carpenter 4, has carpenter 2 Echoes what the player typed rather than the catalogue's plural. Reads oddly next to the BUILD message, which uses the catalogue's name.
C — lead with the waste this month is wasted: producing catapults needs carpenter 4, this unit has 2 Says the consequence first, which is the thing the player cares about. Diverges from every other advisory's shape.

Question 2 when nothing in the game makes it

@produce quicksilver — no skill produces it, at any level. This is a different mistake from lacking the skill: there is no skill to lack.

OptionWhat shows
A — its own sentence, same code cannot produce quicksilver: no skill produces it One code, two sentences. Catches a typo'd or imagined item, which is a wasted month just the same.
B — silence Nothing. The Silver column already shows ? for it (unpriced-production, from ah-19l2.2). Smallest. A player who mistypes an item gets a ? in a column and no sentence anywhere saying why.

Question 3 how far does the bead reach?

There is a second, probably commoner way a PRODUCE wastes a month: ordering a raw resource the region does not have. Every report states it — Products: 31 grain [GRAI], 35 wood [WOOD], 15 furs [FUR], 7 yew… — so @produce iron in that hex makes nothing.

Option
Skill only This bead does what its title says. The region case is filed unranked as its own bead, with its own wording. Two small beads, each shippable.
Skill and region One bead covering both ways a PRODUCE wastes a month, probably two codes (produce-without-skill, produce-not-here). Larger, and the region case needs its own interview about hexes whose Products line is absent or truncated.
