ah-g9sf.9 — what a leader costs to keep in Trident

In New Origins and New Age: Arcanum a leader's monthly maintenance is 50 silver, and one grain, livestock, fish or meal covers up to 50 of what a unit owes. In New Age: Trident the leader costs 90 and one food covers only 30. An ordinary character is 10 in every world. Sources: newage/trident rules/economy_maintenance, newage/arcanum rules/economy_maintenance, rules/economy_maintenance, newage/trident data/MEAL.

Atlantis HUD charges 50 and credits 50-a-food everywhere, so a Trident player is shown a fatter purse than they will have and is not warned when a leader is about to starve.

Agreed: the figures are corrected and no new words appear anywhere. Every chosen panel below is bordered in green.
