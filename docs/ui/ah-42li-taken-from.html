ah-42li — the unit that is taken from

One hex, two of our units. Buyers (900) writes TAKE FROM 902 ALL SILV and then BUY 2 grain. Purse (902) holds 60 silver and writes no orders at all.

Buyers' own row is handled — it is doubted, and that is a separate bead. Purse's row is simply wrong. It shows 60 silver at month end. Purse will have nothing. The 60 is not a guess we could not make: the Give-phase settlement already computes it and already takes it out of Purse's item holdings (semantics.rs:3043) — the SILVER column just never hears about it.

rules/sequenceofevents settles Give orders third, before tax, before the market, before wages — so what leaves Purse is exactly what the report shows it holding.

The one thing that could make A wrong

Under A the two ends of one transfer disagree: Purse says a flat −60, Buyers still says ?.
