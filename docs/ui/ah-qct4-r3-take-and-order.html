A one-line defect found while proving all this, and it decides whether you would see the fix work at all. item_spellings (crates/core/src/movement/rules.rs:374) strips its plural suffixes with lowercase literals: written.strip_suffix("es") and written.strip_suffix('s'). So “ORCS” strips to nothing, resolves to no catalogue item, and the whole transfer is marked unfollowable. Measured, verbatim: [probe moves] Item text="ORCS" spellings=[Some("ORCS"), None, None] find_item=None, against text="orcs" spellings=[Some("orcs"), None, Some("orc")] find_item=Some(("ORC", "orc")). Consequence: GIVE 14723 ALL ORCS — the spelling your own report invites, since it prints 8 orcs [ORC] — keeps the phantom production even after this bead’s fix. ALL orcs, ALL ORC and ALL MEN all work. The plan folds that one line in, because without it the fix visibly fails on the first thing anyone would type.

Q9 The same sentence when the men are TAKEN, or discarded

Men leave a unit three ways this app follows: the unit GIVEs them, a faction-mate TAKEs them (rules/take: “works just like the GIVE order, except that the direction of transfer is reversed”), or they are discarded with GIVE 0. The agreed wording says “the men it gives away”, which is false for the second and awkward for the third.

Q9·A — one sentence, neutral verb   recommended
unit 901
  TAKE FROM 900 5 ORC

unit 900
  PRODUCE sword
900 — items hoverwas: 8 ORC, 20 IRON, 100 SILV
Includes 3 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month.
This unit has men for 3 swords: GIVE and TAKE resolve before production, so the men that leave it this month do not work for it.
  • True for all three routes, and names both orders the player might have written.
  • One string to write, one to test, one to keep in step with the rules.
  • Very slightly longer, and names TAKE even to a player who wrote a GIVE.
Q9·B — two sentences, one per route
after a GIVE… This unit has men for 3 swords: GIVE resolves before production, so the men it gives away this month do not work for it.
after a TAKE… This unit has men for 3 swords: TAKE resolves before production, so the men taken from it this month do not work for it.
  • Each reads exactly right for the order that caused it.
  • The core must carry which route emptied the unit — a new field on the wire, for a wording difference.
  • A unit that both gives and is taken from in one month has no sentence to show.
Q9·C — keep the agreed wording and say nothing after a TAKE
after a TAKE — items hoverwas: 8 ORC, 20 IRON, 100 SILV
Includes 3 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month.
  • Nothing to decide; the string you already chose ships unchanged.
  • The unit whose men were taken by somebody else is the one whose owner is most likely to be surprised, and it is the one told nothing.

Q10 Where the new sentence sits when the materials also bite

Give 5 of 8 men away and 15 of 20 iron away. The men allow 3 swords, the iron allows 5, so it makes 3 — and both sentences have something to say. The shipped materials sentence is the one that refers to “the 3 its men could make”.

Q10·A — the men sentence first, explaining the number the other one quotes   recommended
Items hoverwas: 8 ORC, 20 IRON, 100 SILV
Includes 3 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month.
This unit has men for 3 swords: GIVE and TAKE resolve before production, so the men that leave it this month do not work for it.
This unit has materials for 5 swords, not the 3 its men could make.
  • Reads in causal order: why there are three men’s worth, then what the iron would have allowed.
  • The materials line then says “5 … not the 3”, which is the larger number first and looks wrong at a glance.
Q10·B — keep the shipped sentence where it is, men sentence after
Items hoverwas: 8 ORC, 20 IRON, 100 SILV
Includes 3 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month.
This unit has materials for 5 swords, not the 3 its men could make.
This unit has men for 3 swords: GIVE and TAKE resolve before production, so the men that leave it this month do not work for it.
  • Nothing already shipped moves; the new line is simply appended to the production block.
  • The reader meets “the 3 its men could make” before anything has explained why it is 3.
Q10·C — only ever one of them, men taking precedence
Items hoverwas: 8 ORC, 20 IRON, 100 SILV
Includes 3 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month.
This unit has men for 3 swords: GIVE and TAKE resolve before production, so the men that leave it this month do not work for it.
  • Shortest hover, and names the limit that actually bound the run.
  • Hides the fact that the iron would have run out at 5 anyway — which matters if the player puts the men back.
