ah-deo5 — the exact words — round 2

Since ah-cw75 and ah-v585, there are four ways a unit's men count toward the pillage threshold: Combat skill 1, a weapon it can wield, a mount it can ride, or a spell that damages enemies. A unit with ready == 0 has none of the four.

The warning's tail still names only one of them:

shipped today cannot pillage here: needs 45 combat ready men, this region has 0 — this unit's 19 men hold no weapons they can wield

True, and partial. A player reads it and buys swords, when one point of Combat skill would have done — or already holds horses and is told nothing about why they do not count.

SETTLED Round 1

  • B — name the nearest miss, with a fallback when there is none.
  • Two near misses → name the one needing the lowest skill level; ties go to the one the unit holds most of.

The house style these must match

Shipped warnings name a skill in plain words, never by tag, and use a fixed shape:

shippedcannot help build a Tower: needs building 1, has no building
shippedcannot produce catapult: needs carpenter 4, has carpenter 2

1 · The mount near miss

19 men, 3 horses, Riding 0.

M1 House shape

warning… this region has 0 — its 3 horses need riding 1, and it has no riding
  • Matches the two shipped sentences above, clause for clause.
  • "has no riding" repeats what "need riding 1" implies.

M2 Trimmed

warning… this region has 0 — its 3 horses need riding 1 to count
  • Shorter, and says what the horses would do for the unit.
  • Departs from the shipped shape, so two patterns exist for one idea.

2 · The weapon near miss

10 men, 10 double bows, no Longbow. The skill a weapon needs is not its own tag — DBOW is wielded with LBOW.

W1 House shape

warning… this region has 0 — its 10 double bows need longbow 1, and it has no longbow
  • Names the skill, not the weapon — the thing to go and study.

W2 Trimmed

warning… this region has 0 — its 10 double bows need longbow 1 to wield
  • Parallel with M2.

3 · The fallback — nothing to nearly qualify

19 men, no items at all. There is no near miss to name.

F1 Name all four

warning… this region has 0 — it has no combat skill, no weapon it can wield, no mount it can ride and no damaging spell
  • Complete; the reader learns every route.
  • Long, and this is the case where the unit is furthest from any of them.

F2 Name the concept

warning… this region has 0 — none of its 19 men can tax
  • Short, and it is the rules' own test.
  • The reader must already know the four ways.

4 · Singular

warning… this region has 0 — its 1 horse needs riding 1 to count

Agreement comes from counted_item, which already pairs a count with an item name correctly (ah-rsdz: 84 of 114 items pluralise irregularly).
