Option A — however small the hex, the battle mark steps down below the coordinates

Beveled Tile, the battle hex, from the closest zoom out to the smallest middle-zoom hex, with the small hexes (all but the first) shown at twice their size so they can be seen. The coordinates are always the same size on screen; the hex and its marks shrink as you zoom out, so the further out you are, the further down the mark has to go. On the smallest hexes it ends up in the middle of the hex, over whatever sits there.

The coordinates and the battle are both readable at every zoom. Cost: on small hexes the battle mark sits over the middle of the hex (here, the settlement medallion's spot).
