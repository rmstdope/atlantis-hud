ah-dpvh — C: the row warns too

Everything variant B does, and one thing more: the Skills cell of a unit whose study cannot raise its level carries the same ⚠ the Silver cell already uses for a figure that needs a second look. The wasted month is then visible while scanning, without hovering anything. This page is variant C of three — open all three before choosing.

The scene. Five own units in one hex, all writing a month-long STUDY. Six of Two (13402) is a leader at COMB 5 (450) whose report template writes @study comb — and data/LEAD says a leader stops at level 5. This is the bead's unit: the month buys nothing. Braves (1487) studies combat from level 2 and will reach 3 — an ordinary forecast, and nothing here may change it. Ore Gang (1774) are hill dwarves, capped at combat 2 and already there. Hunters (1901) is a leader at MAGI 5 studying magic, capped and short of the fee — two warnings at once. Watch (1620) studies nothing.

C — a warning mark on the cell, and the amber forecast from B

Three of the five rows carry the mark: the leader at its ceiling, the hill dwarves at theirs, and the mage who is both capped and short of the fee. Braves, who will actually advance, does not.
