Correction, round 2. Round 1 of this page told you the checks’ reader keeps a second FORM 1 where the ITEMS column drops it, and you chose “swallow the second block” on that basis. That was wrong, and I am sorry: read_formed already swallows a FORM whose alias is taken in that region — the same by_alias guard, in the same words, as the ITEMS reader. Your answer is already the shipped behaviour. The two readers do agree, and FORM cannot mint two units with one id in one hex.

What survives the correction is the rest of the research, and it narrows the bead further rather than widening it. No well‑formed report can print two units with the same number in one hex — 386 hex blocks across all 26 committed fixtures contain not one, and the game numbers units once, game‑wide. A merge of an ally’s report cannot introduce one either; that reader de‑duplicates by unit id already.

So exactly one route is left. The region parser pushes every readable * or - line without ever asking whether it has seen that number before. Paste a report whose region block was duplicated, truncated and re‑appended, or hand‑edited, and two rows for one unit reach the ledger — where they share a single balance, and every one of the dozen unit‑id‑keyed maps beside it, so one purse is read once and charged twice.

A′ Refuse the repeated row
