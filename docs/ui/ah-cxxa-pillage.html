ah-cxxa — a pillage empties the hex for your own taxers too

The rules are unusually blunt: “Note that PILLAGE comes before TAX, so a unit performing TAX will collect no money in that region that month.” Pillaging empties the region for every taxer in it — your own included.

Nothing checks. Both surfaces credit TAX from the region alone and never scan the hex for a PILLAGE. So a player who orders one unit to pillage and three others to tax the same hex is shown four sources of income where the game will pay one.

This is a mistake the client is unusually well placed to catch, because both orders are in the file being edited.

The pillager is unaffected — it collects twice the base, which is what ah-abwx shipped. Its own taxing faction-mates collect nothing. A unit that both could tax and does pillage is not affected either: it is not taxing.

Does anything say so?

Nobody here is short of money — this is income that will not arrive, so not-enough-silver is the wrong check. Three shapes:

 What the player gets
A — a new findingOn each taxing unit's TAX line: a unit is pillaging this hex, so this TAX will collect nothing
A new taxed-a-pillaged-hex code and a Settings row. Reachable from the Problems panel, which is where a player goes to fix orders.
B — a hover note onlyIn the Silver panel: A unit is pillaging this hex, so this unit's TAX will collect nothing.
Explains the zero where it appears. No new code, no Settings row, nothing in the Problems panel.
C — bothThe finding marks the line and the note explains the zero. Two strings for one fact, and the note is redundant once the finding is on the line.
The precedent cuts toward A: ah-1wcw.2 settled that an unsellable SELL earns 0 in the column and the shipped not-traded-here check is what tells the player why. This is the same shape — an order that earns nothing for a reason the report can see.
