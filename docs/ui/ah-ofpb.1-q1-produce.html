Q1 — what a PRODUCE puts in the ITEMS column

The ITEMS column already shows a projected BUY, SELL, WITHDRAW, TAKE and GIVE in italic brass. PRODUCE shows nothing at all — neither the goods made nor the materials spent making them.

The one real tension. Production resolves in the last phase of the month, so goods made this month cannot be spent this month. The app already knows that: the ledger deliberately does not credit what a PRODUCE makes, so a unit that produces swords and then gives them away is still warned. The question is what the column should say, since the column is about what the unit will be holding when the turn is over — not about what it can spend.

Two units, drawn at the real 220 px column width, with the real NewOrigins recipes.

P1 — the goods arrive, and the hover says whenrecommended

The column answers “what will this unit be holding”, in the same italic every other projection uses. One extra hover line keeps the promise honest about when.

*5530Lumberjacks120 SILV, 6 WOOD
*5531Weaponsmiths12 IRON, 350 SILV, 8 SWOR
hover on unit 5531's ITEMS cellwas: 20 IRON, 350 SILV Includes 8 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month.
  • The column keeps one meaning: everything in it is what the unit ends the month holding.
  • The materials leaving are visible too — 20 IRON down to 12 — which is half of what a producing player wants to check.
  • Nothing about the shipped not-enough-items warning moves: the goods are drawn, not credited.
  • One more sentence in a hover that already carries up to three.

P2 — the goods arrive, with nothing said

Identical cells; the hover carries only the was: line it already carries today.

hover on unit 5531's ITEMS cellwas: 20 IRON, 350 SILV
  • Quieter. The column already means “at month end”, so arguably nothing needs saying.
  • A player who writes produce sword then give 5530 8 SWOR sees eight swords in the column and a warning saying it has none — with nothing anywhere explaining why both are true.

P3 — only the materials leave; what is made is not counted

The column stays strictly “what this unit can spend”. The materials go, the goods do not arrive, and the cell admits the gap with the mark + ? that BUY ALL already uses.

IDNAMEITEMS
*5530Lumberjacks120 SILV + ?
*5531Weaponsmiths12 IRON, 350 SILV + ?
hover on unit 5531's ITEMS cellwas: 20 IRON, 350 SILV and more that cannot be counted: produce sword
  • Never claims a unit holds something it cannot yet spend.
  • A lumberjack — the commonest producing unit in the game — loses its whole answer and gains a question mark. The app knows exactly what it makes.
  • The mark stops meaning “something here could not be worked out” and starts meaning “something here was deliberately withheld”.
