ah-1mpx.4 — round 2: the states the happy path hides

Round 1 settled the shape: drag onto an Army or onto + New Army; a bulk line above the table carrying every bulk action; right-click opens the popover that already exists, at the pointer, with a row outside the pick picked alone first.

These four are the cases the three-rows-picked picture never shows — and each of them is a place I would otherwise have to write “the implementer chooses” into the plan.
