Magic study tree — the states, and the words

You chose branch cards as the default, with a whole-graph toggle, static for now (the faction overlay becomes its own bead), MANI shown but set apart, and three doors: the command palette, a mage's skill in the unit pane, and a shortcut of its own.

What follows is everything the happy path hides. Read the wording table at the bottom — those are the strings as they would ship.
