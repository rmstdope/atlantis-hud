ah-rgkk.5.1 — round 1: the Move popup

Resting on the Movement cell today opens a popup with exactly one line in it: move  Riding, with was: Walking beside it when the mode moved. It says which mode the unit travels in. It never says how close it is to losing it, and it never says what this month did to bring it there.

The wire already carries the rest and nothing draws it: UnitMovement is { status, load, fly, ride, walk, capacityMode } — the report’s own Weight: 60. Capacity: 0/70/85/0, recomputed after this month’s orders. rules/movement_normal is what those figures answer: a unit rides if its horses can carry its people and everything else, walks if its people, horses and wagons can, “otherwise the unit cannot issue a MOVE order” — which is what Overloaded means.

Three questions. Q1 — which figures the popup draws. Q2 — what it says about why, given a hard limit spelled out below. Q3 — what it says when the load moved but the mode did not. Everything else in the family is settled and is not re-opened: the heading, the twelve-line cap, the pair notation with green up and red down on the after only, the sr-only sentence as the only non-visual route, the single amber warning line, and Movement not disclosed. for a unit that is not ours.

The scene

Woodsmen (15189) is one of ours: one wood elf and one horse. Its report printed Weight: 60. Capacity: 0/70/85/0 — it cannot fly, its horse can carry 70, and elf-plus-horse can carry 85 on foot. 60 is under 70, so the game gives it the faster of the two and it is Riding. This month Farmers (1502) gives it 6 grain (line 5). Grain weighs 5 (data/GRAI), so its load goes to 90 — over the 85 it could walk with, and over the 70 it could ride with. It is Overloaded, and the game will refuse it a MOVE order this month. Nothing in the app says so except the one word in the cell.

The limit, stated honestly

packages/shared holds no item catalogue, by decision (gameData.ts). So this popup can say what moved — every movement is already itemised with a named cause on unit.itemChanges — but it cannot say which of them were the heavy ones, because it does not know what anything weighs. It can say “received 6 grain”. It cannot say “which added 30 to the load”. Q2 is what we do about that.

Q1 — which figures does the popup draw?

All three are the same unit at the same moment, and all three carry Q2’s P underneath, so the panels differ in one thing only. Ask of each: “how much over is it, and by how much would it have to lighten to move at all?”

Q2 — what does it say about why?

All three are variant A with a different block underneath. The unit also sold 2 furs and bought 1 horse this month, so there is more than one movement to say — which is the case that decides this.

  • A second, different rule about which changes are worth naming.

Q3 — the load moved, the mode did not

Same unit, but Farmers gives it 1 grain rather than six. Its load goes 60 → 65, still under the 70 its horse carries, so it is still Riding. The core records a change only when the mode moves, so today the shared code would print its standard sentence — Nothing this month changes this. — directly above three sentences saying what changed. That is the contradiction to settle.
