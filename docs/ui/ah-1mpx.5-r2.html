ah-1mpx.5 — round 2: the bridge, the words, and what is not known

Round 1 settled the shape: one Other factions entry in the rail, and the dossier popover keeps Attitude and Seen in while losing Known units, gaining a line that sends you to the list. This round is the parts the happy path hides.

The load-bearing one is Q1. “One faction is still one click” was the argument that carried option B — but the click has to land on their units and nobody else's, and the filter box alone cannot promise that.

Q1 — what “Show their 52 units in the list” actually does

The existing filter box is a case-insensitive substring match over unit name, unit number, faction name and structure (unitTable.ts:132, read not recalled). It never sees a faction number. So filtering for the text Thane's Ring also matches a unit called “Thane's Ring”, and any other faction whose name contains it.

Q3 — how an Army says a foreign member's skills are not known

A report never discloses a foreign unit's skills (rules/world_regions: Skills: prints only on your own * lines). So a foreign member sits in an Army with an empty Skills cell and exports with "skills": []. ah-1mpx.3 already says so in the export dialog. The question is whether the Army itself says so too — you assemble it weeks before you export it, and assembling is when you could still pick a different unit.

Q4 — the units whose owner is hidden

61 of 254 foreign units at turn 82 show no faction at all — rules/stealthobs: with Observation only equal to their Stealth you see the unit and not who owns it. They belong to no dossier (dossierFor matches on factionId, so a hidden unit is in none of them) and the popover says so out loud today: “A unit hiding its faction is not counted here.” The new list is the first place they can all be seen together.

Decided without you, and why

  • Columns on this source: the same as All my units — the ten, plus Hex, since it spans hexes. No Seen column (everything here is this turn) and no Remove (nothing to remove from).
  • The pin does not survive leaving the source, a turn load, or a reload. It is a way of looking, not a setting.
  • Own units never appear here, and foreign units never appear in All my units. The two are exact complements, and This hex keeps showing both as it always has.
  • Nothing changes about picking or dragging. ah-1mpx.4 puts no ownership test on either, so a row here drags into an Army like any other.
