ah-vw8e — round 2: the shapes the two-line case hides

Settled in round 1: the second sale is warned about on the existing nothing-left-to-sell code — a third cause, no new switch; the sentence is “an earlier order sells all 10 of its furs, so this sells nothing”; any SELL line can be the subject, not only a SELL … ALL; and a line the market ran out under stays silent, because region-pool-oversubscribed already speaks there.

Four things follow from that and none of them are in the two-line picture.

Q1 — widening the subject also wakes the two causes already shipped

Today the warning fires only when the line that sells nothing is a SELL … ALL. Dropping that restriction is what round 1 asked for — but the restriction is on the subject, not on the cause, so dropping it changes the gift and take causes too. This month is silent today and would start speaking:

holds 10 furs; gives them away, then sells a stated ten

unit 4411 GIVE 5512 10 FUR SELL 10 FUR
nothing-left-to-sell this unit gives away all 10 of its furs before the market opens, so this sells nothing

Written SELL ALL FUR the same month already says exactly this. The question is whether the stated form should join it, or whether only the new cause widens and the two shipped ones stay ALL-only.

Q2 — three lines, two of them dead

option 1 — every dead line is underlined

unit 4411 SELL ALL FUR SELL ALL FUR SELL ALL FUR
nothing-left-to-sell an earlier order sells all 10 of its furs, so this sells nothing
nothing-left-to-sell an earlier order sells all 10 of its furs, so this sells nothing

Each line is separately editable, and the player deleting only the middle one is left with a block that still has a dead line in it. Two identical sentences in the list.

option 2 — only the first dead line is underlined

unit 4411 SELL ALL FUR SELL ALL FUR SELL ALL FUR
nothing-left-to-sell an earlier order sells all 10 of its furs, so this sells nothing

One sentence, no repetition — but delete the underlined line and the warning simply reappears on the next one, which reads like the editor moving the goalposts.

Q3 — when a gift and an earlier sale both emptied it

The shipped rule is that a gift of the unit's own beats a take by somebody else, because the unit's own line is the one the player can edit. Here both candidates are the player's own lines, so that tie-breaker says nothing.

holds 10 furs: gives 5 away, sells the other 5, then sells again

unit 4411 GIVE 5512 5 FUR SELL ALL FUR SELL ALL FUR
nothing-left-to-sell naming the sale
an earlier order sells all 10 of its furs, so this sells nothing

…but only 5 were sold; the sentence's count would have to be the 5 the sale actually took, or the sentence has to say something else.

nothing-left-to-sell naming the gift
this unit gives away all 10 of its furs before the market opens, so this sells nothing

…which is plainly wrong here: it gave away 5, not 10.

nothing-left-to-sell naming neither
nothing is left by the time this line runs, so it sells nothing

True in every mixed month, and says less.

Q4 — the count, when more than one line sold

The sentence chosen in round 1 names a number. With one earlier sale it is unambiguous. With two it is not: below, 6 went on the first line and 4 on the second.

holds 10 furs

unit 4411 SELL 6 FUR SELL ALL FUR SELL ALL FUR
C1 — the total sold, plural verb
earlier orders sell all 10 of its furs, so this sells nothing
C2 — the total sold, singular kept
an earlier order sells all 10 of its furs, so this sells nothing
C3 — no count at all when more than one line sold
earlier orders sell everything it holds, so this sells nothing

Whichever is chosen, the single-earlier-line case keeps round 1's exact sentence, and a unit holding one still reads its only fur.
