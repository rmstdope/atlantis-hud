ah-z73s.2 — the level an existing warning quotes

Once the checks read the skills a unit's orders leave it, an existing warning can quote a level that is not the one the report shows. This page is about whether those sentences should say so.

A correction to the first mockup, before you read on

I described "an estimated headcount" wrongly. I wrote that a report says approximately 50 men and that this is "not rare on a large faction". That is not what the flag means. A unit's headcount is a guess only until the item catalogue has been fetched and recognises everything that unit holds; with a current ruleset loaded it is exact for essentially every unit. So the silence you chose is much narrower than I made it sound — it is close to "no ruleset yet", which is already a state in which most warnings do not run. That makes your answer safer rather than more costly, so I have not re-asked it; but the record should be right.

The situation

Unit 2200 is at LUMB 3 (180) with 5 men, and is told to produce something that needs lumberjack 3. Five men who know no lumberjack are given to it in the same month.

unit 2200
  PRODUCE IRON              ; say this needs lumberjack 3
unit 1010
  GIVE 2200 5 HUMN          ; these 5 know no lumberjack
(5×180 + 5×0) / 10 = 90 points → level 2. The unit can no longer do it.
 IdUnitMenSkills
*2200Woodsmen10LUMB 2 (90)
The report says this unit is lumberjack 3, and a player who has not hovered the Skills cell still believes that. The warning is about to quote 2.
H — the sentence exactly as it reads today
Problems
⚠ 2200 Woodsmen — cannot produce iron: needs lumberjack 3, has lumberjack 2.
  • Not a single message string changes anywhere. Every one of the six affected warnings keeps the wording it has.
  • Once the drop warning ships, the panel usually explains itself one line further down: ⚠ 2200 Woodsmen — giving 5 men to this unit lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 2.
  • On its own — and it is on its own until the drop warning ships, and whenever its switch is off — the number contradicts the report with nothing to explain it.
I — the sentence says where the number came from
Problems
⚠ 2200 Woodsmen — cannot produce iron: needs lumberjack 3, has lumberjack 2 once the men given to it arrive.
  • Answers the question the player is about to ask, in the place they are already looking.
  • Stands on its own whether or not the drop warning is switched on.
  • Six messages to change, in six places, each with its own sentence shape — and the clause has to be added only when men actually arrived, so each becomes two strings rather than one.
  • The teaching one does not take the clause cleanly: "this unit is level 2 in force and unit 3401 is level 2, so it cannot teach it" — there are two levels in it and the clause could belong to either.
J — the plain sentence, and a second line under it saying what changed
Problems
⚠ 2200 Woodsmen — cannot produce iron: needs lumberjack 3, has lumberjack 2.
    the report shows lumberjack 3; the men given to it this month bring it down to 2.
  • Explains it once, in one place, whatever the warning above happens to be.
  • The panel has no second line today — every entry is one sentence — so this is new furniture in a panel shared by thirty-three warnings, and it would be the only one with it.
  • It is very close to being the drop warning itself, said twice.

Where this does and does not bite

  • It does not bite at all when the level goes the other way. The formed-unit case — men arriving give a unit the skill — makes a warning vanish, and a warning that is not there quotes nothing.
  • It bites on six warnings: producing without the skill, building without the skill, teaching (twice over), studying at the maximum, and magic study outside a building.
  • It only bites when a gift lowered a level, which is the same condition the drop warning fires on.
