Giving a whole class of goods — what the catalogue can and cannot say

Background for the three questions on the other two pages. Nothing here is a choice; it is what I checked, so that the choices rest on something. Every row was verified against the game engine's own item table (gamedata.cpp, flags IT_NORMAL…IT_SHIP) and then re-checked against all 171 items in our committed ruleset.json. Where it says yes, our reading and the engine's agree on every single item — no near misses, no judgement calls.

The bead assumed four classes were unknowable. It is three. NORMAL is readable after all: the engine prints a withdrawal price for every NORMAL item except silver, and prints This is the currency of Atlantis. for silver. That is 31 items — exactly the engine's 31.
Why the last three genuinely cannot be done. I read the engine function that writes the data page. There is no code path in it that tests IT_ADVANCED, IT_MAGIC or IT_SPECIAL — those three flags are never printed, in any form. Only 21 items carry none of the twelve; six of them say may only be used by a mage and so are certainly MAGIC, leaving 15 items that cannot be told apart at all. A magic wagon is ADVANCED and a magic carpet is MAGIC, and their entries are indistinguishable: magic wagon [MWAG], weight 50, walking capacity 200… against magic carpet [CARP], weight 0, walking capacity 15…. Nothing in either says which.

Three things that follow, worth seeing before you answer

1. GIVE ALL NORMAL gives away your silver — and your food. NORMAL is 31 items and includes SILV, GRAI, LIVE, FISH and MEAL alongside iron, wood, swords and horses. That is the engine's behaviour, not our reading of it.
2. GIVE ALL MOUNTS gives away your centaurs. A centaur is flagged both IT_MAN and IT_MOUNT, so ALL MEN and ALL MOUNTS both sweep it up. Giving one moves people: the MEN cell falls, and the receiving unit's skills are diluted by the arrivals.
3. GIVE ALL MONSTERS selects 60 items and can actually move 9. The other 51 say This item cannot be given to other units. The nine that move are the summoned undead and demons — skeleton, undead, lich, imp, demon, balrog, devil — plus the catapult and the steel defender. So a unit holding twenty lions and ordering GIVE 901 ALL MONSTERS moves nothing at all.
