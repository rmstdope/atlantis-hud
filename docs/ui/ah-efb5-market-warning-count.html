ah-efb5 — the market warning counts what the game will try to buy (agreed 2026-10-19)

Chosen: the count is what the game will try to buy after cutting orders a unit cannot pay for and leaving out men it refuses outright; when the count differs from the orders as written, a clause gives the written total and names each reason with its own number (B, then A, then A). When nothing is cut or refused the sentence is unchanged; when the cut brings the total down to what the market has, there is no warning.
