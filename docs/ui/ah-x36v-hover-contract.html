Set 1 “was paid by”

SourcesSentence
own food onlyThis unit's upkeep was paid by its own food (8).
faction food + a mateThis unit's upkeep was paid by faction food here (12) and a faction-mate's silver (20).

Reads as one fact with a list. The figures sit in brackets, which keeps the sentence short but makes them slightly less prominent than the standalone sentences made them.

Keeps the “covers” verb the retired sentences used. The two lists have to be read against each other to know which figure belongs to which source, which is worse the more sources there are.
