ah-z73s.4 — the recruiting sentence, and what a level of zero reads as

You already settled the gift sentence: giving 5 men to this unit lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 2, one entry per skill for the whole month, on the receiving unit, with no line. Then, while planning ah-z73s.3, you widened this advisory to cover a level lowered by recruiting too, and asked for a second sentence rather than one that stretches over both. This page is those words — and one thing the widening opened that nothing has decided yet.

Nothing already agreed is being re-opened. The gift sentence, the one-entry-per-skill rule, the Problems-panel home, the ordinary brass marking on the Skills cell and the ON-by-default toggle all stand. Section 2 is the only place a settled sentence could move, and only in the case where a level reaches zero.

1 — the recruiting sentence

Ten men who each know lumberjack 1 (30 points), recruiting four peasants. The merge is by man-days, so the unit ends at 21 points per man — lumberjack 0. rules/economy_recruiting: “New recruits will not have any skills or items”; rules/buy: “This will dilute the skills that the unit has.”

unit 900
BUY 4 PEAS
PRODUCE LUMB
R1 — “recruiting 4 men into this unit”
Problems
four recruits⚠ 900 Woodcutters — recruiting 4 men into this unit lowers its lumberjack from 1 to 0.
one recruit⚠ 900 Woodcutters — recruiting 1 man into this unit lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 2.
  • Mirrors the gift sentence word for word except the verb and the preposition — the two read as one family.
  • into rather than to: men are recruited into a unit, and no other unit is involved.
  • “recruiting 4 men” is the app's word, not the order's. The player wrote BUY 4 PEAS.
R2 — “buying 4 men into this unit”
Problems
four recruits⚠ 900 Woodcutters — buying 4 men into this unit lowers its lumberjack from 1 to 0.
one recruit⚠ 900 Woodcutters — buying 1 man into this unit lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 2.
  • Names the order the player actually wrote, so the line to look at is obvious without one being pointed at.
  • The rules call it recruiting throughout, and “buying men” reads as a slave market rather than as raising troops.
R3 — “4 recruits joining this unit”
Problems
four recruits⚠ 900 Woodcutters — 4 recruits joining this unit lower its lumberjack from 1 to 0.
one recruit⚠ 900 Woodcutters — 1 recruit joining this unit lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 2.
  • Uses the same word — joining — that you chose for the crew clause in ah-z73s.3.
  • The verb has to agree with the count (lower / lowers), which the gift sentence never has to do.
  • Furthest from the gift sentence, so the two stop looking like one family.

2 — what a level of zero reads as

A skill can be diluted away entirely: the merged points truncate to nothing and the skill leaves the Skills cell. The gift sentence as agreed says from 1 to 0. The draft of the recruiting sentence said from 1 to none. They cannot both be right, and this is the one place a settled sentence would move.

Z1 — “to 0” everywhere  (nothing already agreed changes)
Problems
gift⚠ 3100 Timber crew — giving 99 men to this unit lowers its lumberjack from 1 to 0.
recruiting⚠ 900 Woodcutters — recruiting 4 men into this unit lowers its lumberjack from 1 to 0.
  • One shape, two numbers, always. Nothing conditional to build and nothing agreed is disturbed.
  • The Skills cell will show no lumberjack at all, and the sentence says 0 — a player checking the cell finds nothing where the message implied a zero.
Z2 — “to none” everywhere  (the gift sentence's zero case moves)
Problems
gift⚠ 3100 Timber crew — giving 99 men to this unit lowers its lumberjack from 1 to none.
recruiting⚠ 900 Woodcutters — recruiting 4 men into this unit lowers its lumberjack from 1 to none.
  • Matches what the player will actually see: the skill is gone from the cell, not sitting there at zero.
  • Still one sentence shape — only the rendering of the number changes, in both sentences alike.
  • Changes a sentence you already approved, in one of its cases.
Z3 — a clause of its own when the skill is lost
Problems
gift⚠ 3100 Timber crew — giving 99 men to this unit costs it its lumberjack entirely.
recruiting⚠ 900 Woodcutters — recruiting 4 men into this unit costs it its lumberjack entirely.
  • Says the strongest thing plainly. Losing a skill outright is a different event from dropping a level.
  • A third and fourth sentence shape, each with its own test, for a case that is rare.
  • Drops the “from 1” — the player is not told what they had.
