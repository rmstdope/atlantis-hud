ah-vw8e — the chosen design: the same goods sold twice in one block

A trapper holds 10 furs. The market here wants furs at $42. Its orders this month are SELL ALL FUR written twice — a shape that turns up when a player edits a block and forgets the line already there, or pastes last month's orders on top of this month's.

Chosen, over three rounds with the navigator. The SILVER column, the ITEMS column and the market-share settlement all price a unit's SELL lines against a running per-tag total, so a second line can only sell what the lines above it left — of the unit's stock and of its settled share of the market line alike. A line that finds nothing left carries the existing nothing-left-to-sell warning, as a third cause beside the gift and the take, and that warning now fires on any SELL rather than only on a SELL … ALL.

Rejected: a warning code of its own with its own switch (The same goods sold twice) — the shipped description already covers a sale, and a 35th switch buys little; correcting the numbers silently, which ah-q7jd rejected once already; two shorter sentences; naming the line number, which the underline already points at; warning a line the market ran out under, where region-pool-oversubscribed already speaks; warning a line its own EXCEPT emptied, where the goods are visibly still there; widening only the new cause and leaving the gift and take causes ALL-only; and underlining just the first dead line of several. The full interview is in bead ah-vw8e's design field.
The arithmetic is not in question — it is the bug. The first line sells all ten furs; the second finds nothing left and earns nothing. The ITEMS column already knows that. The SILVER column pays for twenty furs. This page is only about what, if anything, the second line then says.

Option A — the existing nothing-left-to-sell warning grows a third cause

The warning shipped by ah-q7jd already says “a SELL ALL of goods this month's earlier orders have already moved away”. An earlier SELL line is an earlier order that moved them away, so it joins the two causes already there (a GIVE of your own, a TAKE by somebody else) rather than arriving as a code of its own. One toggle covers all three causes. A player who has switched this off because they know what their gifts do also stops hearing about their duplicated sale.

Three candidate sentences

Every one of them ends “so this sells nothing”, which is how the two causes already shipped end. The choice is what comes before it.

W1 — names the count
an earlier order sells all 10 of its furs, so this sells nothing
W2 — names the line
the SELL on line 2 already sells all 10 of its furs, so this sells nothing
W3 — shortest
an earlier SELL already sells these furs, so this sells nothing

For a unit holding exactly one, the shipped wording says its only fur rather than all 1 of its furs; whichever sentence is chosen keeps that.
