Fetching the same turn again what a reload really replaces

Today, when Fetch brings back the turn you already have, the question warns that loading it again throws away "anything you have changed since". It doesn't. Orders you typed, hex notes and study plans are stored apart from the report, so they come through a reload untouched. New Age and New Origins share the sentence. The drawings use New Age's Trident world on turn 21. New Origins is the same, with "New Origins" in place of "Trident".

Agreed 2026-11-25: Option A and Option 2A, naming what is kept as "orders and notes". Option B and Option 2B are shown only as the rejected alternatives.
