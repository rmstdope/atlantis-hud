ah-ndp9 — mages, leaders, and men that must not arrive

rules/magic: "Only one man units, with the man being a leader, are permitted to study these skills … once a unit becomes a mage the unit number is fixed." Your New Origins ruling adds the other half: a mage may neither recruit people with BUY nor receive people from another unit.

Today the HUD projects all of it happily — it will show you a three-human unit beginning STUDY FORCE, and it will happily pour ten peasants into a mage and dilute its skills. This bead makes those orders warn, and project nothing. Everything below is the wording and the toggles; the arithmetic is settled.

Sibling bead ah-t8ei (in flight) covers the opposite direction — a mage giving men away — and deliberately adds no message. This bead's scope says the incoming side is warned, which is why wording is on the table at all.

Part 1 — how many switches appear in Settings

Every advisory check is one toggle in Settings ▸ Warnings, grouped by concern. This bead adds one or two.

T1 — two toggles (recommended)

Studying / Teaching

Magic study outside a building
A mage above level 2 studying magic where no building houses them, which halves the month's study.
Magic study by a unit that cannot become a mage
A unit that is not exactly one leader ordered to begin a Foundation magic skill.

Orders

Gifts the game will refuse
A GIVE or TAKE naming items that cannot change hands, or men given to another faction.
Men sent into a mage
A GIVE, TAKE or BUY that would add people to a unit that has already begun magic.
  • The two situations are genuinely different: one is about a unit that is not yet a mage and is being told it cannot become one; the other about one that already is.
  • Each lands in the group a player would look in — the study one beside the other two magic-study checks, the arrivals one beside Gifts the game will refuse and Arrivals that lower a skill.
  • Two more rows in a Settings tab that already has 43.
T2 — one toggle for the whole rule

Studying / Teaching

Mage unit composition
A unit that is not one leader beginning magic, or men sent into a unit that has already begun it.
  • One switch, one rule from the rulebook: a mage is one leader, and stays one leader.
  • Turning off the noisy half turns off the useful half. A player who is content to see "you cannot recruit into your mage" every month also loses the warning that a study will simply fail.
  • One of its two halves has to sit in the wrong Settings group.

Part 2 — the study warning's sentence

Four compositions reach this check. The teacher check is the closest existing precedent and reads only leaders can teach; this unit has 3 humans, so a matching shape is available.

unit 900 ; 3 humans [HUMN] STUDY FORCE
S1 — mirror the teacher warning
the unitthe message
3 humansonly a lone leader can begin force; this unit has 3 humans
2 leadersonly a lone leader can begin force; this unit has 2 leaders
1 leader, 1 humanonly a lone leader can begin force; this unit also has 1 human
no peopleonly a lone leader can begin force; this unit has no people
  • Word for word the shape of only leaders can teach; this unit has 3 humans, including its also for a unit that has some of the right people and some of the wrong ones.
  • Names the skill, so a block with two study orders in it points at the right one.
  • "a lone leader" is a new phrase in the app's vocabulary.
S2 — say what will happen
the unitthe message
3 humansforce can only be begun by a unit of one leader, and this unit has 3 humans, so the study will fail
no peopleforce can only be begun by a unit of one leader, and this unit has no people, so the study will fail
  • Says the consequence out loud. The server refuses the order outright and the month is wasted — which the other two shapes leave the player to infer.
  • Long. On a narrow Problems panel it wraps to three lines, where every neighbouring warning is one or two.
S3 — subject first, shortest
the unitthe message
3 humansthis unit is 3 humans, so it cannot begin force
2 leadersthis unit is 2 leaders, so it cannot begin force
no peoplethis unit has no people, so it cannot begin force
  • Shortest of the three, and reads naturally in a list of a hex's problems.
  • Never states the rule, so a player who does not know it learns only that something is wrong — not that one leader is the answer.
  • Two shapes ("is 3 humans" / "has no people") for one sentence.

Part 3 — the sentence when men are sent into a mage

Three routes reach a mage: another unit's GIVE, the mage's own TAKE, and the mage's own BUY. The warning sits on the line that ordered it — so the GIVE case warns in the giver's block, about a unit that is not the giver.

unit 800 ; 20 humans [HUMN] — an ordinary unit GIVE 900 5 HUMN unit 900 ; 1 leader [LEAD], force [FORC] 2 — a mage BUY 5 PEASANTS TAKE FROM 800 3 HUMN
M1 — one sentence, the existing refusal shape
the orderthe message
GIVE 900 5 HUMNunit 900 is a mage and cannot be given men, so this order moves nothing
TAKE FROM 800 3 HUMNthis unit is a mage and cannot take on men, so this order moves nothing
BUY 5 PEASANTSthis unit is a mage and cannot recruit, so this order buys nobody
  • … so this order moves nothing is already the app's phrase for a refused transfer (unit 900 belongs to another faction, so this TAKE moves nothing).
  • The GIVE line names unit 900, because the warning appears in unit 800's block and the reader otherwise has no idea which of its gifts is at fault.
  • Three strings rather than one.
M2 — one sentence for all three routes
the orderthe message
GIVE 900 5 HUMNunit 900 has begun magic, so its people cannot change; this order moves nothing
TAKE FROM 800 3 HUMNthis unit has begun magic, so its people cannot change; this order moves nothing
BUY 5 PEASANTSthis unit has begun magic, so its people cannot change; this order moves nothing
  • One string with one substitution, and it states the reason — the mage's unit is fixed — rather than three variations on the verb.
  • "this order moves nothing" is a strange thing to say about a BUY, which does not move anything even when it works.
M3 — count what was refused
the orderthe message
GIVE 900 5 HUMNunit 900 is a mage, so 5 men stay where they are
BUY 5 PEASANTSthis unit is a mage, so it recruits nobody
  • Names the size of what did not happen, the way Arrivals that lower a skill does (giving 5 men to this unit lowers its combat from 3 to 1).
  • A count is a promise, and BUY 5 PEASANTS would not have bought five anyway — the market share decides. The number would sometimes be a lie.

Part 4 — does the rule cover apprentices?

rules/magic_apprentices: "Apprentices may be created by having a unit study manipulation. Like Mages, only one man units, with the man being a leader, may become Apprentices." — the same composition restriction, in the same words. But the fixed-unit-number sentence in rules/magic is written about mages only, and says nothing about apprentices.

In the catalogue this is an exact, checkable distinction: force, pattern, spirit and manipulation are the four magic skills with no prerequisites; every other magic skill needs one of them first.

optionSTUDY MANIPULATION by 3 humansGIVE men to a manipulation-only unit
A1warned — the rules say so in as many wordswarned — an apprentice is treated as a mage throughout
A2warnedsilent — the rules fix only a mage's unit number
A3silent — the bead says Foundations, and manipulation is not onesilent
Worth knowing: the sibling bead ah-t8ei, already in flight, treats any magic skill as making a unit a mage for the purpose of refusing a GIVE of men. Choosing A2 or A3 means this bead and that one answer "is this unit a mage?" differently, for the same unit, in the same month.

What is settled without asking

  • Silence where the app cannot see. A unit whose headcount the report only estimates, one whose races it could not classify, or one whose month cannot be followed, is not judged at all — the same accept-on-doubt policy that already governs the teacher check and every study check.
  • Nothing is blocked. Every semantic finding in this app is a warning; the invalid order stays in the editor and can still be sent.
  • The composition is the one the month leaves. A unit given a leader this month and ordered to study force is judged on what it will be when the study runs, not on what the report printed.
  • A refused arrival cannot dilute anything. The mage keeps its people, races and skills, so Arrivals that lower a skill never fires off the back of an order this bead refused.
  • GIVE UNIT is untouched — rules/magic expressly permits handing a whole mage over.
