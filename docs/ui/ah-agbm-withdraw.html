Q3 — WITHDRAW, when the faction's fund may not cover it

WITHDRAW is unlike the other three: it costs the withdrawing unit nothing. The goods are drawn against the faction's unclaimed fund, and whether that fund can pay is a faction-wide question that already has its own warning — claims-exceed-unclaimed in the Problems panel. The Silver column deliberately charges the unit nothing for it (ah-tdsi).

The situation on this page. The faction's unclaimed fund holds $400. Grain withdraws at $37 each in this ruleset. Two units in the hex are each told to withdraw, and together they ask for more than the fund holds.

unit 5540
  withdraw 8 GRAI    ; $296 of the fund
unit 5541
  withdraw 6 GRAI    ; $222 — and $296 + $222 = $518 against a $400 fund

Option A — the goods arrive; the fund's problem stays the fund's warning

The ITEMS column shows what each unit was told to withdraw. The overdraft is reported once, where it belongs: in the Problems panel, faction-wide.

Problems panel — already exists todayWithdrawals and claims come to $518, and the faction's unclaimed fund holds $400.
  • One mistake, warned about once. Saying it again in the column would be the double-warning ah-bumi was filed to stop.
  • Matches the Silver column, which already charges a withdrawing unit nothing and never doubts it.
  • The column stays useful for the ordinary case — a fund with plenty in it — which is nearly every turn.
  • In the overdrawn case the column shows grain that one of the two units will not get. Which one the server refuses is not knowable from here.

Option B — show it only when the fund is known to cover it

The projection is withheld from both units the moment the fund cannot pay for everything asked of it.

  • A faction-wide fact now blanks a hex-local column: a unit in a distant hex loses its projection because of an unrelated unit's withdrawal.
  • Two rows go quiet with no explanation on either of them; the reason is in a different panel.
  • The withdrawal is judged twice — once in Problems, once by silence here.
