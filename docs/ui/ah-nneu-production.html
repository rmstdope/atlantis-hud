Production & tax — A: one table, every hex a row

A Production button under the faction's Allowances opens a large window over the map. The count of regions used sits on top; below it one row per hex that this turn's orders tax, pillage or produce in. Each row says whether the tax is collected in full and, resource by resource, how much of what the hex offers is produced. Hexes with something left over come first; hexes worked to the full follow under their own divider.

What it costs: everything is visible at once and easy to scan down, but with forty hexes it is a long table, and a row of small numbers reads slower than a sentence.

Chosen (bead ah-nneu). This table, rather than the leftovers-in-words list. A hex that has tax to collect but is not taxed is a gap. An older report with separate limits shows two count lines. Clicking a row centres the map on that hex, selects it, and closes the window. A pillaged hex counts as taxed in full. An estimate is marked with "at most" or "?" in a neutral colour and never makes a hex a gap. "Production" is also a command palette entry. All words ship as drawn.
