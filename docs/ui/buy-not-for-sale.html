Buying what the market does not sell — what the player sees (bead ah-d8u)

The engine refuses the order and reports it a week later: BUY: Can't buy that. — three times across two turns of the committed corpus. Everything the check needs is in the report already: the region prints its market, and orders::semantics::market() already looks an item up in it.

Decided, 2026-08-17. (1) The message names the Products confusion when the item is on that line, falling back to simply listing the market when it is not. (2) A hex with nothing for sale says so rather than reporting an empty list. (3) The toggle is Buying what is not for sale in the existing Orders group, on by default. (4) Measured afterwards and settled without asking: the market list is never truncated — the largest For Sale line in the whole corpus has four items.

The three real cases cannot be reconstructed. The units that made them (7308, 7309, 7721) are gone from both the turn they appear in and the next, and the turn their orders were written against is not committed — so we know the mistake happened three times but not what was typed. The case below is instead the one the report's own layout invites, taken verbatim from neworigins-3.0.0-g3-f42-t41.rep:484-488.

The mistake the report invites

What the region prints

Wanted: none. For Sale: 88 wood elves [WELF] at $43, 17 leaders [LEAD] at $756. Entertainment available: $85. Products: 31 grain [GRAI], 35 wood [WOOD], 15 furs [FUR], 7 yew [YEW].

Two different lines, and only one of them is a shop. For Sale is what you may buy. Products is what the region's peasants can be set to make — grain, wood, furs and yew — and none of it is for sale. Writing BUY 20 GRAI here is the natural mistake, and the engine simply says Can't buy that.

What the player writes

14unit 7308
15BUY 20 GRAI

The month's silver is spent on nothing, and the unit stands there empty-handed. All three questions below are about what the panel says back.

Orders

How much the message says Option C was chosen

OPTION A — rejected Just the refusal

Problems
7308⚠the market here does not sell grain, so this BUY will be refused
  • Shortest, and one wording however odd the market is.
  • Leaves the player to scroll back to the region and read the For Sale line themselves.

Problems

  • Answers the next question — well, what can I buy? — without leaving the panel.
  • A market with eight lines makes a long sentence; needs a rule for how many to name.

Problems

  • Catches the actual mistake this report layout provokes, and teaches the difference once.

A market with nothing in it Option 1 was chosen

Most hexes print For Sale: none. A BUY there is just as refused, but “it offers wood elves and leaders” has nothing to fill in.

OPTION 1 — chosen Say there is no market

Problems
7308⚠there is nothing for sale here, so this BUY will be refused
  • Names the real situation rather than a missing list.
  • Likely the commonest form of this warning, since most hexes sell nothing.

OPTION 2 — rejected The same sentence, list empty

Problems
7308⚠the market here does not sell grain, so this BUY will be refused
  • One fewer wording.
  • “The market here” implies there is one, when the hex has no market at all.

The four sentences, exactly as they ship the two decisions compose

Decisions 1 and 2 were taken separately, and a hex can be both — it produces the item and sells nothing, which the corpus suggests is the common case. The third sentence below splices the first decision's clause into the second decision's sentence, using its own words. It is the planner's composition rather than a drawn choice, and is easily reversed to the fourth sentence if you would rather.

Problems
the market sells things, and the region produces this one
7308⚠the market here does not sell grain - the region produces it, which is not the same thing; the market offers wood elves and leaders
the market sells things, and this is not one of them
7308⚠the market here does not sell mithril: it offers wood elves and leaders
nothing for sale, and the region produces this
7308⚠there is nothing for sale here - the region produces grain, which is not the same thing, so this BUY will be refused
nothing for sale, and the region does not produce it either
7308⚠there is nothing for sale here, so this BUY will be refused

The item the player asked for is named by item_name, the same helper the Overspent items warning already uses, so the two agree. What the market offers is named in the report's own words, plural and all — 88 wood elves [WELF] gives wood elves.

Why the evidence here is thinner than usual. For the other order checks I could name the exact unit, the exact order and the exact numbers. Here the three refusals name units that appear nowhere in the committed reports, so the mistake is real and counted but its content is lost. The design above is argued from the report's own layout instead — which is why question 1 exists at all.
