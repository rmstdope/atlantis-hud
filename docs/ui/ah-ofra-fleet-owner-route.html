ah-ofra — who decides where a ship is going

Pick a unit that is riding on a ship and the map draws the journey that ship is about to make. Today Atlantis HUD draws whichever sailing order it happens to find among the people aboard — in practice the last one listed. In the game only the ship's owner's SAIL moves it; everyone else's SAIL is an offer of hands, not a steering wheel. So the line on your map can point somewhere the ship will never go, and you can plan a whole month around it.

The owner is simply the first unit listed under the ship. That is the only thing that makes someone the owner, and it is visible in your own report.

Question 3 when the app cannot tell who the owner is

Sometimes the first unit under the ship is not one of ours and is not described — a foreign ship we are merely standing next to, or a hull whose contents our report only partly lists. Then the app cannot say who the owner is, so it cannot say whose SAIL counts. It knows a course was written; it does not know whether it will be obeyed.
