4 — The words for the count

The count is how many of the Army's units this turn's report shows (the same number as in the units pane's list).

A — recommended "5 units" · "1 unit" · "no units this turn"

Plain counting; only the empty case says "this turn", because that's when it matters.

|
Iron Guard5 unitsarmy
Scouts1 unitarmy
Reserveno units this turnarmy
  • Reads naturally; "no units this turn" tells you why the Army looks empty.
  • "5 units" doesn't say that 2 more are remembered but not seen.

B "5 of 7 seen" · "1 of 1 seen" · "0 of 3 seen"

Both numbers: seen this turn, and how many units the Army holds.

|
Iron Guard5 of 7 seenarmy
Scouts1 of 1 seenarmy
Reserve0 of 3 seenarmy
  • Shows units that are missing from this turn's report.
  • Longer, and a number the units pane's list doesn't show, so the two disagree in form. An Army with no units at all reads "0 of 0 seen".
