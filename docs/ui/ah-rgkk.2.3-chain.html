ah-rgkk.2.3 — how many steps the chain draws

Everything on this page is inside decision N2, which you already took: a Skills line is a chain of level (points) steps, the last one next turn’s and drawn in blue. One thing about it was written down two ways and I cannot settle it from the record. ah-rgkk.2’s own words say “one step per thing that happened”; the example beside them, and every popup in docs/ui/ah-rgkk.2-round2.html, draws fewer steps than things that happened. The two readings differ on the screen, so this is yours.

Answered: B. A Skills line shows where the month started, where the market left it, and — when the unit is studying — where next turn lands, in blue. At most three figures, whatever happened; a skill nothing touched shows one. Every arrival is still named in the sentences underneath.
