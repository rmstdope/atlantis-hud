Hovering a passage ring — what it says, and where

When a unit's route runs into a passage inside a structure, the map puts a brass ring on that hex. We agreed that hovering the ring explains the passage. Today it doesn't: whatever you hover on that hex, you only get the hex's own line — mountain (36,4) in Inhead. Two things to settle before it's fixed: where on the hex the explanation appears, and what the hover looks like.

Already settled, not reopened: the words of all three hovers (unknown far side, and the entry and exit rings of a known one), the rings themselves, and that clicking anywhere on the hex — ring included — selects the hex exactly as it does today. A note pin sits in the hex's top-right corner and never covers the ring.
