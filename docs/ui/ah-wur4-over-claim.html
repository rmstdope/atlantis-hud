ah-wur4 — warning when a faction claims more than it has

You asked for this while we settled ah-bumi: "file a new bead to introduce a warning if the total CLAIM in all hexes exceeds the unclaimed silver."

It is the half that makes ah-bumi safe. That bead caps each claiming unit at the whole purse and counts no contention — the posture WORK and entertaining already take — so two units can together read more than the faction holds. This warning is what catches that, exactly as the shipped not-traded-here check carries the case where ah-1wcw.2's forecast earns nothing.

The shape is settled by precedent: check_faction already runs two faction-wide checks, and the nearer one — too many quartermasters — anchors one finding per offending unit, on that unit's own order line. Three things are still open.

Chosen every claiming unit is warned

Purse holds 4935. Three units claim 3000, 2000 and 500 — 5500 between them. No single one of them is at fault.

A Every claiming unitChosen

One finding per unit that claimed, on its own CLAIM line.

Order problems

⚠Scouts (101) your units claim 5500 between them and the faction has 4935
⚠Guards (102) your units claim 5500 between them and the faction has 4935
⚠Paymaster (103) your units claim 5500 between them and the faction has 4935
  • Every claim is reachable from the panel, and each one is a line you might edit.
  • Exactly what too many quartermasters does — the nearest precedent.
  • The message says the total, so it never implies this unit alone is the problem.
  • Repeats one sentence three times. A faction claiming from a dozen units gets a dozen.

B One finding, on the first claim

A single finding, anchored on whichever CLAIM comes first in the document.

Order problems

⚠Scouts (101) your units claim 5500 between them and the faction has 4935
  • Says it once, which is what it is: one problem about the faction.
  • Anchored on a unit that is no more at fault than the others, and picked by document order — the thing this fleet has twice refused to do for shared pools.
  • The other claims are not reachable from the panel.

Chosen for the degenerate case: a report that states no unclaimed silver at all — the header line absent rather than zero. the check does not fire, because a faction with no stated purse is not evidence of an empty one, and warning about a limit nobody has stated would be inventing the limit. Consistent with ah-bumi, which counts a claim as written when the purse is unstated, for the same reason.
