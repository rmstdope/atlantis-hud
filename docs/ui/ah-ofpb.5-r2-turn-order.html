Round 2 — the words, the turn order, and a split

Round 1 is banked: ranges merge into the stock (4-5 RUNE); a range gets no + ? mark; SWIN’s materials leave at the ceiling so the column, the SILVER column and the warnings read one number.

Your summoning answer changes the shape of the bead, and I want to check I have it right before I write it down. You gave me the engine’s arithmetic: num = (level × mOut + rand(0..99)) / 100, then RandomiseSummonAmount(num) flips 2 × num coins and counts heads, minimum 1 when num > 0. For a level 3 wolf master that is num = 6, twelve flips, so 1 to 12 wolves, averaging 6. I confirmed against the data page that exactly four skills word it as an average — WOLF “averaging”, BIRD “an average of”, SUSK and RAIS “at an average rate of” — and the other five summons do not.

One thing I could not look up: the engine source is not the rules page or the data page. I will record 1..2n as your decision, from the game engine, and cite it that way rather than pretending it came from a lookup.

Q5 — the turn order, and a warning that would fire wrongly

I looked up rules/sequenceofevents and found something that points the opposite way from PRODUCE. The order of a turn is:

… GIVE and TAKE …gifts settle
… TAX …
Spells are CAST← the cast happens here
SELL, then BUY
WITHDRAW
movement
STUDY, PRODUCE, BUILD, ENTERTAIN, WORK← where PRODUCE happens, last

So what a cast creates can be sold, bought with, withdrawn against, built with and produced with this month — it just cannot be given away, because GIVE is already past. That is the reverse of PRODUCE, whose goods arrive too late for anything.

ah-ofpb.1 deliberately does not credit produced goods to the ledger, so a not-enough-items warning still fires on a unit that produces then spends. If this bead copies that, an enchanter ordered cast Enchant_Swords then sell 15 MSWO is told it is 15 short — and by the rules it is not.

A — credit what a cast creates, so it can be spent. The ledger gains the created items, and the warning does not fire.

Q7 — a CAST the app cannot count

ah-ofpb.1 gave every uncountable PRODUCE the + ? mark, one rule with no exceptions. CAST is not symmetrical, because most spells legitimately move no items at all and the app knows it: a fireball, earth lore, phantasmal entertainment. Marking those would put a question mark on every mage in the faction.

A — mark only what could not be worked out. A spell the ruleset has no entry for, a bare CAST, and a transmutation naming a material that will not resolve. A spell that is known to create and consume nothing is silent.
