Already settled by earlier decisions shown for completeness

  • When two orders take the silver, for example a CLAIM 500 and this WITHDRAW, both lines get the warning. That is how "claiming more than the faction has" already works: neither order is singled out.
  • A WITHDRAW the game will refuse, such as one for a non-basic item, in the Nexus, or one the fund can't afford, takes no silver. It gets no new warning; its existing refusal warning stays.
  • A report that states no unclaimed silver gives no warning, the same as the other unclaimed-silver warnings.
  • Amber, on by default, and listed in Problems under the order's unit like every advisory warning.
