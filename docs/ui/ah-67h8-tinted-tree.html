Magic study tree, tinted by your own mages — the chosen design

The design settled with the navigator over six rounds for bead ah-67h8. Everything here is drawn from the committed fixtures and the shipped ruleset: Six of Seven (881) of g7f95t71, and Brian de Bois-Guilbert (1159) of g3f42t82, who holds two skills at the game's maximum.

One mage at a time. A picker in the header is the view's subject; every tint on screen is a fact about that one unit, which is what rules/magic_skills makes it. The overlay is always on when a report is loaded, and it reaches both the branch cards and the whole graph.

The tinted branch view

Six of Seven: 8 known, 9 at their ceiling, 21 open to him, 32 locked. Each row carries a left edge whose pattern as well as colour says which, so the five states separate in monochrome.
