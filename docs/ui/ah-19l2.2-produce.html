ah-19l2.2 — a PRODUCE order that spends silver

Once ah-19l2.1 puts the recipes in the ruleset, the client can finally cost a PRODUCE. Two of the ruleset's 53 recipes take silver — catapults and steel defenders, 3000 each, 1 per 4 man-months.

The case is real, not hypothetical. In the committed turn g3-f42-t82, unit 12881 “Carpenters” — ours, 10 wood elves, carpenter 5 — orders @produce catapult. Ten men at 1 per 4 man-months is 2 catapults, so 6000 silver, plus 500 wood, 60 ironwood and 160 furs. The Silver column shows nothing for any of it.

Question 1 how many does the unit make?

The silver spent is 3000 × however many it produces, so this question is the number in the column. The engine makes as many as the unit can pay for and has materials for; the client can see the men and the holdings, but it cannot see what the unit will be given this month.

OptionCarpenters (10 men, 3000 silver, 500 wood, 60 ironwood, 160 furs)
A — men alone Expense 6000 — 10 men ÷ 4 man-months = 2 catapults. What the order asks for. Overstates whenever materials or silver fall short, and overstating expense shows red where the truth may be black.
B — men, capped by what it holds Expense 3000 — materials allow 2, but 3000 silver pays for only 1. What the engine will actually do, as far as the report can see. Understates if the unit is given silver or wood this month by a faction-mate.
C — decline to price it Expense ?, with “This unit's production cannot be counted.” Never wrong. Also never useful, and this is the one order in the corpus where the figure would have mattered.

Question 2 do the materials move too?

A catapult eats 250 wood, 30 ironwood and 80 furs as well as the silver. The ledger already tracks every item tag and already emits not-enough-items.

OptionWhat happens
A — silver only The Silver column is right; a unit ordered to produce a catapult with no wood is not warned. Smallest bead, and it matches the parent's title. Leaves a second false silence in place, of exactly the kind this family exists to remove.
B — silver and materials The column is right and “short 250 wood: this unit can have 0 and its orders spend 250” fires. Uses the shipped not-enough-items machinery and the inputs ah-19l2.1 is capturing anyway. Larger, and it makes question 1's answer bite on items as well as silver.

Question 3 what the hover says

Every other priced order is silent in the hover — the Expense figure speaks for itself. A production is different in one way: the count is derived, not written in the order.

OptionThe sentence
A — say the count “This unit's 10 men produce 2 catapults, at 3000 silver each.”
B — say it only when capped Silent at full rate; when short: “This unit has silver for 1 catapult, not the 2 its men could make.”
C — say nothing The Expense figure alone, like every other order.
