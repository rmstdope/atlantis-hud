Every pane below is at the application's real column width and in the application's real font (monospace throughout — the earlier mockup drew names in Helvetica, which flattered the fit).

The row

A flex line with a 6px gap: skill name (13px, map blue, opens the dictionary) · tag (9.5px, dim) · within-branch gate (9.5px, dim) · standing chip (9.5px, tinted fill) · then, pushed to the right edge, the crossing prerequisite pill (9.5px, violet, clickable). Tag, gate and both kinds of chip never break; the name is the only thing that wraps.

A locked row

The row fades to opacity: 0.55 — the fade is what tells you at a glance how much of a branch is shut, and 32 of 70 skills are locked for a typical mage. The fade is applied to the row's full-strength ink rather than to an already-dim grey, so the tag and gate land at 4.01:1 and stay readable when you look straight at one. A locked row keeps its gate text and takes no standing chip: what is missing is the reason for showing it at all.
