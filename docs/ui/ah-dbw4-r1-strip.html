ah-dbw4 — the pinned-faction strip

The strip above Other factions when the list is narrowed to one faction. Today it pushes the attitude to the far right with a spacer, and leaves the ✕ floating loose beside the faction name. Four questions: what the chip looks like, how the attitude is set off from the name once they sit together, whether the Army strip gets the same treatment, and what the concealed-owner case says.

Note: the mockup you approved for ah-1mpx.5 (round 3) already drew a chip with the ✕ inside it and the attitude right beside it. What shipped drifted from it. So part of this is simply putting back what was agreed — but the chip's exact look was never pinned down, and the questions below are the ones that were left open.

Q3 — does the Army strip get the same treatment?

The bead asks this and it is yours: the Army strip sits in the same place and also uses an ml-auto spacer — but its spacer separates a name from three actions (Export…, Rename, Delete), which is a different job from separating two facts. It has no dismiss control, so nothing would go inside its chip.

Q4 — the concealed-owner case, and the words

rules/stealthobs: when your Observation only equals their Stealth you see the unit and not its owner. There is then no faction to hold an attitude toward, so the strip says a sentence instead. The chip still exists — you still need a way out of the pin — but it holds Faction not shown. Both options use Option A's chip.

Option P — keep the sentence as it ships recommended
Faction not shown✕ Their owner is concealed from you this turn.

  • Explains why there is no attitude, which a reader will otherwise wonder about.
