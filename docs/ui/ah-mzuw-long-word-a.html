Orders panel, a long order word — the order word wraps inside its chip

Option A, as agreed: each warning flows like a sentence. This drawing is about one warning whose quoted order word is longer than the row itself is wide at a narrow rail, such as a unit's full name in quotes. (The panel is drawn a little taller than its smallest here so that you can see the whole row.)

The grey chip around the order word wraps onto as many lines as it needs, the same way the message does, so every letter of it can be read.

Cost: a chip that runs over two or three lines looks less like a chip.
