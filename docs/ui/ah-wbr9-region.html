ah-wbr9 — the region half, round 2

Settled: the skill message mirrors build-without-skill (“cannot produce catapults: needs carpenter 4, has carpenter 2”); an item nothing makes gets its own sentence under the same code; and the bead covers the region case as well.

One thing that falls out cleanly and is worth stating, because it decides which orders the region check touches at all: only a recipe with no material inputs comes from the region. iron, wood, grain and the rest are produced from the hex itself and appear on its Products line; a sword is made from iron and can be made anywhere. So the region check runs on exactly the recipes ah-19l2.1 records with inputs: [], and a @produce sword with no iron is already covered by not-enough-items.

Question 4 the region message

@produce iron in a hex whose line reads Products: 31 grain [GRAI], 35 wood [WOOD], 15 furs [FUR].

OptionThe message
A — say what the hex has cannot produce iron here: this region produces grain, wood and furs Tells the player what they can order instead, which is the next thing they will want. Longer, and it repeats a line already on screen.
B — say only what is missing cannot produce iron here: this region has none Short, and shaped like the other advisories. The player looks up at the Products line themselves.
C — mirror the skill sentence cannot produce iron: this region produces none Closest in shape to “cannot produce catapults: needs carpenter 4…”, so the two read as one family.

A hex that produces nothing at all (Products: none. — 5 of the 350 in the corpus) reads the same way under every option.

Question 5 when both are wrong at once

A unit with no mining skill orders @produce iron in a hex with no iron. Both checks fire.

OptionWhat shows on the line
Both marks Two findings on one order. Each is independently true, and each is separately toggleable, so suppressing one must not hide the other.
Skill only One mark. The skill is the more fundamental problem — the unit could not do it anywhere — and two marks on one short order is noise.
Region only One mark. The region is the thing the player can fix by moving, and the skill will still be wrong when they arrive.

Question 6 where the two toggles live

The settings tab groups advisories by concern: Studying/Teaching, Resources, Markets, Guarding, Orders, Building, Sailing. None of them is about producing.

Option
A new Producing group Two toggles, “Producing without the skill” and “Producing what the region has not”, in a group of their own after Building — which is its closest neighbour in kind, and itself a group of four.
Into Resources Beside “Overspent silver” and “Overdrawn items”. Producing is about resources; the group becomes a mix of “you cannot afford it” and “you cannot do it”.
Into Orders The catch-all group. No new heading, and the two sit among unrelated order checks.
