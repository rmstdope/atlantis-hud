ah-dxfd — the weapons are stale too, and that changes the bead

Chasing your point about the swords: there is no guessing in that example, and I picked a bad one. A TAKE of swords is already counted — the ledger applies it. But following it through turned up something the bead as filed does not mention, and it is load-bearing.

A unit is combat ready in the numbers the app uses as min(men, things it can wield or ride) (readiness, silver.rs:1930). It reads the men from UnitFacts.men — the report's — and the weapons from UnitFacts.items, which is also the report's (semantics.rs:862). The bead names the headcount and the skills. It does not name the items, and the pillage half does not work without them.

Fixing the men alone makes the pillage warning worse

The bead's own headline case. Isles of Kel, tax base 2400, so 24 combat ready men are needed.

unit 1234 ; Home guard — 19 HUMN, COMB 1 (30), 35 SWOR FORM 1 PILLAGE END GIVE NEW 1 10 HUMN GIVE NEW 1 10 SWOR
 Home guardRaiders (new 1)Region totalWarning
Todaymin(19, 35) = 19min(0, 0) = 019 fires, with a false explanation
Men and skills onlymin(9, 35) = 9min(10, 0) = 09 fires, and now the number is wrong too
Men, skills and itemsmin(9, 25) = 9min(10, 10) = 1019 fires; correct number, no false explanation
Today's 19 is right by accident — the ten men the app forgets to give the Raiders are the same ten it forgets to take off the Home guard, so the error cancels. Move the men and leave the swords behind, and the cancellation stops: the app would say 9 where the truth is 19, and tell you an order that works cannot be filed. A men-only fix is a regression on the very warning this bead is named after.

The answer is already computed — but in the wrong order

ledger_for (semantics.rs:1583) seeds every unit's item balance from the report and then applies every GIVE, TAKE, BUY, SELL and WITHDRAW to it. That balance is the post-order weapon count. The trouble is when it is ready:

ledger_for: 1. seed balance from the report's items 2. region_wages(hex) → combat_ready_in → readiness → wants the weapons 3. apply every intent → the balance becomes post-order     … and the PILLAGE arm here reads region.combat_ready from step 2 4. charge_upkeep
Combat-ready is needed to price a PILLAGE, and the weapons that decide combat-ready are not settled until after the PILLAGE has been priced. That is a genuine cycle, not an oversight — it is why region_wages is computed once per hex, before the intent loop (ah-1ad6.2, ah-lu0f.3). Breaking it means applying the item transfers in a pass of their own before the silver is priced, which is a real change to a function two surfaces share.

The question. The bead as filed is "the headcount and the skills". The work it actually needs is "the headcount, the skills and the weapons", and the third one is the awkward one. How should it be shaped?
W1 — split ah-dxfd, weapons first
Child 1 — the weaponsChild 2 — the men and the skills
readiness counts the weapons and mounts the unit will actually hold, out of the ledger's balance. The intent walk is reordered so item transfers settle before the silver is priced. Safe on its own: it can only make the pillage count more right, and today's cancelling error is untouched because the headcount does not move. Everything the bead was filed for — upkeep, tax, entertaining, CAST income and the pillage headcount — reads the post-gift figures. Lands on top of child 1, so the regression above can never ship.
  • Each child is one small increment with its own PR, and either one alone leaves the app better than it found it.
  • The hard part — reordering the ledger's passes — is isolated in a bead of its own, where it can be reviewed as the structural change it is rather than as a detail of a headcount fix.
  • A dependency edge makes the ordering the fleet's business rather than a note somebody has to remember.
  • Two review cycles instead of one, and the fix you are waiting for is behind the other one.
W2 — one bead, weapons as its first increment
  • One PR, so the two halves cannot be separated by accident and the regression is impossible by construction.
  • The reviewer sees the whole argument at once, including why the ledger's passes had to be reordered.
  • It is a large bead — a struct's inputs, a pass reordering, and six arithmetic surfaces — and this project's rule is small increments delivered independently.
  • If the ledger reordering turns out to be worse than it looks, the headcount fix is stuck behind it with no way to land alone.
W3 — keep ah-dxfd to men and skills, and leave PILLAGE out of it
  • Smallest possible change; upkeep, tax, entertaining and CAST all get right immediately.
  • It cannot be done. readiness reads the same UnitFacts the upkeep does — there is no way to give the maintenance pass the new headcount and the pillage pass the old one without keeping two views of one unit, which is the fault the bead exists to remove.
  • Listed only so it is on the record as considered and rejected.
