Magic study tree — the whole graph

The chosen design for ah-gjbs.2, the second half of the magic study tree. Settled with the navigator over four rounds. Real data from the shipped config/public/ruleset.json: 70 magic skills, tiers of 4 / 15 / 38 / 11 / 2, four roots, and 34 skills with more than one prerequisite. The graph is 1366 × 1100px and cannot reflow, so it pans and zooms at every window width.

The grey frames are a 1560px window; the dialogs inside them are drawn at their real size. Scroll inside a frame — in the app that is drag-to-pan and wheel-to-zoom.

The toggle, and the two frames

Branches keeps the 56rem box it was drawn at in ah-gjbs.1. The whole graph opens the dialog out to 94vw × 80vh. The box resizes when the view is switched; the toggle sits in the header of both, and the zoom controls appear only in the graph.

A click lights the lineage

Clicking create ring of invisibility lights it and every path back to the foundations — ARTI, INVI, ILLU and the three foundations — and drops everything else to 22%. A second click on that same lit skill opens the game data dictionary; clicking a different skill moves the light. A click on empty background, or the Show all button, puts the graph back.

The keyboard

The graph is one focus stop, not seventy. Tab moves to the graph, then on to Close; inside it the arrows pan and the view answers. A keyboard reader who wants to walk the skills one by one uses the branch view, which is a list of buttons and says so.

KeyDoes
Tabthe graph surface, as a single stop
← ↑ → ↓pan the view
+ −zoom in and out, one whole step at a time
0fit the whole graph
Escapecloses the dialog — as every dialog in this workspace does, whether or not a skill is lit

A screen reader meeting the graph hears: “Whole graph: 70 magic skills in 5 tiers, drawn as a diagram. Arrow keys pan, plus and minus zoom. Switch to Branches to read the skills as a list.”
