3 · The states this has to survive

Whichever option is chosen, these are the cases the mockups above do not show, and they are the next thing to settle: a long route (eight steps over three months, roughly twice the height drawn here); the planner armed but with no route yet (64 px, which fits today); a refusal (“The sea is in the way at (8,52)”); a foreign unit selected, where the panel only says the unit cannot be planned for; and a taller window, where there is room for all three and none of this bites.
