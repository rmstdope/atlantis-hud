Option A — the editor simply opens

Every own unit standing in the current turn gets a working editor, whether or not the report’s Orders Template happened to list it. There is no notice and no button: the block is written into the orders document the moment the first order is typed, and until then nothing is added to the file. The rules are on our side here — the template is a convenience, not a permission list: “This template gives you a formatted orders form, with all of your units listed. You may use this to fill in your orders, or write them on your own… or turning it off altogether” (rules/reportformat, rules/option).
