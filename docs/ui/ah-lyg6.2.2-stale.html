ah-lyg6.2.2 — a mage you have not heard about this turn

Your report is turn 23. The newest mage sheet Selado (34) ever sent you is turn 20, so three months of Mordoc's studying have happened that no sheet describes. The bead says such a mage is shown and flagged, never dropped, and his progress since his sheet's turn estimated — and leaves what "estimated" means on screen to this conversation.

What the game actually lets us say (rules/skills_studying): level 1 costs one month of study, level 2 two more, level 3 three more, and so on. What the published rules do not say is how many of a report's point numbers — spirit [SPIR] 3 (180) — one month is worth. Every variant below is a different answer to that gap.

Chosen: S3, the estimate counted from the report's own point numbers at thirty a month. S1 (no projection) and S2 (the rules' month arithmetic alone) were rejected for the sharper estimate, knowing that thirty points a month is inferred from the fixtures and not published in the rules. The interview is recorded in bead ah-lyg6.2.2's design field.

S1 — say what is not known, and no more

The levels are exactly the ones the sheet carried. Nothing is projected.

S1 The sheet's own numbers, plainly dated

The section head carries the age and the size of the gap; the rows are tinted so a mage from an old sheet cannot be mistaken for one from this turn's report.

S2 — the rules' own month arithmetic, as a ceiling

Each skill he holds gains "up to N" where N is the highest level those unreported months could have bought, counting from the level the sheet stated.

S2 "Spirit 3 → up to 4"

Three unreported months. Spirit 3 → 4 needs four months, so Spirit cannot have moved; Force 1 → 2 needs two, and 2 → 3 another three, so Force could be 2. Every arrow is an upper bound and the header says so once.

S3 — the same, from the report's point numbers

A report writes stealth [STEA] 5 (450), and 450 is fifteen months at thirty a month — so the application could count part-finished levels too, if it asserts that rate.

S3 "Spirit 3 (180) → 4 next month"

Mordoc's sheet said spirit 3 (270) — ninety points past level 3, so three more months puts him at 360, which is level 4. The estimate becomes exact, at the price of a number the published rules never state.
