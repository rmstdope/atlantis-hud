ah-lyg6.2.2 — the study planner pane: how every mage is laid out

A pane of its own listing every mage in one place — your own from the loaded report, every ally's from the mage sheets you have taken in. Read-only in this bead. Choosing next turn's study and writing a per-mage note is ah-lyg6.2.3; teaching is ah-lyg6.3; the export is ah-lyg6.4 — but the layout chosen here is the one those three build on, so a Study column and a Note column will join whichever shape you pick.

The depth view already exists: F3, the magic tree, tinted for one mage at a time. This pane is the breadth view — everyone at once — which is why none of the variants below tries to redraw seventy skills.

The same fictional game throughout: your faction Wardens of the North (12) on turn 23, an ally Borg (21) whose sheet is also turn 23, and an ally Selado (34) whose newest sheet is turn 20.

Chosen: variant C, the mage list beside a mage detail — with A's faction grouping kept inside its list. A and B were rejected for the room the detail gives, knowing that ah-lyg6.2.3's per-mage note then becomes one visit per mage. The empty, loading and error wording below was chosen as drawn. The interview is recorded in bead ah-lyg6.2.2's design field.

Variant A — grouped by faction

A section per faction, yours first, each headed with whose mages these are and which turn they were last heard of. One row per mage.

A Faction sections, one row per mage

The grouping is the same one ah-lyg6.4 exports by, so what you see is what a sent order file will be grouped into. Staleness belongs to a faction, so it is stated once in the section head rather than repeated on every row.

Variant B — one table, sortable

Every mage in one list with a Faction column, and clickable headers. Opens sorted the way A groups.

B One flat table

The same rows, ungrouped, with staleness carried on the row instead of a section head. Clicking a column head re-sorts — so "everyone who may begin something new, most first" is one click away, and so is "all my Spirit mages".
