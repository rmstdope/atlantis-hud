ah-fjty — the unclaimed fund pays a unit's upkeep

Step 7 of the rules' maintenance payment order, and it needs no order: “If you have silver in your unclaimed fund, then that silver will be automatically claimed by units that would otherwise starve.”

Today the whole warning path is blind to it. FactionPurse is built once and handed only to the Silver column's forecast; charge_upkeep and report_shortfalls never see it. So a young faction still holding most of its 10,000 starting silver is told its units are about to starve — a false alarm in the commonest situation the game has.

1–2. Food (CONSUME)  ·  3. The unit's own silver  ·  4. Faction silver in the region  ·  5–6. Food again  ·  7. Unclaimed silver  ·  8–9. Allied

Steps 4 and 5–6 are ah-e66j and ah-eacd. They reduce the shortfall this bead then settles the fund against, so the three do not collide.

When the fund is short the total is exact, the blame is not

Same hex, late in the game, header Unclaimed silver: $100. Three units owe 160 of maintenance they cannot pay. The fund pays 100. 60 is genuinely short, and nothing in the report says which unit goes without — the engine drains the fund in its own order.

Scouts (101), un-rescued. The pessimistic answer: assume this unit is the one that goes without. Always a number, never optimistic, and the note says the faction may yet reach it.
Chosen over ?. Doubting the figure would have matched contested faction food and ah-1wcw.1's never a number that might be wrong — but in a poor faction every short unit's column goes to ? at once, and ? is less useful than a number. The accepted cost: summing the column across units overstates the faction's real shortfall, because each is shown its whole fee while the fund will in fact pay 100 of the 160.

So this bead adds no new doubt variant, and never makes At month end unknown.

CLAIM spends the same fund, and spends it first

CLAIM is an order and resolves during the month; maintenance is settled at its end. So a claim takes its silver out of the fund before any starving unit reaches it.

Unclaimed: $8,450     CLAIM 8,400 (unit 107)     unpayable upkeep across the faction: $160

  fund left for upkeep = 8,450 - 8,400 = 50     ->  110 short, and all three units are named
claims-exceed-unclaimed is untouched: it asks whether the claims overrun the fund, which is a different question from whether what is left feeds the units. The two share one total_claimed helper so the fund cannot be spent twice by two pieces of code that disagree — which is what ah-ycuj was filed about.

The second row is not arbitrary against the fourth. With $1 somebody is partly rescued, so blame is undeterminable and the message must speak for the faction; with $0 every unit's shortfall is exactly its own, and a broke faction keeps the line-level marks it most needs. Absence is not evidence of an empty fund — check_claims already declines to fire for the same reason.

{available} is the fund after this month's claims, not the header figure. {n} is silver, not a count, matching its two shipped neighbours. The finding's $ signs are literal as in claims-exceed-unclaimed; the hover notes carry none, as in the two food notes. The new check joins Settings → Resources beneath “Claiming more than the faction has”, and is on by default like every check but hex-unguarded.

Rejected and why

ConsideredRejected because
Warning only — leave the Silver column aloneKeeps ah-1wcw.1's “the column counts each unit alone”, and keeps the drain visible. But it leaves a unit red with no warning and nothing explaining it — the divergence ah-abwx and ah-ycuj were filed about.
Column untouched, but a hover noteCheaper than moving both, and still shows a negative number that will not happen.
One faction-scope finding instead of one per claimantQuiet and it scales, but it has no line to point at, so nothing marks the units in the orders being edited.
Rescue in report order, warn the restPrecise-looking and invented. ah-7cdt rejected exactly this for the food pool: the order is ours, not the engine's.
? for a contended fundConsistent with contested faction food, and the planner's own recommendation — but ? everywhere at once is less useful than a pessimistic number.
The fund-aware message for a stated $0One message shape whenever the header states a figure, and it says the fund is empty — but it costs a broke faction its line-level marks every turn.
Reuse not-enough-silver rather than a new codeNo new switch, but turning off “Overspent silver” would then also silence every starvation warning.
Ignoring CLAIM when sizing the fundSimpler, and wrong in exactly the situation where the fund matters most.
