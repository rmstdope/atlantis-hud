A unit formed this month — two things rounds 1–3 did not reach

Bead ah-jw85, round 4. Rounds 1–3 settled the figure (Option A: priced exactly like any other unit), the mark (W2), where a click goes (C1: the forming unit), what the Problems panel calls it (N2: new 1) and the understated message (M2). Nothing below re-opens any of those.

Why these two arrive now. To price a formed unit at all, the orders inside its FORM block have to be filed under it — today they are read by nothing whatsoever, so no check has ever seen them. Filing them makes them visible to every check at once, not only to the Silver arithmetic. That is question 1, and it is the larger of the two.

Question 1 · Which checks a formed unit faces

The app has 33 checks. All of them walk the units of a hex; none has ever met a unit with no report row, no skills and no men. Once the FORM block's orders are filed under the new unit, whether those checks see it is a choice — and it is visible, because each one that fires puts a line in the Problems panel.

S1Silver only

The formed unit enters the silver arithmetic and nothing else. The column, the ⚠, and the not-enough-silver entry. Every other check goes on skipping it, exactly as today.

Problems

  • ⚠new 1short $100: this unit can have $100 and its orders spend $200
  • Only what this bead is about appears. Nothing else on screen moves.
  • No check has to be re-reasoned against a unit that has no skills and no men.
  • A BUY of goods the market does not sell stays silent inside a FORM block, and warns one line above it.

S2Every check

The formed unit is an ordinary unit everywhere. All 33 checks run over it, exactly as they run over 1922.

Problems

  • ⚠new 1short $100: this unit can have $100 and its orders spend $200
  • ⚠new 1cannot produce lumber: needs Lumberjack 1, has no Lumberjack
  • The inside of a FORM block stops being a blind spot in the checker.
  • The second line is wrong and unfixable. The unit has no skills because it was formed this second; the men and their skills arrive by GIVE or BUY, which the checks do not model. The player cannot silence it by writing a better order.
  • Every skill-reading and men-reading check has the same defect, so this is a family, not one line.

S3Silver, plus the checks that read only the order and the hex

The silver arithmetic, plus the handful that need nothing of the unit itself — whether the market sells what a BUY names, whether a GIVE's target is in this hex. Everything that reads skills, men or the unit's stock keeps skipping it.

Problems

  • ⚠new 1short $100: this unit can have $100 and its orders spend $200

Nothing more here — the market does sell Plainsmen. Had the block said BUY 5 MITH, an entry would read this hex does not buy mithril — its market has ….

  • Catches the mistakes a player can actually act on, and stays quiet about the ones they cannot.
  • The dividing line is a judgement made check by check, so it is a list somebody has to maintain as checks are added.
  • Larger than this bead: it is a second decision about 33 checks, taken inside a bug about one column.
Chosen: S2 — every check. The recommendation below was S1 and was not taken; it is kept so the reasoning that was weighed against S2 is on the record.

The recommendation was S1. It is the only one of the three whose blast radius is the bead's own subject. S2 ships a warning the player is told to fix and cannot, on every PRODUCE and every skill-reading check in every FORM block. S3 is right in principle and is a bead of its own — it needs the 33 checks gone through one at a time, and doing that here would bury the column fix underneath it. If you pick S1 I will say in the plan that the inside of a FORM block is still unchecked, so it can be filed separately.

Question 2 · What the Id cell does on a formed row

Every row's Id cell is a button: it selects that unit and takes the map to it. On a formed row it reads new-1 and there is nothing to select. C1 already settled the ⚠ and the Problems entry — both go to the forming unit. The Id cell was not part of that question, and it is the same click on the same row.
