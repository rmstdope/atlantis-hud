A mage sheet arrives — Option B: it just takes it in

Same file, same marker, same refusals. Option B asks nothing: opening a mage sheet takes it in, and the status line under the header says what happened — the way loading your own turn report does.

Compare with Option A, which stops and asks in a box under the header before keeping anything.

The ordinary case

One step: open the file, and this is all you see

The Disinherited Knights (42)turn 23 ImportExport
6 mages from Borg (21), turn 23, taken in

When it replaced a sheet you held

The Disinherited Knights (42)turn 23 ImportExport
6 mages from Borg (21), turn 23, taken in — replacing turn 21

A faction with no mages

The Disinherited Knights (42)turn 23 ImportExport
Borg (21) had no mages on turn 23 — the sheet is empty, and it was taken in
  • One step. A sheet you asked an ally for is not a file you need to be asked about twice.
  • Nothing on screen moves — no turn change, no map change — so there is nothing to undo and nothing to be startled by.
  • No chance to say no. The only way back from an unwanted sheet is a newer one, or (later) Forget in the chip's popover.
  • The one line of status is the whole account of what arrived, and it is gone on the next action.
  • Differs from a map export, which does ask — two files that arrive the same way behave differently.
