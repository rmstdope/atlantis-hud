ah-bai — completing the words after the order name

The completion popup speaks only in the command position — the first word of a line. Everything after it is on the player to remember and spell, including the words the ruleset fixes to a short closed list. This is the interview that decided what changes, held with the navigator on 2026-08-16. The full record, including what was rejected, is in bead ah-bai.1's design field.

Chosen. The popup gains a second source that answers argument positions from the ruleset's own grammar: the closed keyword lists, the six directions with IN/OUT, and the 22 item classes (ah-bai.1), then the game catalogue's 171 items and 96 skills (ah-bai.2). It stays quiet until a letter is typed, says nothing when what is typed matches nothing, accepts on Enter or Tab as today, and leaves a trailing space behind — in every position, the command position included. Entries are bare words with the icon a command gets, in the ruleset's own order; the 22 item classes alphabetically, as the one exception.

The trailing space can leave two spaces where a word is completed in the middle of a line. That is accepted: the lexer skips runs of blanks, so the orders file means exactly the same thing.
