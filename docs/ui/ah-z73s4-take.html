ah-z73s.4 — a TAKE of men is a third route, and nothing has named it

I checked the shipped code rather than trusting the plan. Since ah-dxfd.1, a TAKE of men is followed and merged exactly as a GIVE is — the test men_taken_rather_than_given_are_judged_on_what_arrives pins it. So this advisory can fire on men that nobody gave, and the agreed sentence would say “giving” about them.

Everything else stays as you chose it. This is the one sentence the earlier rounds could not have asked about, because they were written from the plan rather than the tree.

The case

unit 3100
TAKE FROM 2200 5 HUMN    five unskilled men taken, not given
PRODUCE LUMB

The rules put GIVE and TAKE in the same phase — Give orders in rules/sequenceofevents — and rules/take says a TAKE is “a GIVE order with the direction reversed”. Both run before Market orders, so buying is always last.

E1 — a verb of its own, and the joined sentence lists whichever routes apply
Problems
take only⚠ 3100 Timber crew — taking 5 men into this unit lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 2
give + take⚠ 3100 Timber crew — giving 5 men to this unit and taking 2 more lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 1
all three⚠ 3100 Timber crew — giving 5 men to this unit, taking 2 more and buying 4 more lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 1
  • Follows every rule you have already chosen: name the order the player wrote, name every route that brought men, and give the report's level and the month's level.
  • Bounded — there are exactly three routes, so the sentence can never grow past three clauses.
  • The three-clause row is long, and it is the one a narrow panel will wrap worst.
E2 — taken men count as given
Problems
take only⚠ 3100 Timber crew — giving 5 men to this unit lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 2
give + take⚠ 3100 Timber crew — giving 7 men to this unit lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 1
all three⚠ 3100 Timber crew — giving 7 men to this unit and buying 4 more lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 1
  • Two clauses at most, and nothing new to word or test. The rules do call a TAKE a reversed GIVE.
  • Says “giving” about men the unit took. A player searching their orders for a GIVE to this unit will not find one.
E3 — route-neutral whenever a TAKE was involved
Problems
take only⚠ 3100 Timber crew — 5 men joining this unit lower its lumberjack from 3 to 2
give + take⚠ 3100 Timber crew — 7 men joining this unit lower its lumberjack from 3 to 1
all three⚠ 3100 Timber crew — 11 men joining this unit lower its lumberjack from 3 to 1
  • Keeps the give and buy sentences exactly as agreed, and never grows past one clause.
  • A fourth shape, with its own count agreement, that appears only for the rarest route — so the same unit can be described two different ways in two consecutive months.
  • Names no order at all in the case where naming one would help most.
My recommendation is E1. It is the only option that keeps the rule you have chosen three times already — name the order the player actually wrote — and a TAKE of men is rare enough that the three-clause row will almost never be drawn.
