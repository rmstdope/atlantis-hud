Round 2 — the cases the happy path hides

Settled in round 1: derived skills appear in the export, the units table and the Unit panel; a derived skill carries the turn in the cell; and the scan reads every stored turn. This page is the rest of it — the states, the words, and the two rules a reader would otherwise have to guess.

D Two battles disagree about the same unit

This is real, not hypothetical: 70 of 595 repeated observations in one game disagreed, and 68 of the 70 were the level going up. Unit 8113's crossbow reads 4 at turn 71 and 5 at turn 72.

D1 — the newest observation wins   recommended
UnitNameSkills
–8113BowmenXBOW 5 (turn 72)
  • The plain reading of “what do we know now”, and it is right 68 times out of 70 by construction.
  • Same rule the Army member snapshot already follows: the last report that showed it wins.
  • In the two cases where the level fell, this reports the fall — which may be a unit that lost its veterans, or may be noise.
D2 — the highest ever seen wins
UnitNameSkills
–8113BowmenXBOW 5 (turn 72)
  • Treats a skill as a floor, which is what it mostly is — you cannot unlearn.
  • Identical to D1 in 68 of the 70 real disagreements; the two rules differ only where the level fell.
  • The turn shown would then be the turn of the highest reading, not the latest — so the cell says “turn 68” about a unit last seen at turn 72, which reads as staler than it is.

F Whose stored reports does the scan read?

A game can hold reports from more than one faction — an ally's report can be merged in. Turns are keyed by (game, faction, turn), and the turn comparison picker deliberately filters to the active faction only.

  • F1 — every stored turn of the game, whoever's report it is. A battle roster is a fact about the world; a battle your ally watched happened just as much as one you watched. Every report in the game is one you imported yourself, so nothing is revealed that you did not already have on disk. recommended
  • F2 — only the faction whose report is on screen. Matches what the comparison picker does. Consistent, and narrower; also throws away battles you are holding, for a reason that does not really apply — the picker filters because a turn 40 of a different faction is a different document, not because the world is different.

Unit 8113's crossbow 4→5 between turns 71 and 72, and unit 5284's riding 4→5, are two of the 70 real disagreements in neworigins-3.0.0-g7-f95-t71/t72.rep. Watazka's three skills are its actual roster line at turn 71.
