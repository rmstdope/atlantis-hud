Hex coordinates on the map (agreed)

The agreed design. With Hex coordinates on (under a new Grid heading at the bottom of the Badges menu, on by default, left alone by All and None, never lighting the Badges button), every known hex shows its coordinates as 33,25 in small print just inside its top edge. On a hex with a settlement name they join the name on its line, e.g. Kerrin 33,25. They show at close and middle zoom, are hidden zoomed far out, and stay full strength on faded hexes. Hexes nobody has seen get none. Try the Zoom buttons and the switch.

Zoom: · the Badges menu is open over the map and its switch works

Drawn in the default map look. Kerrin (a city) and Ashwell (a town) carry their names; blue and red shields are your units and others, with counts beneath; the red diamond is a monster. Dashed outlines are hexes nobody has seen, and they get no coordinates. The new switch is outlined in blue dashes.
