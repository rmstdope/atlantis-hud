ah-w544.2 — saying that a line of the turn report could not be read

Decided 2026-08-22, over four rounds. A turn report line the parser cannot read is dropped today with no trace at all, and ParsedReport carries no error channel — so a player simply does not see one of their own units and has no way to know. This page is the agreed surface for saying so. It is the second half of epic ah-w544; the first half, ah-w544.1, removes the commonest cause.

The decisions: a header chip that drops a panel (not a status line, not a banner); the widest definition of unreadable, region blocks included; a kind column with the cost of a lost hex spelled out; line ranges; no cap; a Copy all shaped for pasting into a bug report; rebuilt from the loaded report rather than stored or dismissed.

Chosen the chip, and the panel it opens

The chip sits with the other header chips and is absent entirely when there is nothing to say — which is every healthy report. Amber always, whatever is in the list: its job is to get the panel opened, and the panel does the explaining.

Keyboard and focus

The chip behaves exactly as the events chip beside it. Where the two disagree, the events chip wins.

BehaviourAgreed
Tab orderA real button in the header's natural order, immediately after the events chip.
OpenEnter or Space. Focus moves to the panel heading.
CloseEscape, the ✕, or a click outside. Focus returns to the chip in every case.
ShortcutNone. The header has no single-key shortcuts today and this does not introduce the first.
Copy allIn the panel's tab order after the list; label becomes Copied for two seconds.
PersistenceNone. The chip is rebuilt from the loaded report, follows a turn switch, returns on reload, and cannot be dismissed.

Rejected and why, so nobody re-opens them

ConsideredRejected because
Status line + modalThe status line is one shared channel; the next message overwrites it and there is then no way back to the list. A modal also interrupts to report something the player cannot act on.
Inline banner under the headerSteals map height, its dismissal is one-way, and it would be a third notice pattern in the same 100px of header.
Only marker lines (*/-/+)Misses a whole lost hex, which is the largest silent loss there is.
A cap on the listA truncated list is a second lie about the same report.
Grouping by file after a batchThe chip would mean two different things depending on how you last loaded, and it needs storage outliving the load.
A red chip when a hex was lostA longer chip in a header that already wraps, plus a second string with plural forms. The panel explains; the chip only invites.
Dropping the kind column when narrowHides information at exactly the width where the raw text is hardest to read.
Making the chip dismissableThen the dismissal has to be remembered somewhere, and a dismissed loss is a silent loss again.
