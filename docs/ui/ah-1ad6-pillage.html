ah-1ad6 — PILLAGE credited to a faction that cannot pillage

Found by Psylocke verifying ah-abwx. That bead correctly taught the Silver column to credit PILLAGE with twice the region's tax base. Neither surface checks whether the faction may pillage at all.

“To do this requires the faction to have enough combat ready men in the region to tax half of the available money in the region.”
“Only combat-ready units may issue this order.”

A taxer collects $50, so half of $8,963 needs 90 men. The observed case — The Lost One (683), a single leader, avoiding and behind, in mountain (36,4) — is credited the full $17,926 and would in truth earn nothing.

The rules page never defines “combat ready”. It uses the phrase three times and explains it nowhere, and the scraped ruleset marks no item as a weapon — its combat block is on monsters only. So the exact test cannot be built from what we have.

But it does not have to be. Combat-ready men are a subset of men, so if the faction's whole headcount in the region is below the threshold, it certainly cannot pillage — no definition needed. That test is one-sided, and what to do above the threshold is the question.

Question 1 what to do above the threshold

Below it — headcount alone too small — every option agrees: PILLAGE earns 0, certainly. The observed case is here, and every option fixes it.

OptionA hex with 200 of our men, tax base $8,963 (needs 90)
A — credit in full Income 17,926, as today. The headcount is sufficient, so the faction probably can pillage. Overstates whenever those 200 men are unarmed peasants. Smallest change, and it fixes the reported defect exactly.
B — doubt it Income ? — “The report does not say which of these men are combat ready.” Honest: we genuinely cannot tell. But every PILLAGE above the threshold shows ? for ever, including the ones that plainly work.
C — scrape weapons first Income 17,926 or 0, counted properly from who holds a weapon. The item descriptions do say it — “This is a slashing weapon… No skill is needed to wield this weapon” — so a scraper could learn it, as ah-19l2.1 is doing for production. A ruleset bead plus this one.

Worth weighing: there are zero PILLAGE orders in any committed turn in the corpus. Psylocke found this by writing one during verification.
