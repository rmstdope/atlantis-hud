ah-af7i — does the pupil list stay live, or is it a snapshot?

Independent of which pupils arrive ticked. The question is what a teach cell means once it is set, and it only shows itself when the rest of the plan changes afterwards.

The story below is three edits in a row, on the same hex, turn 26. Six of Seven teaches; Two, Four and Five of Seven are studying skills he outranks. Then the player gives Three of Seven, who had nothing planned, a month of force shield — making him a fourth eligible pupil.

Chosen: Q — live until edited. An untouched teach cell means “teach whoever is eligible this turn” and is recomputed as the rest of the plan changes; the first tick or untick freezes it into a fixed list. The heading carries — everyone eligible and the grid cell reads TEACH everyone (3) while it is live. Rejected: P, the snapshot, which leaves a teacher quietly under-used whenever a pupil becomes eligible after the cell was set, with nothing on screen saying so. Paired with A — see the companion mockup, teach-pupils-preselected.html. The full interview is in bead ah-af7i's design field.
