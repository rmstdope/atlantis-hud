Pillaging counts against the faction's region limit

A faction may tax and trade in only so many regions a month, set by its Martial points (rules/tablefactionpoints). The order editor already warns when this month's orders go past that limit — but it counts only PRODUCE and TAX. A faction pillaging its way across its own regions can go past the limit and hear nothing.

Already settled by the team: only PRODUCE, TAX (including units set to tax every month) and PILLAGE use up a region. BUY, SELL and TRANSPORT do not — the team checked the game itself on TRANSPORT, whatever the rules page says.

Chosen: 1A, the warning sits on the first order that counts toward the limit it warns about. 2A, every order that counts toward that limit is always named, and the Settings line becomes “Taxing and trading in too many regions”. 3A, older reports with two limits get a second warning, for TAX and PILLAGE against the tax limit.
Rejected: 1B (first PRODUCE only, which leaves a faction that produces nothing unwarned), 2B (naming only the kinds ordered this month), 3B (leaving older reports unchanged).

Example faction on every screen below: limit 10 regions, orders taxing in 6, pillaging in 4 more, producing in 2 more — 12 regions in all.

1 · Which order the warning sits on

The warning belongs to the whole faction, not one hex

It still has to sit on one line the player can click. Today it sits on the first PRODUCE order — so a faction that only taxes and pillages, with no PRODUCE anywhere, is never warned at all.

No warning. Pillaging counts toward the total, but only once some unit produces.
  • The warning stays on the same line it always has.
  • A faction that taxes and pillages but produces nothing can still go over the limit in silence — the gap this work is about, left half open.

2 · The words

The warning, and its line in Settings

Today: PRODUCE and TAX orders in 12 regions; this faction may tax and trade in 10, so 2 regions' orders will be refused, listed in Settings as “Producing in too many regions”.

2A · always name all three
WarningPRODUCE, TAX and PILLAGE orders in 12 regions; this faction may tax and trade in 10, so 2 regions' orders will be refused
Warning · limit of nonethis faction may not tax or trade in any region, so every PRODUCE, TAX and PILLAGE order will be refused
Taxing and trading in too many regions
PRODUCE, TAX and PILLAGE orders in more regions than the faction's allowance permits.
  • One fixed sentence; the player always learns the full list of what counts.
  • Names orders the player may not have written this month.
2B · name only the kinds actually ordered
WarningTAX and PILLAGE orders in 12 regions; this faction may tax and trade in 10, so 2 regions' orders will be refused
Warning · limit of nonethis faction may not tax or trade in any region, so every PILLAGE order will be refused
Taxing and trading in too many regions
PRODUCE, TAX and PILLAGE orders in more regions than the faction's allowance permits.
  • Reads as a description of this month's own orders.
  • The sentence changes shape with the orders; the player never sees that BUY or TRANSPORT were left out on purpose — Settings is the only place the full list lives.

Settings is drawn the same in both: the old title “Producing in too many regions” stops being true once taxing and pillaging count. Say if you'd rather keep it.

3 · Older reports, which print two separate limits

“Tax Regions: 14 (15)” and “Trade Regions: 15 (15)”

Some games still send reports with a separate tax limit and trade limit. There, today's warning compares only PRODUCE against the trade limit — taxing is not checked against anything.
