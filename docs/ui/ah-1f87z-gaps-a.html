Option A keep the gap as an empty block, write the whole plan

Ereb has a turn with nothing planned (75) between two studies; Vess has a turn whose study the planner refuses (74 — he needs pattern 1 first). The game runs TURN blocks on successive turns, one each, so a gap must either be kept as an empty block or everything after it dropped.

Option A — empty block for a gap

A turn with nothing planned, or a refused study, becomes an empty TURN block saying why, so the turns after it still land on the right month. Turns at the end of the schedule with nothing planned are simply not written.
