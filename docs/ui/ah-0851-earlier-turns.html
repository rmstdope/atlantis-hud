Fetching every earlier turn — what the player is told

Fetch, with This turn's report and every earlier turn not yet loaded chosen, is meant to bring down every turn the world holds for the faction. Today it brings down only this turn, then says the world has no report for you yet — which sends the player looking for a report that is sitting right there.

Getting all the turns down is simply a fix; it changes nothing on screen. What needs deciding is what the player reads when things don't go to plan. The example is a faction on turn 84 in Arcanum. The dialog, its fields and the working lines are unchanged and not shown again.

Chosen: Q1 B · Q2 A · Q3 A · Q4 A · Q5 B.

Q1 · The list of earlier turns can't be read — what it says

This turn has already loaded. Then the world's answer about which earlier turns it holds can't be made sense of. Only the wording differs between A and B; both appear in the same place.

today
Export ▾FetchSend
Arcanum would not say which turns it holds: the world has no report for you yet.
Blames the world, and suggests a missing report. Neither is true.

APlain and short

Says what happened and what it cost. Doesn't say whose fault it is.

Export ▾FetchSend
turn 84 loaded, but Arcanum's list of earlier turns could not be read, so none were fetched.
the same frame, for the other causes
turn 84 loaded, but Arcanum's list of earlier turns could not be fetched: could not reach atlantis-newage.com.
turn 84 loaded, but Arcanum's list of earlier turns could not be fetched: the world refused the request (500).
  • Fits one line on a normal window.
  • Leaves the player unsure whether trying again would help.

BOwns the fault

Says plainly that Atlantis HUD, not the world or the faction, is the problem — so the player knows to report it rather than retry.

Export ▾FetchSend
turn 84 loaded. Atlantis HUD did not understand Arcanum's list of earlier turns, so none were fetched — trying again will not help.
the other causes keep A's frame — those are not the app's fault
turn 84 loaded, but Arcanum's list of earlier turns could not be fetched: could not reach atlantis-newage.com.
  • Stops a player retrying something that can't work.
  • Longer; wraps on a narrow window.
  • Two frames for one kind of failure.

Q2 · Where that message appears

Every other fetch ends by closing the dialog and letting the status line have the last word. Should this one?

AStatus line, as every fetch ends today

Dialog closes itself. The amber line from Q1 is the only trace.

Export ▾FetchSend
turn 84 loaded, but Arcanum's list of earlier turns could not be read, so none were fetched.
  • Consistent with every other way a fetch ends.
  • Easy to miss: the next thing written to the status line replaces it.

BDialog stays up and says it

This turn is already loaded behind the dialog; the dialog stays until the player closes it.

Fetch from New Age: Arcanum

atlantis-newage.com · turn 84
turn 84 loaded, but Arcanum's list of earlier turns could not be read, so none were fetched.
Close
  • Can't be missed.
  • The only fetch outcome that needs a click to dismiss — a new pattern.

Q3 · Nothing earlier was missing

Every earlier turn is already in the game — say, the second press of Fetch on the same faction, or a faction on its very first turn. Today this shows a red failure, as if something broke.

today
no turns could be fetched; still showing turn 84.

ASay so, calmly

every earlier turn was already loaded; still showing turn 84.
  • The player knows the earlier turns were checked.

BOnly this turn's line

As if they had asked for this turn alone.

turn 84 loaded — 4 regions, 11 units.
  • Nothing extra to read.
  • Can't tell "nothing to fetch" apart from "didn't look".

Q4 · Some earlier turns came down and some didn't

Now that the list will be read, this is the case a player with 84 turns is most likely to meet.

AA count, as today

81 turns stored for history, 2 could not be fetched; still showing turn 84.
  • Unchanged; short.
  • The player can't tell which turns to look for.

BName the turns

81 turns stored for history; turns 12 and 40 could not be fetched; still showing turn 84.
many failed — the first three, then a count
60 turns stored for history; turns 3, 7, 12 and 20 more could not be fetched; still showing turn 84.
  • The player knows exactly what is missing.
  • Longer line.

Q5 · The world holds no earlier turns at all

A faction on its very first turn: the world's list comes back with only this turn in it. You chose Q3 A, every earlier turn was already loaded — but here there were never any to load. The example is turn 0.

AThe same line as Q3

every earlier turn was already loaded; still showing turn 0.
  • One sentence for "nothing to fetch", whatever the reason.
  • Hints at turns that never existed.

BIts own line

Arcanum holds no earlier turns for this faction; still showing turn 0.
  • True in both cases it covers.
  • One more sentence to keep in step.
