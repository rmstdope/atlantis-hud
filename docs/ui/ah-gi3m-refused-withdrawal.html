ah-gi3m — the SILVER hover and a withdrawal the game will refuse

Today unitTooltip.ts:1085 says “This unit's withdrawal is paid from the faction's unclaimed silver.” for any WITHDRAW of more than zero. Two of those the game refuses outright — in the Nexus (rules/withdraw: “Withdraw CANNOT be used in the Nexus”), and naming anything that is not a basic item (rules/withdraw: “if you try withdraw any other than a basic item, an error will be given”). For both, nothing is withdrawn, nothing is paid, and the sentence explains a zero that needs no explaining.

Settled either way, and not the question: the sentence goes away for a refused withdrawal. The question is what, if anything, takes its place in the hover. Note that the refusal is already reported elsewhere — each of these orders is underlined in the editor and listed in Problems by an advisory that shipped in ah-x6do.

Chosen 2026-09-09 — option A, silence. The sentence goes away for a refused withdrawal and nothing takes its place; the advisory on the order's own line already names the reason. Case 4 stands as drawn: a unit with one honoured and one refused withdrawal keeps the honoured one's sentence.

In B and C the two sentences sit side by side and read a little oddly — “paid from the unclaimed silver” and “nothing is withdrawn” are both true, of different orders. In A there is one sentence and the underlined order says the rest.

What each option costs

OptionWhat the hover says when a withdrawal is refusedCost
A — silence Nothing about the withdrawal. Usually “Nothing this unit is ordered to do moves silver.” The refusal is on the order's own line and in Problems. Smallest. One boolean stops being set; no new field crosses the wire.
B — one sentence “The game will refuse this unit's withdrawal, so nothing is withdrawn.” — same words for both refusals. One new boolean on UnitSilver, regenerated into TypeScript. One new note.
C — the reason “Only basic items can be withdrawn, …” or “Nothing can be withdrawn in the Nexus, …” The reason has to cross the wire, so the flag becomes a small enum, plus two notes. Says in the hover what the advisory beside it already says.

A case for A

Both refusals already produce an advisory that underlines the order and appears in Problems, with a message that names the reason and the item. The hover's job here was only ever to explain an Out of zero on a unit that is withdrawing; where the game refuses the order there is no zero to explain, and the second copy of the reason in the hover is a sentence that will be read once and never again.
