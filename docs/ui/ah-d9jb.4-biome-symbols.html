Biome symbols on the map (agreed)

The agreed design. Biome symbols sits under its own Terrain heading at the bottom of the Badges menu and is off until a player turns it on; it is shown on here so the map can be seen with it. Symbols are scattered over each hex in plain ink and leave out any spot that would fall on a settlement, unit counter, monster, building or road. They show at close and middle zoom and are hidden when zoomed far out. All and None in the menu do not touch it, and having it off does not light the Badges button.
