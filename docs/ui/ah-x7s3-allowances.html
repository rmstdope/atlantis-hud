Chosen (bead ah-x7s3). Regions counts the hexes this turn's orders tax (including autotax), pillage or produce in, never BUY, SELL or TRANSPORT, exactly as the Production window counts. Quartermasters, Mages and Apprentices start from the report's figure and rise by one for each unit whose orders this turn study quartermaster, a magic foundation (force, pattern, spirit) or manipulation for the first time. Counts never go down from orders. No "last turn" figure, and no caption telling the rows apart. Over a limit: red bar, red number, red note. The limit after the slash is always the report's.
