what B costs, and what it buys

Buys: back-filling a whole game is one visit and one button. Room for the season and the mark without truncation, and a per-row failure that does not stop the rest.

Costs: a new modal surface — focus trap, Escape, focus return, and the awkward two-modal case above. A run of fetches that can half-fail needs its own summary. Roughly twice the work of A.

6 · One correction to the drawings above

The world's turn list is numbers and nothing else — it says which turns it holds, not what season each was. A season is only known for a turn the game has already parsed and stored, so a row that has not been fetched shows — where the season would be, and fills it in once the turn lands. That is why the rows above are drawn that way; the first draft of this page showed a season on every row, which the world cannot supply.
