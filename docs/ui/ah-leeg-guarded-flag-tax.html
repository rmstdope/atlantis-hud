ah-leeg — a unit taxing by its flag, in a hex a foreigner guards

A foreign unit on guard may stop us collecting tax. A unit with a TAX line is already told so, on that line. A unit that taxes by its taxing flag collects nothing there for the same reason, but has no TAX line to hang the mark on — so the mark hangs on the unit's block, as ah-fvzu already does for the pillaged-hex mark.
