ah-sgn6 — the unit that takes it all

One hex, two of our units. Buyers (900) holds 500 and writes three orders: TAX, TAKE FROM 902 ALL SILV, BUY 2 grain. Purse (902) holds 60 and writes nothing. The tax is worth 150; the grain costs 40.

Purse's own row was fixed by ah-42li: it now draws “was taken −60 by Buyers (900)” and ends the month at 0. Buyers' row is the one left. The one term that cannot be priced silences everything: the tax, the purchase and the month end all disappear behind a single ?.

rules/sequenceofevents processes GIVE and TAKE together, before TAX, before the market — so what Buyers takes is exactly Purse's 60, settled before either unit earns anything. That fact is what makes Option A below possible at all.

Chosen: Option A — count it. The line reads took +60, from Purse (902), identical to a stated TAKE FROM 902 60 SILV. The class form (TAKE FROM 902 ALL NORMAL) keeps its doubt, because the ledger returns early for a class selector and the two surfaces would disagree about a BUY ALL.
