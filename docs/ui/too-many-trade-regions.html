Producing in too many regions — what the player sees (bead ah-8om)

A faction may only conduct trade activity — which means producing — in so many regions a month. Order production in one region too many and the engine refuses that region's orders outright: PRODUCE: Faction can't produce in that many regions., five times across three turns of the committed corpus.

The arithmetic works exactly, and I checked it by hand. In neworigins-3.0.0-g3-f42-t41.rep the faction's status block says Trade Regions: 15 (15) and its orders carry PRODUCE in 16 distinct regions. 16 − 15 = 1 region refused; that region is tundra (35,9) in Bedba; and it holds exactly the two units the report errors on. Nothing subtler is going on — every PRODUCE counts, primary or manufactured, basic good or advanced.

The situation

What the report says

Faction Status: Tax Regions: 14 (15) Trade Regions: 15 (15) Quartermasters: 1 (2) Errors during turn: Smiths (12256): PRODUCE: Faction can't produce in that many regions. Miners (11127): PRODUCE: Faction can't produce in that many regions.

Both errored units stand in tundra (35,9) in Bedba — the sixteenth region, and the one that lost.

What the orders say

2103;*** forest (36,0) in Reprau ***
2118@PRODUCE YEW
… fourteen more regions
2891;*** tundra (35,9) in Bedba ***
2903@PRODUCE IRON
2918@PRODUCE swor

Forty PRODUCE orders across sixteen regions. The player cannot see the sixteenth is one too many without counting the whole file by hand, which is exactly what a client is for.

Decided, 2026-08-17. (1) The warning lands on the first PRODUCE order in the document — one line for one mistake, underlined on a real order, and the header count stays honest. (2) The message gives the count, the limit and the consequence, in three forms covering the two report schemas and a faction that may not trade at all. (3) The toggle is Producing in too many regions in the existing Orders group, on by default. A faction-wide row of its own was the honest answer and was deliberately deferred — it would mean teaching findings an optional hex and changing the panel, the region filter and the header count, which is a second bead nobody has filed.
The problem this bead has and the others did not. Every warning the panel shows belongs to a hex — that is what makes it filterable, and what puts it in the right group. This one belongs to the faction. A finding with no hex is silently dropped by the client (orderEditor.ts:194 skips them by design, so it would vanish from the panel and from the header count), and a finding with a blank hex renders as a nameless row. So the question below is not cosmetic: it decides whether the warning can be seen at all.

Where the warning appears Option B was chosen

OPTION A — rejected In every region that produces

The same line under each of the sixteen hexes, wherever the player happens to be looking.

Problems
forest (36,0) in Reprau
⚠PRODUCE orders in 16 regions; this faction may trade in 15
forest (38,0) in Pla'ere
⚠PRODUCE orders in 16 regions; this faction may trade in 15
…fourteen more, all identical
  • Impossible to miss, whichever hex you are working in.
  • Sixteen copies of one sentence, and the header's problem count jumps by sixteen for one mistake.

OPTION B — chosen On the first PRODUCE order in the file

One line, in the hex holding the first PRODUCE the document contains, underlined on that order.

2103;*** forest (36,0) in Reprau ***
2118@PRODUCE YEW
Problems
forest (36,0) in Reprau
13797⚠PRODUCE orders in 16 regions; this faction may trade in 15, so one region's production will be refused
  • One line for one mistake, and the count in the header is honest.
  • Lands on a real order, so the editor underlines something the player can click to.
  • Deterministic and explicable — the first PRODUCE in the file, every time.
  • The hex it appears under is not itself at fault; it is simply the first.

OPTION C — deferred, not dismissed A faction-wide row of its own

A new bucket in the panel above the hexes, for warnings that belong to the faction rather than to a place.

  • Honest about what the warning is: not about a hex.
  • Somewhere for the next faction-wide check to go — the quartermaster one (ah-oq3) is the same shape.
  • A second bead. Findings would have to carry an optional hex, and the panel, the region filter and the header count all have to learn about it. Too much to bolt onto this one.

The words Wording 1 was chosen; and the two report formats

The corpus contains two different status blocks, and the check has to read both. Older reports print Tax Regions: 14 (15) and Trade Regions: 15 (15) separately. Newer ones print a single Regions: 10 (10) pooling taxing and trading together — so there the count must include TAX orders as well, and it checks out on the fixture that has one.

WORDING 1 — chosen Count, limit, consequence

Problems
separate counters
13797⚠PRODUCE orders in 16 regions; this faction may trade in 15, so 1 region's production will be refused
one pooled counter
11852⚠PRODUCE and TAX orders in 11 regions; this faction may tax and trade in 10, so 1 region's orders will be refused
a faction that may not trade at all
7308⚠this faction may not trade in any region, so every PRODUCE order will be refused
  • Gives the player the two numbers and the size of the excess, which is what they need to choose what to drop.
  • The third form is real: two committed reports show Regions: 0 (0) for a pure-magic faction.
  • Three wordings.

WORDING 2 — rejected Name the surplus only

Problems
13797⚠one region too many is producing this month; drop a PRODUCE order somewhere
11852⚠one region too many is taxing or producing this month; drop an order somewhere
  • Shorter, and says what to do about it.
  • One shape covers a faction that may not trade at all (“sixteen regions too many”).
  • Hides the two numbers, so the player cannot check the reasoning against the report's own status block.
