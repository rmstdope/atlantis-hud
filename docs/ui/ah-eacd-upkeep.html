ah-eacd — a unit with food but no CONSUME flag is reported as starving

The rules' maintenance payment order names food twice. The CONSUME flag only decides whether food is spent before silver; it does not decide whether food is spent at all.

1–2. Food, if the unit is set CONSUME  ·  3–4. Silver  ·  5–6. Food — for every unit, flag or no flag  ·  7–9. unclaimed and allied

The application models steps 1–2 and stops. So a default unit sitting on grain, with no silver, is warned that it cannot pay its upkeep and shown a negative month-end figure — when the engine will quietly eat the grain and the unit will live.

The part that is plainly wrong is the warning, and both options below silence it. What is left to choose is what the Upkeep figure says in that case.

Option A food is coverage, wherever in the order it is eaten

The Upkeep figure means what this unit still owes once everything the game will actually spend on it has been spent. Food eaten at step 5 counts exactly as food eaten at step 1 does — one rule, one number, and a unit that will be fed reads as fed.
