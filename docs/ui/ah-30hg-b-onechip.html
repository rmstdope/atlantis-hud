Option B — the three amber chips become one

The header can carry three advisory chips at once — what the engine reported (1 error · 333 events), what order validation found (10 problems), and what the parser could not read (6 unreadable) — and each new one costs another 130–200 px. Option B replaces all three with a single chip and gives its panel three sections. The count is things wrong: errors + order problems + unreadable lines. Turn events are not warnings and stop being counted; they keep their section in the panel.
