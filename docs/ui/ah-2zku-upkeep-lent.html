Option A — upkeep a faction-mate pays is a line on both units

The draw for the GIVE is always shown whole. Silver handed over at month end to pay upkeep gets its own pair of lines: “was lent for upkeep” on the unit that was fed, “lent for upkeep” on whoever paid. The list always adds up to the month end. Recommended: every movement of silver is a line, on both sides, the way a ledger does it.
