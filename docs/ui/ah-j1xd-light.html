The light theme's own failures — proposals (bead ah-j1xd)

Split out of ah-v09e, which you scoped to the dark theme. The light theme is worse: five accent tokens fail AA even on an opaque panel, and brass — the worst of them at 3.61:1 — carries every panel title, every section heading, the wordmark and every order warning in the application.

This bead is blocked by ah-v09e, because its numbers depend on the pane transparency default that bead changes from 90 to 20.

§3  The squeeze, which is the one thing I need you to decide

To clear AA over dark terrain, ink-dim has to darken to roughly #43495a — which is darker than ink-soft is today. Keep the surfaces as they are and the three-level text hierarchy collapses: secondary and dimmed text end up the same colour, or dimmed ends up darker than secondary, which is worse than either.
