The month-long warning — on a unit with no men

Your words: "It'd be nice not to get the 'no month-long order' warning for units that have no men left in them." The warning is "has no order that spends the month", shipped in v0.10.0. A unit with no men cannot spend a month on anything, so there is no order you could add that would satisfy it.

This amends a decision rather than fixing an oversight. That bead's acceptance said "There are no exemptions: an empty block warns and a unit on GUARD warns." So this is the first exemption, asked for with a case in hand — and the old line is not binding.

One thing found in the code that neither of us had. A unit's men are counted from its man-type items, and the model carries a second flag beside the count: men_estimated. When the ruleset does not recognise every item a unit holds, the count is a guess. An exemption on "men == 0" alone would silently hide a real unit whose only crime was carrying an item the ruleset has not been taught. Whichever option you pick, the exemption will only fire when the count is known.

1 · What "no men left" means

A unit at zero men may still hold silver, equipment or a mount — and may still be something you care about rather than a husk.

ANo men, whatever else it holds

The exemption keys on the men count alone. A unit with 7500 silver and no men is exempt, because it still cannot spend a month.
* Pack Train (1902), Borg TNG (95),
7500 silver [SILV], 2 horses [HORS].
Weight: 100. Skills: none.
→ exempt
  • Matches the reason exactly: no men, no month to spend
  • One number to test, and it is already on the model
  • A unit holding 7500 silver goes quiet, and that may be worth a look

BEmpty of everything

Exempt only a unit holding nothing at all — no men, no silver, no items. A true husk.
* Remnant (1903), Borg TNG (95),
Weight: 0. Skills: none.
→ exempt

* Pack Train (1902) … 7500 silver
→ still warns
  • Nothing with anything in it ever goes quiet
  • Does not answer the ask: a unit with silver and no men still cannot spend a month
  • A husk is the case that matters least — it is the one you would not miss

CNo men, and nothing else to say

Exempt a unit with no men unless it holds something a player would act on — silver above a threshold, or equipment. Between the two.
* Remnant (1903) — nothing
→ exempt
* Pack Train (1902) — 7500 silver
→ warns
* Husk (1904) — 2 silver
→ exempt
  • Quiet where it should be, loud where there is something to collect
  • A threshold is a number nobody can defend, and it will be wrong for someone
  • Two rules to explain instead of one

2 · Does the unit vanish from the list, or change what it says?

You flagged this yourself in the filing: a unit that has lost all its men may itself be news, and suppressing the only line that mentions it could hide something worth knowing.

iSilently skipped

The check does not fire. The unit appears nowhere in the warnings, and the count drops by one.
Problemsthis turn 9 problems
  • unit 14451 has no order that spends the month
  • unit 1902 has no order that spends the month
  • unit 13432 has no order that spends the month
  • Exactly what was asked for, and the list gets shorter
  • Nothing new to design, and the toggle still turns the whole check off
  • A unit that lost its men this turn is now mentioned nowhere

iiSays something true instead

The unit keeps a line, but one that is not advice: it observes rather than warns, and does not count towards the problems chip.
Problemsthis turn 9 problems
  • unit 14451 has no order that spends the month
  • unit 1902 has no men left
  • unit 13432 has no order that spends the month
  • The unit is still visible, and the line says the thing that is actually true of it
  • Losing every man in a unit is news, and this is where you would see it
  • A new kind of finding — an observation, not a problem — which nothing else has
  • Every unit that has ever been emptied says this, for ever, not just the turn it happened
