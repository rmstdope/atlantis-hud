Same exclusion as A — no apprentice row, no apprentice in a count, none offered as teacher or pupil — but the line under the title ends with the number left out, so a player who wonders where fifteen units went is answered without leaving the pane. The number is your own apprentices only; an ally's are not counted, because his sheet is not a census of his faction.

Decisions (recorded from the planning discussion, 2026-09-07): variant B (count them) over variant A (silent); the same rule applies to an ally's mage sheet, and an ally left with no mage loses his group heading entirely; a unit holding manipulation and a foundation stays, so the filter is written on "only manipulation" and never on "has manipulation"; the count is your own apprentices only, because an ally's sheet is not a census of his faction.
