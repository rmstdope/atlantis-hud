ah-omn7 — what the HUD says when a BUY is cut down to what the unit can pay

rules/buy: a unit that cannot afford the quantity it asked for buys as many as it can afford. The HUD currently charges the whole line and then warns that the unit is short. Capping the quantity is the fix — but it removes the only way a purchase can push a unit into the red, and sixteen existing tests use exactly that to prove the SILVER column and the not-enough-silver warning agree with each other.

So the question is not whether to cap. It is what the player is told when the cap bites. Three shapes, each shown against the same three orders.

The three shapes

Scene 1 is the plain case: unit 5 holds $25, grain sells at $12, orders BUY 5 grain. Scene 2 is money that arrives too late: unit 5 holds nothing, orders WORK then BUY 5 horse at $12 — the $120 of wages is paid after the market closes. Scene 3 is money that may not arrive at all: unit 1 holds $100 and taxes a $500 base that a second taxer also wants, then orders BUY 4 sword at $100.

AA new warning of its own

The purchase is cut, and a new finding says so and names the line. not-enough-silver keeps its present meaning exactly — the unit's silver actually goes below zero — which after this change only upkeep and other uncapped spending can do.
