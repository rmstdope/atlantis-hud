Walking the problems one at a time — proposals (bead ah-dlao)

Working through a turn means finding each problem in a list and clicking to the unit that has it. The bead asks for next problem / previous problem, by mouse and by keyboard, so a player can remedy them one at a time without leaving the keyboard.

Half of it already exists. walkProblems (AppShell.tsx:737) already steps the list on F8 and Shift-F8, follows the unit into another hex, and lands the selection once the unit has loaded. What is missing is a way to do it with the mouse — and one behaviour the bead asks for that would change what F8 does today.
