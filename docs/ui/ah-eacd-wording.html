ah-eacd — naming the food, round 2

Settled: food eaten at step 5 or 6 counts as coverage, so the Upkeep cell drops (Option A); the step-5/6 hover gets its own sentence, distinct from the step-1 one; and it names the loss in items, not silver.

step 1  ·  chosen  ·  “This unit's own food covers 60 of its upkeep.”  — unchanged
step 5  ·  forced  ·  “This unit has no silver for its upkeep, so 2 grain of its own will be eaten.”

Two cases the item wording does not answer by itself. Both are common, not corner cases.

Case 1 a mixed larder

Hodfarers owes 120 and holds 1 grain and 3 livestock. Three items are eaten. The rules do not say which, and the engine's choice is not visible from a report — so the application has to pick a story and tell it consistently.

Three ways to say it
OptionThe sentenceWhat it assumes
1 — name a mix This unit has no silver for its upkeep, so 1 grain and 2 livestock of its own will be eaten. That the game eats in the order the report lists items. Precise, and precisely wrong whenever the engine disagrees.
2 — count, don't name This unit has no silver for its upkeep, so 3 of its food items will be eaten. Nothing. Exact about the amount, silent about the kind — which is the part nobody can know.
3 — name only when there is one kind One kind: “…so 2 grain of its own will be eaten.”
Mixed: “…so 3 of its food items will be eaten.”		 Nothing. Says the most that is true in each case; two sentences to keep in step.

Case 2 faction food, held by other units

At step 6 the food comes from somebody else's inventory, pooled across the hex. The hover is built from one unit's data and the shared UnitSilver figures — it has no view of a neighbour's items today, so naming them means the core carrying the tags out.

Two ways to say it
OptionThe sentenceWhat it costs
Items, like step 5 This unit has no silver for its upkeep, so 1 grain of faction food in this hex will be eaten. The core must carry which tags the pool gives up, per fed unit, through a pool that is shared and settled all-or-nothing. Real work, and a number that can be wrong when several units are fed at once.
Count only This unit has no silver for its upkeep, so 1 faction food item in this hex will be eaten. Nothing beyond the count the pool already knows. Says less about a loss that is not this unit's to begin with.

Note the step-2 sentence for a CONSUME FACTION unit stays as it is — “Faction food in this hex covers 40 of this unit's upkeep.” — in silver. Only the new forced-feeding sentences are in items.
