ah-rgkk.4.3 — round 1: the Silver popup

Resting on the Silver cell today opens the same six-row working the whole-unit tooltip shows — Held now, In, in time, In, too late, Out, Upkeep, At month end — plus every note that applies, of which there are thirty-odd in SILVER_NOTES. It says how much moved. It never says what moved it, and the red figure and the ⚠ mark on the cell are explained by colour alone.

ah-rgkk.4.4 (planned, in flight) makes the causes knowable: UnitSilver.changes, one entry per movement of this unit’s silver, each with an amount, a cause and the order’s line, in the turn’s own order. This bead spends it.

Three questions. Q1 — which figures the popup draws. Q2 — how the red figure and the ⚠ mark are said in words, and where. Q3 — what becomes of the notes the new lines now restate. Everything else in the family is settled and is not re-opened: the heading, the twelve-line cap, the pair notation with green up and red down on the after only, the sr-only sentence as the only non-visual route, and the single amber warning line.

The scene

Collectors (1732) is one of ours, eight men, in a region it can tax. Its report showed 340 silver. This month it taxes the region (line 4, +200), Watch (1604) gives it 50, it studies Combat (line 6, −60), buys 2 horses at 45 (line 7, −90) and gives 40 to Scouts (1502) (line 8). Upkeep for eight men is 80, and the Silver column is counting it (ah-1wcw.4). It ends the month holding 320 — twenty less than it started with, after a month in which 200 came in from tax alone. That is the case this popup exists for: the figure moved by −20 and six separate things moved it.

Q1 — which figures does the popup draw?

All three draw the same words underneath (Q2’s W1 and Q3’s N2, so the panels differ in one thing only). Compare where your eye goes to answer “why is this unit poorer than last month?”

  • Nothing a reader already knows how to read is disturbed — the same six rows, in the same order, as the whole-unit tooltip one hover away.
  • The in-time / too-late split, which is the whole of ah-uwa3, keeps a row of its own.
  • Bounded: six rows however busy the month, so the twelve-line cap can never bite.
  • No cause carries a figure of its own on a line. taxed 200 is prose, and the eye that scans the right-hand column never meets it.
  • Reads nothing like the Items and Men popups one column along, which draw a line per thing and a sentence per thing.

Q2 — how are the red figure and the ⚠ mark said in words?

The cell colours a figure red when this unit, counted alone, is in trouble: it ends the month below zero, or it cannot pay for its own orders out of silver that reaches it in time (ah-uwa3). It draws ⚠ for something else — a check that names this unit, not-enough-silver (which pools across the hex’s sharing units) or upkeep-exceeds-unclaimed (ah-fjty). The two mean different things on purpose, so a ⚠ on a black, positive figure is not a contradiction. Today neither is explained anywhere except by its colour. All three panels use Q1’s V2.

Q3 — what becomes of the notes the new lines now restate?

Today the popup pours in every note that applies, in SILVER_NOTES order — thirty-odd of them, and about half a dozen say nothing but an amount the ledger is about to draw as a line of its own. Both panels use Q1’s V2 and Q2’s W1; the unit is Collectors (1732) from the scene, with a take added — it also takes 25 from a unit its report does not show.

  • Nothing agreed in an earlier bead is disturbed, and no wording anybody signed off disappears.
  • The whole-unit tooltip and this popup keep saying identical things, so nobody has to hold two versions in mind.
  • The same gift is on the screen twice, four lines apart, in two different notations.
  • The prose block grows exactly where the lines already grew, so a busy unit gets the longest popup in the table saying everything twice.

  • Only this popup changes. The whole-unit tooltip keeps every note it has today, word for word.
  • Two surfaces now say the same month in two different ways, and a reader moving between them has to notice that neither is missing anything.
  • Wording agreed in earlier beads (ah-awcm, ah-tdsi) stops shipping on this surface.
