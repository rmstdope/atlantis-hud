ah-dxfd.2 — which moment in the turn each number is priced from

Before the questions, one correction to the mockup you approved for the parent bead. That page said the answer was to read the ledger's post-order balance and reorder ledger_for so item transfers settle before the silver is priced. I looked the turn sequence up (rules/sequenceofevents) and that turns out to be both harder than it needs to be and wrong in one direction.

The game runs GIVE and TAKE first, and PILLAGE and TAX immediately after — before the market opens. So a sword bought this month is not in the unit's hand when it pillages, and the ledger's end-of-month balance counts it as though it were.

What this buys. The three highlighted rows are the whole of the bead's headline case — the pillage warning, the tax income and the CAST income — and every one of them is priced from the picture immediately after GIVE and TAKE. That picture is already half-built: the walk that follows gifts of men for the skill checks (apply_gifts_of_men, shipped by ah-z73s.2) keeps a running holdings map per unit and simply throws it away. Widening it from "gifts of men" to "gifts of anything" and publishing what it already knows fixes all three — and needs no change to ledger_for at all, so the cycle that page called out never has to be broken.

What is left over — and it is a real question

The bottom half of the turn — ENTERTAIN, WORK and maintenance — sees the post-market picture, which includes men bought from the market. Recruiting is the market in this game (rules/economy_recruiting: "The BUY order is used to recruit new people"), and ah-dxfd.1 — merging now — makes the MEN cell show them.

A — this bead follows gifts and takes only
  • One view of a unit, taken immediately after GIVE and TAKE. Every number in the bead's title is right.
  • Smaller: one walk widened, two call sites repointed. No change to ledger_for.
  • A unit that BUYs men keeps pricing its WORK, ENTERTAIN and upkeep on the old headcount, so its MEN cell and its SILVER cell disagree until a follow-up bead lands.
  • Same for food: grain bought this month will not be counted as paying upkeep. Grain given will be.
B — this bead also gives the late orders a post-market view
  • The row agrees with itself for a recruiting unit, which is the fault the whole family exists to remove.
  • Food bought this month pays maintenance, which is what the game does.
  • A unit now has two headcounts and two holdings — one for PILLAGE/TAX/CAST, one for WORK/ENTERTAIN/maintenance — and every arithmetic term in the column has to say which it takes. That is the single largest source of "why does this number differ from that one" in this module.
  • Meaningfully larger bead: the late view has to come off the ledger's balance, so forecast_hex and charge_upkeep both grow a second source of facts.
