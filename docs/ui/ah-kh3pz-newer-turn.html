When a newer turn arrives what we can honestly promise

We agreed the newer-turn question would say "Your turn 21 orders and notes are kept with turn 21", and that turn 21 would come back when picked from the turn list. Two things turned out differently. The turn list cannot take you back to turn 21: it only compares an older turn with the one on screen. Turn 21's orders are kept, and the assistant can still read them, but the player has no way to open them again. Hex notes are not tied to a turn: they belong to the game, so they come along to turn 22 rather than staying with turn 21.

Agreed 2026-11-25: Option A. Options B and C are shown only as the rejected alternatives.

The same-turn question ("Load it again … Your orders and notes stay as they are.") is unaffected and stays as agreed. Only the newer-turn sentence is open.
