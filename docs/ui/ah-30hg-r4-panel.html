ah-30hg.2 round 4 — what the one panel looks like inside

Round 2 settled the chip’s words (⚠ N to check, Turn report when nothing is wrong) and the order of the sections (Order problems · The engine said · Lines not read · Turn events, fixable first, an empty section not drawn). What it did not settle is how much of each section you see at once — and that matters, because three panels are being folded into one and each of them has a body of its own today: the problems panel groups by hex and each hex is a button that takes you there; the unreadable panel has a Copy all button and a fixed three-column grid; the messages panel groups 333 events by the unit that caused them.

All three below are at true size — 512 px wide, the width of today’s widest of the three — with the real turn-71 numbers: 10 order problems across 4 hexes, 1 engine error, 6 lines not read, 333 events. The body is boxed to the height it actually gets, so what you see is what fits before scrolling.
