ah-rgkk.3.3 — round 2: the sentences the popup already has

Answered in round 1: Q1 = B, everything that moved first and then the rest, hard cap at twelve. Q2 = S2, one joined sentence per changed item, at the foot.

What round 1 did not draw: the Items popup already carries eight families of sentence from itemsTooltip, agreed in ah-agbm and ah-64wm. Five of them now say a thing the new cause block also says — produced, cast-created, transported in, transported out, spent on a build — but each ends with a caveat about when the month resolves that the cause block does not carry: “Production resolves late, so this unit cannot give them away or sell them this month.” The other three (a take from a unit your report does not show, an unreadable order, the silver working) overlap with nothing and stay whatever you choose.

Answered: T2. Each of the five overlapping families collapses to one short caveat naming no item. The plan's wording differs from the panel below in one way that matters: it keeps “this unit cannot give away or sell” rather than “the wood cannot be given away or sold”, because since ah-728m.2.2 a manufactured output can be spent this month by a later manufacturer or a BUILD in the same hex.
