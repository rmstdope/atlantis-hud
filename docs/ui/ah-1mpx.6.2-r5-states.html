Round 5 — the states behind S2

Chosen in round 4: S2 — a grey notice while the scan runs and Export… disabled until it lands. W4-B — three sentence shapes, all / some / none. N1 — a turn imported now folds in now.

S2 raises three things the happy path hides, and W4-B leaves one plural to pin down. Nothing here reopens a round-4 choice.

V When the waiting line is shown at all

The scan is often over before the dialog can be opened, and on a game with one stored turn it is instant. A line that appears for 80 ms and vanishes is a flicker, not information. And a file with no foreign units in it does not depend on the scan at all.

V1 — only when it would change something   recommended
SituationWhat the dialog does
Scan running, foreign units in the fileWaiting line shown, Export… off.
Scan running, no foreign unitsNothing shown, Export… on. The scan cannot change this file.
Scan already finishedNothing shown, Export… on, the real foreign notice in place.
Game has no stored turnsScan finishes with nothing at once; nothing shown.
  • The player is only ever made to wait for something that would actually change their file.
  • An all-own-units export — the common case — is untouched by this bead entirely.
  • Two conditions rather than one, so the rule has to be stated rather than assumed.
V2 — whenever the scan is running, always
SituationWhat the dialog does
Scan running, foreign units in the fileWaiting line shown, Export… off.
Scan running, no foreign unitsWaiting line shown, Export… off — for a file the scan can have no effect on.
Scan already finishedNothing shown.
  • One condition. Trivially explainable: “we are still reading, so wait”.
  • Blocks an export of your own units for a reason that has nothing to do with them.

F A stored turn that will not load or will not parse

The scan walks every stored turn. One of them may fail — a truncated row, a storage read that rejects. The other turns are fine and their skills are perfectly good.

F1 — carry on with what was read, and say so in the dialog   recommended
14 units will be exported.
6 units belong to another faction. 4 of them go out with combat skills read from battle reports; the other 2 go out with none.
2 stored turns could not be read. Any battle in them was not counted.
CancelExport…
  • The export still happens, and still carries everything that was recovered.
  • Says that the answer is incomplete in the one place where being incomplete costs something.
  • Singular: “1 stored turn could not be read. Any battle in it was not counted.”
  • A fourth possible notice line.
F2 — carry on silently; log it and nothing more
14 units will be exported.
6 units belong to another faction. 4 of them go out with combat skills read from battle reports; the other 2 go out with none.
CancelExport…
  • Matches how the Armies store already treats a failed cache write: log, swallow, never roll the turn back.
  • No fourth line for a case most players will never hit.
  • The dialog states a count — “4 of them” — that is quietly a floor rather than the answer.
F3 — treat it as a failure of the whole scan
14 units will be exported.
This game's battle reports could not be read. No foreign unit's skills were recovered.
CancelExport…
  • Never states a number that is secretly partial.
  • Throws away 30 good turns because of one bad one, which is the opposite of what the player wants.

P W4-B's plurals, written out

The mixed sentence names three numbers, and any of them can be 1. These are the exact strings; there is nothing left for the implementer to decide.
