Finding a structure in the palette — proposals (bead ah-wkwk)

The palette should find an individual structure in the player's world — by its name or its id — not only a structure type in the scraped data.

ah-5jkt has just landed, and it put the types in. The palette already has a building kind holding Mine, Tower, Stockade and the rest, from the game-data dictionary. So typing mine will now match a dictionary page and every Mine on the map. That collision is the whole design.

§1  The collision

Two different things share a word. “Mine” the page tells you it costs 10 wood or stone and increases iron. “Mine” the building is a thing standing in a hex you can go to. A reader who types mine wants one of them and the palette cannot know which — so the list has to make the difference obvious at a glance.

§4  What choosing one does

OptionWhat happens
Go to its hexThe selected hex becomes the one the structure stands in, exactly as choosing a region does. Simple, and the structure is then visible in the region pane.
Go to its hex and select a unit inside itCloser to "take me to this building" — but which unit? The first is arbitrary, and an empty structure has none.
Go to its hex and open the region pane's structure listMost precise. Needs the region pane to be able to be told what to show, which it cannot be told today.
