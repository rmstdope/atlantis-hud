Everything known about a faction — proposals (bead ah-bu2c)

A faction's name appears in the attitudes list and in the units-in-hex table, and naming it is all it does. The bead asks for it to be clickable, opening a dialog of the attitude toward it, the hexes it has been seen in and its known units — where hovering a hex or a unit highlights that hex on the map.

Those two requirements are incompatible as written, and that is the first thing to settle.

§1  A dialog dims the map it is pointing at

Every dialog in this workspace is modal — fixed inset-0 … bg-black/50 with aria-modal="true" (BattlesDialog.tsx:49-54, ChangesDialog.tsx:53-58). It covers and dims the whole screen. Popovers are anchored under their chip and leave the map alone.

§2  What a highlight looks like, if there is one the map already draws a selection ring, and it must not be confused with one

D A brass ring

selected
hovered
  • Reads as "the map is pointing at this", and brass is the app's pointing colour.
  • Two rings on screen at once; a reader must learn which is which.

E A tint over the hex

selected
hovered
  • Unmistakably not a selection — a different kind of mark entirely.
  • A fill hides the hex's own terrain and badges, which is what you were trying to look at.

F Every hex at once, no hover

Opening the faction marks all its hexes; hovering does nothing.
  • Answers "where are they?" at a glance, which is the actual question.
  • Works with a modal dialog too, if the dialog can be dismissed and the marks persist.
  • Not what the bead asked for, and a faction seen in twenty hexes is a lot of rings.
