ah-tgtp — a proved verdict carries forward

Since ah-rx0r.2 the Products line marks a hidden resource as 0 floater hide when a skilled unit of yours stands there now and the report names none, and as mushroom? when nobody there could tell. The verdict is read from this turn’s units only — so the moment the hunter marches away the proof is thrown away.

“If turn n discovers that a certain hex has or has not a specific advanced resource due to a unit with the right skill being there, that knowledge should be brought over to future turns (> n) even if the unit with the right skill has moved on.”

Every hex below is a real hex from a committed turn of g5-f21. In turn 23 two hunting [HUNT] 3 drones (11851, 11854) stood in swamp (36,46) and the report named no floater hides. By turn 24 they had ridden to swamp (35,47), and by turn 39 no hunter of yours is in (36,46) at all.

This is what the navigator chose, over a sister page that named the proving turn on the line itself. Whether a hex holds floater hides is a durable fact about the hex, so once proved it is simply stated — no turn on the line, the turn only in the hover. Chosen with it: a remembered presence is shown too, and a verdict never expires.

question 2 does a remembered verdict ever go stale?

A resource is not something a hex grows back. The rules page states each terrain’s resources once and says nothing anywhere about a region gaining or losing one (rules/region_resources; no rules text matches “depleted” or “run out”). So the case for an expiry is caution, not a rule.
