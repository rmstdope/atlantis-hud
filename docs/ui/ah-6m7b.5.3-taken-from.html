ah-6m7b.5.3 — a unit that another of your units TAKEs from

Planning this bead measured the SILVER column against the ledger's own record of every silver movement, and found a figure a player reads that is simply wrong. One question follows from it, and it is about a line in a popup, so it is yours.

Two of your units share a hex. Guards (901) writes TAKE FROM 900 100 SILV. Scouts (900) holds 1000 silver and writes nothing at all.

The taker's side is already right. The source unit's side has never existed: the receipts pass that feeds the SILVER column records only silver arriving, so Scouts' column never hears that 100 left. Its month-end figure is 1000 when the truth is 900.

Today the money leaves and nobody says so

Silver — Guards (901), the taker
Guards (901)
month end  100 took  +100  ·  from Scouts (900)
Silver — Scouts (900), the source
Scouts (900)
month end  1000 — no rows at all —

1000 is wrong. The ledger charged Scouts the 100 and knows it ends on 900; the column does not. A player planning Scouts' next order is looking at money it no longer has.

Option A a row of its own, with a name of its own

The column gains a new cause meaning another unit took this from me. The popup line reads was taken from, and names who.

Silver — Scouts (900) — option A
Scouts (900)
month end  900 was taken from  −100  ·  by Guards (901)

Says exactly what happened, and reads differently from a gift the unit chose to make — which matters, because the source unit wrote no order. Cost: one new value in the core's cause list, one new label, and every surface that lists causes learns it.

Option B reuse the wording we already have

The column books it as the gift cause it already has. No new name anywhere.

Silver — Scouts (900) — option B
Scouts (900)
month end  900 gave away  −100  ·  to Guards (901)

The figure is right and nothing new is invented. Cost: the sentence is a small lie — Scouts gave nothing, and a player reading gave away beside an order it never wrote may go looking for one. It is also the same wording a real GIVE gets, so the two become indistinguishable in the popup.

Option C leave it, and file it

The column stays as it is. This bead's agreement check simply excludes the source leg of a TAKE, and the wrong figure becomes its own bug bead for you to rank later.

Silver — Scouts (900) — option C
Scouts (900)
month end  1000 — unchanged —

Keeps this bead to the check it was filed for. Cost: a known-wrong figure ships on, and the check that would have caught it has a hole cut in it by hand — which is the shape of exclusion this bead exists to be sceptical about.

Not a question the other defect the measurement found

A unit that SELLs and then BUYs in the same month is priced by the column as if the sale had not happened, so the purchase vanishes from its list and its month-end figure is too high. rules/sequenceofevents settles "SELL orders are processed" before "BUY orders are processed", so the sale does fund the purchase and the column is wrong.

month end  300 sold  +300  ·  10 grain at 30 each
Silver — after this bead
Traders (5)
month end  20 sold  +300  ·  10 grain at 30 each bought  −280  ·  4 horses at 70 each
