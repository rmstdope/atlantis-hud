ah-t2pn — your own units share a region's pools

A region's tax base, wage pool, entertainment demand and market quantities are shared, and the rules say what happens when they are oversubscribed. The forecast gives every unit the whole pool: forecast_hex builds one RegionWages and hands the same copy to each unit, and the only cross-unit accounting in the whole function is food.

So a player with three taxing units in a city is shown three times the money. Foreign units cannot be predicted and it is right not to try — but your own units are all in the orders document in front of us.

Tax income is “split evenly among all taxers”. Market oversupply and oversubscription “split up in proportion to the number each unit tried to sell” / “in proportion to the amount each buyer attempted to buy”. For wages and entertainment the rules say only that the pool runs out.

Chosen: model the contention. The column shows what the game will pay. This reverses ah-1wcw.1's per-unit rule for the four regional pools, knowingly. CLAIM against the faction purse is untouched — ah-bumi settled that column and ah-wur4 shipped its warning.

One rule for all four pools

“Split evenly among all taxers” is read per man — which is how the rate is stated in the first place (“each taxing character collects $50”). And per man is arithmetically identical to in proportion to what each unit asked for, because a taxer's ask is exactly men × 50. So the proportional split the rules state outright for markets is the same function, and it serves all four pools.

share[i] = wants[i]                      when the pool covers everybody
share[i] = pool * wants[i] / total       when it does not, rounded DOWN
Rounded down on purpose: the shares then never add up to more than the pool, and a few silver goes unpromised. Understating by a handful is the safe direction for a figure whose negatives a player acts on; the alternative is promising money the region does not have, which is the defect being removed.

And rejected outright: reusing estimated-men, which needs no new variant and no regenerated types but tells the player something untrue about the unit they are looking at.

And a warning as well

With the column corrected, a unit earns less than its men could collect alone and nothing on screen says why. One code, region-pool-oversubscribed, one finding per contending unit, on its own order line:

Scouts (101), on its TAX lineyour units here tax for $3,000 between them and this region has $2,500
Garrison (102), on its TAX lineyour units here tax for $3,000 between them and this region has $2,500
Mirrors the shipped claims-exceed-unclaimed — “your units claim {total} between them and the faction has {purse}” — so the two read as a pair, one faction-scope and one hex-scope. One finding per contending unit rather than one for the hex, for ah-wur4's stated reason: “a single finding must anchor on some unit, and picking one by document order blames a unit no more at fault than the others.”

Rejected: a hover note only, which was the planner's recommendation once the column was correct; and nothing at all, which leaves a taxer earning under $50 a man unexplained.
