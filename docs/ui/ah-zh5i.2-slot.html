ah-zh5i.2 — round 2: how “Unit” and “Movement” share one slot

You chose option C: the Movement panel stands where the Unit panel does, so the column is always two panels — 95 px and 144 px — exactly as it is with the planner off. Everything below is drawn at true width (336 px) and true height (the 95 px slot).

One thing the measurement turned up that the first page did not say: even with no route at all, just having the Movement panel in the column crushes the Unit panel to 21 px — a title bar and nothing else. So the two really do have to share the one slot, and the question is who decides which one you are looking at.
