ah-tjaz — three states wearing the same labels

When Psylocke fails a bead she asks you one question: is the plan wrong, or is the build wrong? That answer decides who picks it up, and it is recorded by removing planned, or not.

build wrong → planned stays → implementers
plan wrong → planned removed → a planner revises the design

That much works. The gap is downstream: an implementer that hands a bead back also removes planned — skills/implement-bead:323 and skills/beads-workflow:96 both say to. So a bead you judged build wrong can arrive at a planner wearing the labels of plan wrong, and the answer you gave is overwritten by a recipe that never knew about it.

What happened ah-t2pn.3, twice

Psylocke   failed it, you said BUILD wrong   -> planned kept, verification:failed
Storm      picked it up, nothing to build   -> planned REMOVED, human added
Xavier     P0 check returns it as PLAN wrong

  correct action: leave it for Psylocke
  the label rule's action: rewrite the plan, and the interview behind it
Storm was right — an implementer that keeps re-claiming a bead with nothing to build is a loop, and removing planned stops it. The recipe it followed is right too, for the case it was written for. Nothing here is anyone's mistake; the labels simply cannot carry three states.

Why the planner's rule is the fragile half

agents/planner.md:133 reads the state from an absence:

A reopened bead that reaches you with its planned label removed (the plan itself was judged wrong, not just the build) is a P0 with an existing design and a recorded failure to read — amend it in place.

An absence cannot say why it is absent. Every future reason to drop planned will land in the planner's lap wearing this meaning.

  
A — say it positivelyPsylocke adds a label when you answer plan wrong — plan:revise — and the planner looks for that, not for an absence. Removing planned then means only what it says: not ready for an implementer. Every other reason to remove it becomes harmless.
B — label the third state insteadThe hand-back adds verification:ready when there is nothing to build. The planner skips beads carrying it. Fixes today's case; the next new reason to drop planned is a fresh collision.
C — no new label, change the ruleTell planners that a removed planned never means plan-wrong on its own, and the notes must be read. Costs nothing to build, and puts a judgement call in the path of every reopened P0 for ever.

The second half the hand-back routes to the wrong role

The same recipe adds human, which parks the bead in your queue. On ah-t2pn.3 what it needed was Psylocke — verification:failed is what puts it on her list. It was fixed by hand; nothing stops the next one.

1 — no human when the bead is already failedAn implementer handing back a verification:failed bead leaves it for Psylocke. You are not asked about something no one needs you to decide.
2 — keep human alwaysEvery hand-back reaches you, which is the safe default and how it works today — at the cost of a queue carrying items that route themselves.
