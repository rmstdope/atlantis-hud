ah-q7jd — a SELL ALL its own month has already emptied

A unit holds 10 furs. Its orders this month are GIVE 5512 10 FUR then SELL ALL FUR, in a hex whose market wants furs at $42 each. The rules process GIVE before SELL (rules/sequenceofevents), so by the time the market opens the unit holds no furs and the sale earns nothing.

Chosen, over two rounds with the navigator. The SILVER column, the ITEMS column and the market-share settlement all settle a SELL … ALL against what the unit holds when the market phase runs — the report’s stock moved by this month’s GIVE and TAKE and by nothing else, which is what rules/sequenceofevents describes. A sale those transfers empty entirely then carries a new nothing-left-to-sell warning on its own line, switchable off under Markets in the Warnings tab as Sale with nothing left to sell.

Rejected: correcting the number and saying nothing (the sale would become an invisible no-op, while the same month written SELL 10 FUR already warns); two shorter wordings; warning when a gift only partly reduces a sale; filing the toggle under the catch-all Orders group; a neutral sentence covering both GIVE and TAKE; staying silent when a TAKE was the cause; and repeating the sentence in the SILVER column’s hover. The full interview is in bead ah-q7jd’s design field.
This is a warning, never blocking, and it stays silent whenever it cannot be sure — a market that does not want the goods, a report that showed none to begin with, or a transfer this month’s projection could not follow. That is the house rule for every semantic check.

Taken, not given — T1 chosen

Another unit writes TAKE FROM 4411 10 FUR. Our unit’s stock is emptied in the very same phase, but it did not give anything — so “this unit gives away” is a false statement about whose order it was.

T1 — chosen. A second sentence for TAKE

SELL ALL FUR

Two sentences to maintain; each is exactly true.

T2 — one neutral sentence for both causes
