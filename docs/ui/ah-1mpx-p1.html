Proposal 1 — an Army is something a unit belongs to

Membership is edited where you already look at the unit. Nothing new to learn, and no mode.

The Unit panel on the right rail — which already tells you a unit's men, items, skills and flags — gains one more entry: Armies. It lists the Armies this unit is in, each removable, with an + Add to army control beneath. That is the whole of adding and removing.

A sixth floating panel, Armies, joins the right rail beneath Orders. It holds the roster, the totals and the export — the place you go to look at a whole force, not the place you go to edit one unit.

Everything below is drawn to the real chrome: theme.css's palette, the monospace face, the 28px panel title bars, the 19rem left and 21rem right rails, the 330px units dock, panels at 80% opacity over a full-bleed map.

  • Two additions, both small: one entry in a panel you already read, one more floating panel that folds to a 28px bar.
  • No mode and no second kind of selection — the app behaves identically whatever you did a moment ago.
  • Removing is exactly where adding is, so undoing a mistake needs no hunting.
  • One unit at a time. A twelve-unit force is twelve select-and-add cycles.
  • The right rail is 21rem and already holds Unit and Orders. A third panel there is real competition for space.
  • Scanning the units table tells you nothing about membership — only the one open unit shows it.
