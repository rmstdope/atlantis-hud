ah-lauy — the chosen design: the same goods bought twice in one block

Everything Option A does, and one thing more: a finding on the line itself, so a dead BUY is underlined in the orders editor and listed in the problems panel — the way nothing-left-to-sell already treats the selling side. That is the only difference between the two options, and it is the whole question.

Chosen, over three rounds with the navigator. A unit's BUY lines for one item are priced against a running per-tag total, so a second line can only buy what the lines above it left — of the market line and of this unit's settled share of it alike. The settlement counts an unbounded claim once per unit rather than once per line, so two BUY ALL lines no longer win a share against a faction-mate that they are not owed, and no longer push a line into a false region-pool-oversubscribed. A line that finds nothing left carries a new nothing-left-to-buy warning — on any BUY, exact or unbounded — and the BUY ALL hover names the unit's own earlier order rather than blaming the market or a faction-mate.

Rejected: fixing the numbers and changing no wording, which leaves a second line saying “your other units in this region have claimed all 5 this market has” where there are no other units; a hover sentence with no warning behind it (round 1, option A), which would leave BUY 5 grain written twice silently corrected and never mentioned, since exact lines have no hover at all; letting silver keep narrating a line that its own earlier order had already emptied; and naming the market line rather than the unit's share in the sentence, which claims a size the unit never had to buy.
Why it might be worth a warning. The hover is only seen by a player who goes looking for it, on the one unit, in the one column. A dead BUY line is a line the player meant to do something and it does nothing — and unlike the hover, a finding reaches them without their asking, underlines the exact line, and covers the BUY 5 grain written twice, which has no hover at all.

Why it might not. It is a 35th warning code and a 35th switch, for a shape that only arises from an editing slip; the corrected numbers may be all the player needs.
