Atlantis writes the lead vessel’s type, or the word Fleet when there isn’t one. Both mean “vessels sitting in this hex”. Only one of the seven Fleet lines draws correctly, and only because Airships happens to contain the letters ship — the substring test in isShip(). Three of these are even named Ship or Fleet by their owner.

§2  What should a fleet draw?

A A fleet is a ship

Fleet, 8 Corsairsship
  • Consistent with what already happens. A Galley, 40 Galleons, 11 Galleys, 10 Balloons — 61 vessels — already draws as one ship. A fleet is the same thing under a different first word.
  • Probably one word added to SHIP_KINDS; fixes all six at once.
  • The Ships badge already exists and already means “vessels here”.
  • A lone raft and a fleet of forty look identical on the map.

B A fleet gets its own mark

Fleet, 8 Corsairsfleet
  • A stack of vessels is militarily a different thing from one boat, and the map would say so.
  • A new mark to design, in four themes, plus a badge — far bigger than the bug.
  • The distinction is not the one the data draws: a Galley, 40 Galleons… is also a stack, and would still be a plain ship.
§3  A related thing I am NOT proposing to fix here. The Ships badge counts structures, not vessels — so Galley, 40 Galleons, 11 Galleys, 10 Balloons counts as 1, not 61. That is already true today and has nothing to do with fleet being missing from the set; it would apply to galleys and cloudships equally. If you want the count to mean vessels, that is a separate bead and I will file it. Say so and I will.
