Factions in the palette

Chosen. A faction is found in the command palette by its name or number. Its row shows your attitude towards it (or your faction), plus last seen turn N for a faction met only in an earlier turn. Choosing it opens that faction's details under your faction chip, with ‹ back to your own faction. Factions from earlier turns show where they were last seen. With nothing typed, factions come right after the towns and cities. The chosen option on each question is outlined.

Already settled, drawn for the record

No report loaded: no faction rows. No match: the existing nothing matches. A unit whose owner is concealed adds no faction. No attitude declared and no default in the report: not declared, dim italic, as the faction details already say. Esc or a click outside closes the palette and returns focus where it came from; choosing a faction moves focus into its details, and closing those returns focus to your faction chip. Nothing is saved.
