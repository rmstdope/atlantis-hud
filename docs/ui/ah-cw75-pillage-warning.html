Giving PILLAGE to Taxers (10116) in forest (36,0) of Reprau produces:

cannot pillage here: needs 24 combat ready men, this faction has 0

The unit visibly holds 19 high elves, so being told 0 reads as a counting bug. It is not. The unit carries the avoiding flag — an avoiding unit has no combat ready men whatever it holds — and, independently, it holds no weapons at all. Both are true of this one unit. The threshold of 24 is right too.

Two things are wrong with the sentence: it never says why, and the count is over the region, not the faction — so “this faction has” could send a player looking at units in another hex.
