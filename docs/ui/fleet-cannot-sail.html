A fleet that cannot sail — what the player sees (bead ah-j0e)

The report already states a fleet's numbers for your own ships — + Ship [329] : Longship; Load: 110/150; Sailors: 4/4; MaxSpeed: 4. — and the ruleset carries every hull's cargo capacity and crew requirement (Longship 150 / 4, Cog 750 / 6, Galleon 2700 / 15…). So the two facts the check needs exist; the question is how much of this month's orders the warning takes into account, and then what it says.

Question 1 — what the warning counts pick one

SCOPE A The fleet as the report shows it

Reads Load: H/N and Sailors: H/N off the ship line (ruleset arithmetic when the report does not state them). Warns on a SAIL when H>N (load) or H<N (sailors) as things stand. This month's boarding and giving are ignored.

  • Exact: the server's own numbers.
  • Smallest to build.
  • Silent on the common mistake — you loaded it this month.

SCOPE B …plus who boards and leaves this month

Aboard = the report's units in the ship, plus units ordered ENTER / MOVE IN it this month, minus LEAVE / MOVE OUT. Load = the sum of those units' report weights; sailors = their sailing levels. Capacity and crew requirement as in A.

  • Catches “I moved a heavy unit aboard and SAILed”, and “the sailors left”.
  • A unit that is given cargo this month still counts at its old weight — silent there.

SCOPE C …plus what is given aboard this month

B, and every GIVE/TAKE in the hex re-weighs the units it touches, using the ruleset's item weights (a Cog-full of grain is priced). If a moved item has no known weight, the fleet's load is not judged (accept on doubt); its sailors still are.

  • Catches the loading-by-GIVE mistake, which is the usual one.
  • Most code; the weight ledger has to be built alongside the silver one.

Recommendation: B in this bead, C as a follow-up bead filed today — B is a real check that ships fast and C is a strict extension of it (same warning, more accurate load). If you prefer, C can be this bead.

Question 2 — one warning or two and the words

OPTION 1 Two codes, one line each

Overloaded and undercrewed are separate mistakes with separate fixes, so each is its own toggle and its own line — a fleet with both problems gets two lines.

The states same whichever is chosen

Who gets the line

The unit whose SAIL order it is (the captain — the first own unit aboard giving SAIL); underlined on that SAIL line. Two units aboard both ordering SAIL: one line, on the first.

Exactly at the limit

150 aboard on 150 sails; 4 levels on 4 sails. Silent. “Within a man of not sailing” is a different, later check.

Unknown hull, or a foreign ship

A ship the report does not state numbers for and the ruleset does not know: nothing is said (accept on doubt). Foreign ships are never yours to sail.

Units carrying cargo of their own

A unit's own Capacity does not lighten the ship — everything aboard weighs what it weighs. Silent as long as the ship's capacity covers the total.

SAIL with no route

A bare SAIL (the template's form) counts as a SAIL order for this purpose: the fleet is being told to move.

No report loaded

Nothing is said, like every check in the panel.
