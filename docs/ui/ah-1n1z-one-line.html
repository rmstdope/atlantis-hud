Option A — the star and the note share one line

On a stale hex with a battle, the star moves left and the revision note is written beside it, the pair centred under the coordinates. Everywhere else nothing moves.

Recommended. Standard map labelling: a label that collides is placed beside its symbol, and here the note dates the battle too (“this battle, two turns old”). Cost: on the smallest close-zoom hexes the pair nearly fills the hex’s width, and the star is no longer centred on these hexes.

Hexes drawn at 1.7× life size so the lettering can be judged.
