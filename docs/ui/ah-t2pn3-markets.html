ah-t2pn.3 — markets, and one thing the family interview did not cover

You settled that own units in a hex split a contended pool in proportion to what each asked for. For markets the rules say so outright: oversupply and oversubscription “split up in proportion to the number each unit tried to sell” / “in proportion to the amount each buyer attempted to buy”.

Applying that is straightforward. But reading the code turned up a separate defect sitting in the same arm, and the fix for one is the fix for the other — so it needs your call.

The surprise a lone buyer is charged for goods that are not there

The BUY arm charges count × price and never looks at how much the market has unless the order is BUY ALL. So:

Market has 100 horses at $60.   One unit orders BUY 200 HORS.

  today    charged 200 x 60 = $12,000   for 100 horses that exist and 100 that do not
  capped   charged 100 x 60 =  $6,000

This has nothing to do with contention — there is only one unit. But the settlement fixes it for free: splitting a pool of 100 between asks of 200 gives 100, whether the asks come from one unit or five. split_pool does not treat a lone claimant as a special case.

 What happens
A — apply the settlement whenever anyone buys or sells The lone over-buyer is capped too, and one rule covers both. Costs: a figure changes for a single-unit case the family interview never discussed, and markets then behave unlike tax and wages, where you settled that contention needs two units.
B — settle only when two or more units want the pool Consistent with the other three pools and strictly in scope. Costs: two units asking 200 between them are capped at 100, while one unit asking 200 alone is charged for 200 — the same market, two different answers.
C — B now, and file the lone over-buyer separately Keeps this bead to exactly what was interviewed, and leaves the inconsistency on the screen until the new bead is ranked and built.

Either way

A SELL is already capped at what the unit holds (asked.min(market_takes).min(unit_holds)), so the selling side has no equivalent surprise — only BUY does. Goods this market does not trade still earn nothing and are still explained by the shipped not-traded-here finding; goods nothing could identify are still doubted. Neither changes here.
