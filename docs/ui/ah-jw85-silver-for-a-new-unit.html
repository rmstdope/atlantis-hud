The Silver column for a unit that does not exist yet — as agreed in planning

Bead ah-jw85. A unit created this month by FORM 1 already has a row in the units table — the one marked new — and that row's Items cell already shows the silver its parent is ordered to hand it. Only the Silver cell is blank.

Option A: the new unit is priced exactly like any other unit, always. Its figure is what it will hold when the month ends: nothing to start with, plus what it is given, less what its own orders spend. A new unit nobody has given anything shows a dim 0, the same dim 0 any other penniless unit shows.

This was chosen over the alternative of leaving the cell blank until something actually moves silver: on screen that blank is indistinguishable from the defect being fixed, and the hover then has no Silver section in which to explain itself.
