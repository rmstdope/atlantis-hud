ah-lbd9.2 — Option B: sign in from Settings

The same job as A, put somewhere else. Here the header gains nothing at all: the faction number and password live in a New Age world section on the per-game Settings tab, beside the ruleset and the map — the tab that already holds everything that is true of this game. The wording, the states and the two smaller choices at the bottom are identical in both options; only the place differs, so read section 1 against Option A’s and pick a place.

Already decided on the parent bead and not being reopened: the faction number and password are never stored — no keychain, no settings file, no “remember me”; they are typed once per run of the app. And New Age is desktop only (the world refuses the live web origin), so nothing on this page exists in the browser build.

Rejected (2026-09-04) in favour of A, the header control: signing in is how a session begins rather than something you change, nothing on screen would say whether you are signed in, and a session that runs out mid-action would send you through Settings to fix it. Kept as the record of what was weighed.

The header is untouched in this option — which is also the objection to it: nothing on screen says whether you are signed in without opening Settings.

  • No new header control, and the header is already crowded on a narrow window.
  • It sits beside the other facts about this game — the world, the ruleset, the map.
  • Signing in is a thing you do at the start of a session, not a setting; Settings is where you go to change something, not to begin.
  • Nothing on screen shows whether you are signed in. Two clicks to find out.
  • When a session runs out mid-action, there is no control nearby to sign in again with.

3 · Choice 2 — what happens when the session runs out

A New Age token expires and the world offers no way to renew one, so the answer is always “sign in again”. The question is where that is asked — and it decides what ah-lbd9.3 (fetch the report) and ah-lbd9.4 (send orders) do when a call comes back unauthorised. The two candidates are drawn on Option A’s page, section 4; they are the same whichever place you pick here:

  • E1 — ask again where you are. The dialog you are already in becomes the sign-in prompt and then does what you asked. Nothing typed is lost.
  • E1 costs: the prompt has to be embeddable in two other dialogs, so ah-lbd9.3 and .4 each carry a little more.
  • E2 — say so, and put you back where signing in happens. The action stops with a sentence; you sign in again and ask again. One idea, less to build.
  • E2 costs: two dialogs and a re-open to get back to where you were — and in Option B that means going through Settings.

4 · Choice 3 — more than one game open

The HUD holds several games at once, and two of them can be different factions on different worlds.

S1One session at a time

Signing in belongs to the game you were in. Switch game and you are signed out there and then.

Simplest; one token in memory. The cost: two factions on Arcanum means retyping a password every time you switch between them.

S2One session per game

Each game keeps its own signed-in faction for as long as the app is open. Switch back and forth and both stay signed in.

A little more to hold, still nothing on disk. Several tokens in memory at once, all dropped when the app closes.
