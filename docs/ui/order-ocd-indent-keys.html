Order OCD — keys, undo and paste

Settled: indent both as you type and on the whole block; closers align with their openers; trailing blanks collapse when the tidy runs; unclosed blocks indent by running depth; comments indent and blank lines stay empty; an empty unit stays empty. What is left is what the keyboard does, and what happens to text that arrives all at once.

1 · Backspace on a line the editor indented for you

ABackspace clears the whole indent

One press from inside the auto-indent takes the caret to column 0. A second press joins the line to the one above, as usual.

2 · Undo, twice over

Two different edits the player did not fully type: the indent that came with Enter, and the whole-block tidy that runs when a unit is opened.

iEnter is one step; the tidy is not undoable

One Ctrl+Z after Enter takes back the newline and its indent together. The whole-block re-indent stays out of the history entirely — exactly what the case tidy already does, because the player did not do it.

Ctrl+Z →
 FORM 1
both gone in one press
  • Matches the precedent already set for keyword shouting (ah-bn6.2)
  • Undo never rewinds past something you typed to reach something you did not
  • A re-indent you disagree with cannot be taken back — only edited

iiBoth undoable, one step each

The whole-block tidy is a history step of its own: Ctrl+Z on opening a unit puts the indentation back as it was.
unit opened, block re-indented
Ctrl+Z → back to as typed
Ctrl+Z → ...your last real edit
  • Everything on screen can be taken back
  • The first Ctrl+Z after opening a unit does something you did not ask for
  • The tidy would then re-run and undo the undo

3 · Pasting a block of orders

Orders copied from another unit, from Discord, or from last month's file — arriving all at once, with whatever indentation they had.

P1Tidied on paste

The paste lands and is immediately uppercased, re-indented and trimmed, in the same step — one Ctrl+Z removes the whole thing.
clipboard:
    form 1
study combat
    end
↓ pasted as
 FORM 1
  STUDY COMBAT
 END
  • The setting is true of everything on screen at all times
  • No "click away and back" needed to see it take effect
  • A third moment the tidy runs, with its own way of being wrong

P2Tidied when the block next is

Paste is a plain paste. The text is tidied the next time the tidy runs — on reopening the unit, or on the setting being toggled.
↓ pasted as
    form 1
study combat
    end
(tidied next time the unit
 is opened)
  • Nothing new to build — paste is already covered by the on-open tidy
  • What you pasted is what you can check before it is rewritten
  • The most obvious moment to want tidying is the one that does not
