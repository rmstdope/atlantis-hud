Question 1 — which way does a GIVE of men count?

Men can leave a unit (given away, or GIVE 0, discarded) and men can arrive (given to it, or taken). Leaving is arithmetic we can do exactly: the unit's sailing level is held by each of its men, so fewer men is fewer levels. Arriving is not: when men are given into a unit the game merges them and recomputes the unit's skill, which this application does not model.

R1 men out exact, men in silences

Men leaving reduce the levels. Men arriving into a unit aboard put that fleet's crew check to silence for the month, rather than guessing what the merged unit knows.

orderscrew check
give 0 1 GNOLwarns — 1 of 2
give 9509 1 GNOLwarns — 1 of 2
give 9508 2 GNOL
(into a unit aboard)		silent — cannot say
take 9509 2 GNOLsilent — cannot say
never a warning we cannot stand behind
  • catches the case verification found
  • same "silent on doubt" rule the rest of the module uses
  • a reinforced fleet is still unchecked

R2 both directions counted

Men arriving count too, at the receiving unit's own sailing level. Symmetric and simple to state.

  • invents skill the merged unit will not have — the wrong direction of error for a warning
