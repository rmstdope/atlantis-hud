ah-rx0r — Inline in the Products line

Nine resources in this game are hidden until somebody with the skill looks. rules/region_resources lists what each terrain may hold; data/skills says, for nine of them, that “a unit with this skill is able to determine if a region contains…” — MINI 3 mithril, MINI 5 admantium, LUMB 3 ironwood, LUMB 5 yew, QUAR 3 rootstone, HUNT 3 floater hides, FISH 3 giant turtles, HERB 3 mushrooms, HORS 5 winged horses.

So an empty row means two opposite things today. Every hex below is a real hex from a committed turn, not an invention. Inline in the Products line is what this page proposes; the other file proposes the alternative.

“after scouting a region for advances ressources, would be nice to show that there are none. So if a unit with hunt3 entered a swamp, it would be great to show ‘0 floa’ if there were no floater hides”

The four cases what the hex pane shows

The bead’s case, exactly. Two HUNT 3 units looked; the report named no floater hides; the swamp has none. Mushrooms need HERB 3 and the best here is HERB 1.

Products

14 grain · 12 wood · 8 floater hides · 14 herbs · mushroom?

The neighbouring swamp, one hex away, does have them. Nothing new is added to a resource the report already names.

Products

17 grain · 17 wood · 15 herbs · floater hide? · mushroom?

Nobody looked. Today this hex and (36,46) read identically; under this change they must not.

mountain (38,4) in Femont

turn 41 · The Disinherited Knights (42) · MINI 5 and LUMB 5 stand here
Products

38 livestock · 25 iron · 10 stone · 0 mithril · 0 admantium · rootstone?

MINI 5 covers mithril and admantium; rootstone needs QUAR 3 and nobody here has it.

Question 2 does the terrain filter the list?

Mountain (38,4) again. The LUMB 5 woodsman standing there can tell you whether the hex holds ironwood and yew — and a mountain never holds either (rules/region_resources). Filtering by terrain costs one extra scrape of that table; leaving it out costs two dead rows here, and more on a hex where several skilled units are parked.
