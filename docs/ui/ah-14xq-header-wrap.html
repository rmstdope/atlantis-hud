Option A — the buttons drop to a second row when they don't fit

When the panel is too narrow for the tabs and both buttons on one line, the buttons move to a slim row of their own under the tabs, right-aligned. The words stay exactly as they are. As soon as there is room again, they go back up.

Cost: at narrow widths the panel's content starts about 22px lower.
