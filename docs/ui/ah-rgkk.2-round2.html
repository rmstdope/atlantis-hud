U — When the projection cannot honestly be made

Four real cases. The projection needs the after-market headcount, the teachers settled, the fee paid, and a ceiling (rules/skills_limitations: a unit of several races may only study as far as the least of them allows). The question is one rule for all four: show a figure with a caveat, or withhold the figure and say why?

U1 — withhold the figure; say what is missing
the fee is unaffordable
Braves (1487) — skills
combat COMB2 (90) → 1 (53)
Studying combat costs 200 silver and this unit is 160 short, so the study may not happen at all.
the headcount is a guess
Rabble (3300) — skills
combat COMB1 (30)
This unit’s headcount is a guess, so neither recruiting nor studying can be worked out for it.
already at the race ceiling
Militia (2088) — skills

Studying combat: worth one month. Hill dwarves may not take combat past level 2, so the points rise and the level holds.
  • No number on the screen is one the game might not produce.
  • The commonest of the four — a short unit whose neighbour will almost certainly cover it — loses its projection over a shortfall that may never bite.
