Q5 — what the strip says when no attitude has been declared

A faction with no declaration of its own falls back to your default attitude, and if there is none either, attitudeToward returns null and the strip prints not declared today. With the lead-in in front of it that becomes “you have declared not declared”, which does not read.

Option 5a — drop the lead-in in this one case recommended
Grey Company (17)✕ not declared

  • No new words at all — both strings already ship and are already verified.
  • not declared stands alone perfectly well; it is a statement, not an attitude.
  • The strip has two shapes: sometimes a lead-in, sometimes not.
Option 5b — one lead-in, reworded so both cases fit
Grey Company (17)✕ your attitudenot declared
−4010Woodsmen(8,30)
Thane's Ring (10)✕ your attitudehostile
−3021Ring Guard(11,55)
  • One shape, always. The lead-in never has to be conditional.
  • Two words rather than three, so less of the strip is spent on it.
  • Changes the lead-in you chose in round 1 (you have declared).
  • Slightly weaker: “your attitude” does not say you declared it.
Option 5c — change the null word instead
Grey Company (17)✕ you have declarednothing
−4010Woodsmen(8,30)
Thane's Ring (10)✕ you have declaredhostile
−3021Ring Guard(11,55)
  • Keeps the round-1 lead-in exactly, and reads as a whole sentence.
  • Retires not declared, a string that ships and is pinned by a test.
  • “nothing” where every other faction shows a word like ally is easy to skim past.
