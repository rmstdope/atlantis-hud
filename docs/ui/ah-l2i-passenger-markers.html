Settled — and one thing the mockup above got wrong

You chose variant 2, destination plus aboard the ship. Also settled: a passenger's own MOVE wins over the ship's SAIL; passengers follow an untraceable ship with → …; and the fleet arrives with its hull, so in reads the ship in the destination hex.

The correction: every variant above wrote Wavecrest, and this table does not name ships that way. The in column renders [<structure id>] and nothing else — literally [329]. The ship's name is available (Structure.name, often the bare word Ship when the player never renamed it) and so is its kind (Longship). So the words in the marker are still open, and they are below, exactly as they would ship.
