Question 4 — the items the catalogue cannot classify

The transport rule's fifth class is “items created using artifact lore”, and unlike men, mounts, ships and war machines the catalogue cannot identify them. The game's own data page never prints the flag; the skill entry for artifact lore lists nothing it produces. This is the same blind spot ah-3sp7.1 measured and recorded when it taught the catalogue the eleven readable classes.

The blind spot is small and it is bounded. 21 of the game's 171 items sit in no readable class. Seven of those are plainly ordinary trade resources — mithril, ironwood, floater hide, rootstone, yew, mushroom, admantium — leaving fourteen that might or might not be refused:

RING · AMTS · CARP · PORT · MWAG · GLID · HPOT · AEGS · WCHM · GTCR · STAH · SORB · CORN · IENT

Transporting one of these is a rare order. The question is what the column does when somebody writes it.

unit 5530
  transport 6857 30 STON
  transport 6857 1 RING    ; a ring of invisibility — nobody here knows if this is allowed

D1 — move it, and say nothing   recommended

Treated like any other item. If the game refuses it, the column was wrong about one rare order for one turn.

UnitNameMenSkillsItemsLong order
*5530Miners6mining 3120 SILV, 10 STON—
hoverwas: 120 SILV, 40 STON, 1 RING
  • The column is already optimistic in exactly this way: a TAKE of a stated quantity from a unit the report cannot see is granted, on the grounds that the order says how much.
  • Nothing new to build, nothing new to read, and no mark on 157 items to cover 14.
  • Silently wrong when it is wrong, with nothing on the row to hint at it.

D2 — move it, and let the hover admit the doubt

The item moves, and one sentence says the app cannot tell whether the game will allow it.

UnitNameMenSkillsItemsLong order
*5530Miners6mining 3120 SILV, 10 STON—
hoverwas: 120 SILV, 40 STON, 1 RING Counted 1 RING as transported. The game refuses to transport items made by artifact lore, and this app cannot tell which those are.
  • Honest, and costs nothing on the 157 items that are not in doubt.
  • The row still shows the likelier answer rather than freezing.
  • A long, technical sentence about the app's own limits, in a hover meant to be read at a glance.

D3 — do not move it, and mark the cell + ?

The ring stays; the mark says the month could not be fully counted.

UnitNameMenSkillsItemsLong order
*5530Miners6mining 3120 SILV, 10 STON, 1 RING + ?—
hoverwas: 120 SILV, 40 STON, 1 RING and more that cannot be counted: transport 6857 1 RING
  • Claims nothing it cannot support, and reuses a mark the column already has.
  • Most of these fourteen probably can be transported — a magic carpet and a healing potion are not obviously artifact-lore work — so the common answer is to show doubt about an order that will simply happen.
Whichever way this goes, the plan records it so that a later bead teaching the catalogue the ADVANCED and MAGIC classes knows what it is allowed to change.
