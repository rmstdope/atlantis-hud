Part 8 — BUY 5 PEASANTS

rules/buy: "you may use PEASANT or PEASANTS to recruit whichever race is present in the region." The HUD does not resolve that word today — PEASANTS names no item in the catalogue, so the order is already marked uncounted and its silver is left unprojected, for every unit, mage or not. It is a known gap with a bead of its own.

unit 900 ; 1 leader [LEAD], force [FORC] 2 — a mage BUY 5 HUMANS ; resolves → warned BUY 5 PEASANTS ; resolves to nothing today → ?
P1 — leave PEASANTS alone (recommended)
  • The mage check fires wherever the order names a race the app can resolve, and stays quiet on PEASANTS along with everything else about that line — no silver, no headcount, no warning. Nothing about this bead changes.
  • Consistent: the line is already marked as one the app could not read, so the player is not being told it is fine.
  • The most natural way to recruit is the one spelling that will not warn, and a player who types BUY 5 PEASANTS into a mage sees only the general "could not read this line" marker.
P2 — resolve PEASANTS in this bead, at least far enough to warn
  • Both spellings warn, so the check does what a player would expect of it.
  • Resolving that word properly means deciding which race the region offers and feeding it into the market share, the silver and the headcount — a bead's worth of work, and one that is already filed. Doing half of it here means the line warns about the mage while its silver is still unprojected.
