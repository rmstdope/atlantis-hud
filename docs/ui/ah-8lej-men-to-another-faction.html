Settings

Gifts the game will refuseA GIVE or TAKE naming items that cannot change hands, or men given to another faction.

Turning this off hides the finding only; projected holdings do not change. There is no dialog, focus move, cancellation, persistence, loading, or error state.

Rejected variants

Generic existing words

Rejected because “8 orcs cannot be given away” hides that giving the same men to one of our own units is legal.

Silent refusal

Rejected because an all-men order would leave an unchanged row with no explanation, while a mixed order would partly apply without saying why.

Two findings or one umbrella

Rejected in favor of one finding per order with a clause for each reason. Two findings repeat the consequence; an umbrella loses which item met which rule.
