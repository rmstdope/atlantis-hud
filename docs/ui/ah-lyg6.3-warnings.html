ah-lyg6.3 — the four warnings, and whether a shelter changes the numbers

The bead asks for four warnings: a student not in the same hex as his teacher, a teacher with room for more students, an oversubscribed teacher, and study outside a building that houses mages. Two questions here — where they are drawn, and whether the last one changes the projected levels or merely says something.

Looked up, not recalled. rules/magic_skills: "study into a magic skill above level 2 requires that the mage be located in some sort of building which can offer specific facilities for mages … If the mage is not in such a structure, his study rate is cut in half." Halved, not lost — the bead's own wording says "wasted", and it is not. The seat count is the building's own: data/objects for a Hermits hut says "This structure will allow one mage to study above level 2"; the ruleset we hold carries Fort 1, Castle 2, Citadel 3, Magical Citadel 50, Tower 0. rules/skills_teaching: "Each person can only teach up to 10 students in a month; additional students dilute the training."

Fiction: Ereb (2431) and Sable (2517) share a Fort — one mage seat — in Ryehold. Ereb is force 3 and studying; Sable reaches force 3 on turn 28 and needs the seat from then on. Kestrel (2688) is in Dunmoor with no building at all.

Does a missing shelter change the projected levels?

This is the question that changes what the grid means, not where a sentence sits. Above level 2 an unsheltered mage studies at half rate, so his levels land later — and the planner's whole job is saying when a level lands.
