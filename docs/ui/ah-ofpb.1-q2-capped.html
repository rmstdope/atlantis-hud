Q2 — a production the unit's own holdings cut short

The app already works out that a unit makes fewer than its men could, and the SILVER column's hover already says so: “This unit has silver for 1 catapult, not the 2 its men could make.” The figure is capped by whichever bites first — the silver the recipe wants, or the materials.

Two things make this awkward. First, the cap is measured against what the report shows the unit holding, not against a running total — deliberately, so that the ITEMS and SILVER columns cannot disagree. A unit ordered to buy 10 IRON and then produce really will make more than the figure says. Second, a unit capped all the way down to none moves nothing at all, so its row looks exactly like a unit given no orders.

unit 5531 ; Weaponsmiths, 8 men, holds 5 IRON, 350 SILV produce sword ; men could make 8; the iron allows 5 unit 5532 ; Armourers, 8 men, holds 0 IRON, 350 SILV produce sword ; men could make 8; the iron allows none unit 5533 ; Smiths, 8 men, holds 5 IRON, 350 SILV buy 10 IRON ; the market has 10 produce sword ; will really make 8; the report-time cap says 5

C1 — show the settled figure, and leave the explaining to SILVERrecommended

The column shows what the unit will really make. Nothing extra is said in the ITEMS hover; the row's own SILVER hover already names the cap, one row-width away.

unit 5532 — nothing moved, so the row is upright and its ITEMS hover says nothing
  • One rule, and the two columns can never contradict each other.
  • Nothing new to word, nothing new to maintain.
  • Unit 5532 — ordered to produce and producing nothing — is indistinguishable from a unit given no orders at all.
  • Unit 5533 will really end the month with 8 swords and 7 iron, not 5 and 10. The column is confidently a little wrong, and says nothing about it.

  • The one row that shows nothing at least has something to say when hovered.
  • Unit 5532's row is still upright and unmarked, so nobody will ever hover it — there is nothing on screen inviting them to.
  • Says the same thing twice on one row, which is the duplication ah-bumi was filed to stop.

C3 — show the settled figure, and mark the cell whenever a cap bit

The figure is a floor rather than a certainty, so the cell carries the + ? it already uses for a month it could not fully count.

IDNAMEITEMS
*5531Weaponsmiths350 SILV, 5 SWOR + ?
*5532Armourers350 SILV + ?
*5533Smiths10 IRON, 350 SILV, 5 SWOR + ?
hover on unit 5531's ITEMS cellwas: 5 IRON, 350 SILV and more that cannot be counted: produce sword
  • Unit 5532 is finally distinguishable from a unit with no orders.
  • Honest about unit 5533, whose real figure the column cannot reach.
  • A cap is the ordinary case — almost every producing unit is short of something — so most producing rows would carry a question mark, and the mark stops meaning anything.
  • The hover's wording is wrong here: the order was counted; it was counted to a smaller number.
Recipe: WEAP makes 1 sword per man-month from 1 iron, so 8 men want 8 iron. The cap and the figure are the ones the SILVER column already computes and shows — this question is only about what the ITEMS column does with them. The ITEMS column does not track silver: a BUY records no SILV movement (ah-agbm), so the SILV entry stays at the figure the report printed and the SILVER column owns the money.
