Round 1 — a range in the ITEMS column

You have already settled the two big things and I am not re-asking them: family A (the level-scaled certainties — ESWO EARM ESHD CRPA CRSS CRMA TRNS) shows the exact figure capped by what the unit holds, and family B (the chance spells) shows the arrival as a range, 2-3 RUNE. This page is the four things that decision left open, plus one finding of mine that changes the shape of the answer.

The finding first, because it moves two of the questions. The families are not fixed lists of spells. I computed every creating spell at every level from config/public/ruleset.json: a chance spell’s range collapses to a single number whenever the total is a whole multiple of 100. A level 5 ring maker is 20×5 = 100% — exactly one ring, no range. A level 5 staff maker is 40×5 = 200% — exactly two. So the rule has to be arithmetic (floor and ceil of the same total), never a spell list, and the same spell looks different at different levels.

The other half of that finding: SWIN (create cloudship) is the only chance spell that consumes materials — 75 FLOA + 75 IRWD — and it is a range only at levels 3 and 4. That is Q4.

Q1 — a range meeting a stock the unit already holds

A level 3 runesmith already holding 2 RUNE and 40 HERB, ordered cast Create_Runesword. It makes 2 certain and has a 70% chance of a third. The column’s settled meaning is what the unit ends the month holding, so the two numbers have to meet somewhere.

A — merge, and the whole holding becomes the range. One entry per item, exactly as every other order already merges. You cannot see how much was already there.

  • The cell always answers the question the column asks: what will this unit have?
  • A player who wants to know what the cast contributes has to do the subtraction, and cannot when the stock is itself a range.
B — keep the arrival as its own entry. The stock stays exact and the cast’s contribution is legible on its own.

  • The + sign collides with the + ? mark that already means “something here could not be counted”.

  • Long. The ITEMS cell already clips with an ellipsis at this width, and this is the widest form of the three.
  • A rule that fires only for ranges, so two chance mages and one certainty mage in the same hex read three different ways.

Q4 — the one chance spell that eats materials

SWIN (create cloudship) costs 75 FLOA + 75 IRWD per cloudship and is a chance spell — 20% per level, from level 3. So a level 3 wind mage has a 60% chance of one cloudship. It is the only spell in the game where a range is charged against items rather than silver, and it makes the arrival and the consumption disagree about how sure they are.

What is already fixed: ah-ofpb.4 charges the ledger the ceiling — 75 and 75, for one cloudship — because you chose “the most it can cost”. The not-enough-items warning fires off that figure. The question is only what the ITEMS column prints beside it. The mage below holds 200 of each.

A — the consumption at the ceiling, the arrival as a range. The column matches the ledger exactly: 75 leave for certain, 0-1 arrives.
IDNAMEITEMS
*8908Wind mage L3125 FLOA, 125 IRWD, 0-1 CLOU
  • The ITEMS column and the SILVER column and the warnings all read off one number.
  • Nothing has to compute a second, floored cost.
  • The cell asserts 150 items are certainly spent on a cloudship that is 40% likely never to exist. In the game, a failed cast still spends them — but the cell does not say so.
B — both ends as ranges. The consumption is as uncertain as the arrival, and the cell says so on both sides.
IDNAMEITEMS
*8908Wind mage L3125-200 FLOA, 125-200 IRWD, 0-1 CLOU
  • Symmetrical: the same uncertainty on what goes and what arrives.
  • It contradicts the ledger, which charged 75 — so the SILVER column, the warnings and this cell would be computed from two different numbers, which is the defect this whole epic was filed about.
  • Needs a floored cost that CastPlan deliberately does not carry (ah-ofpb.4, Q2).

Whichever you pick, it applies to exactly one spell at exactly two levels. Every other chance spell costs only silver, which this column has never tracked.
