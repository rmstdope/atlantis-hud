Selling “all except some”, which the game doesn't allow

A unit holds 30 furs. The hex's market wants 40 furs at $24. The player writes SELL ALL FUR EXCEPT 10, meaning to sell 20 and keep 10.

The rules give SELL two forms only: SELL [quantity] [item] and SELL ALL [item] (rules/sell), and BUY the same two (rules/buy). GIVE is the order with an EXCEPT form. The game stops reading a SELL order once ALL FUR is complete and ignores the rest, so this order sells all 30 furs, for $720. Today the app shows $720 and says nothing, so the player never learns their 10 furs are not being kept.

Already settled by the team: this order is an error. The app does not honour the reserve.

Chosen, over two rounds: 1A, where SILVER counts what the game does (all 30 furs, +720) and the error stands beside it. W1, which underlines only the ignored words and says what happens. BUY gets the same error. V1 endings, which say what the unit will do (section 4).
Rejected: 1B, counting nothing and marking the order unread, because it is wrong for what the game does and spreads doubt to other sellers. W2, "write how many to sell, e.g. SELL 20 FUR", with the whole order underlined. Keeping the check to SELL only. V2, "reads this as SELL ALL FUR".
