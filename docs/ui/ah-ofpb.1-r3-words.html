Round 3 — the words, and the states the happy path hides

You chose P1 (the goods arrive, and the hover says when) and C2 (the settled figure, with the cap named in the ITEMS hover too). This page settles what those two sentences actually say, and walks the three states neither mockup drew.

The ITEMS hover is built from lines joined by newlines, always opening with was:. Today it can carry three kinds of line; this bead adds two more, so the order matters.

Q4 — the sentence that says the goods arrive late

Unit 5531, 8 weaponsmiths holding 20 IRON and 350 SILV, ordered produce sword. Cell reads 12 IRON, 350 SILV, 8 SWOR.

W1 — two sentences: what is included, then why it matters.

hoverwas: 20 IRON, 350 SILV Includes 8 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month.

W2 — one sentence, shorter.

hoverwas: 20 IRON, 350 SILV Includes 8 SWOR produced this month, which arrive too late to spend.

W3 — names the mechanism rather than the consequence.

hoverwas: 20 IRON, 350 SILV Includes 8 SWOR this unit will produce; production resolves after every spending order.
  • All three sit on the same line of the same hover; only the words differ. The existing line above them, Includes 5 GRAI taken from unit 999, which your report does not show here., is the shape they are following.

Q5 — the sentence that names the cap

Unit 5531 again, but holding only 5 IRON: its men could make 8 swords and its iron allows 5. The SILVER column's own hover on this row already says This unit has materials for 5 swords, not the 8 its men could make.

K1 — the ITEMS hover says exactly the same sentence, so the two columns cannot drift.

hover, materials capwas: 5 IRON, 350 SILV Includes 5 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month. This unit has materials for 5 swords, not the 8 its men could make.
hover, silver cap (a carpenter short of the 3000 silver a catapult wants)was: 250 WOOD, 2000 SILV This unit has silver for 0 catapults, not the 2 its men could make.
  • One sentence, one meaning, wherever you hover.
  • On a silver cap it is word-for-word what the cell next door says.

K2 — only when the cap is materials. A silver cap is the SILVER column's business and the ITEMS hover stays quiet about it.

hover, materials capwas: 5 IRON, 350 SILV Includes 5 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month. This unit has materials for 5 swords, not the 8 its men could make.
hover, silver capwas: 250 WOOD, 2000 SILV
  • Each column speaks about its own subject and nothing is ever said twice on one row.
  • A player hovering ITEMS on a silver-capped carpenter is told nothing about why no catapult appeared.

K3 — an items-flavoured sentence naming the material that ran out.

hover, materials capwas: 5 IRON, 350 SILV Includes 5 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month. Its men could make 8 swords; its 5 IRON allows 5.
  • Says which material bit, which the SILVER sentence never does.
  • A recipe with four inputs (a catapult wants wood, ironwood, fur and silver) has no single material to name, so the sentence needs a second form and a rule for choosing between them.

All five hover lines, in the order they would appear

A unit that manages every kind of note at once. Lines 1–3 ship today; lines 4 and 5 are this bead's.

was: 20 IRON, 350 SILV, 5 GRAI Includes 5 GRAI taken from unit 999, which your report does not show here. Includes 8 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month. This unit has materials for 5 swords, not the 8 its men could make. and more that cannot be counted: produce meal
  • Known before unknown, which is the order round 3 of ah-agbm settled. The two new lines go between the existing “includes” line and the “cannot be counted” line.
Recipes are the committed NewOrigins ruleset: WEAP makes 1 sword per man-month from 1 iron; CARP makes 1 catapult per 4 man-months from 250 wood, 30 ironwood, 80 fur and 3000 silver; COOK makes meals from any one of grain, livestock or fish, which is why the app refuses to price it.
