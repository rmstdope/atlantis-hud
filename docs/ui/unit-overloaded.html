A unit too heavy to move — what the player sees (bead ah-yfo)

“A unit can walk provided that the carrying capacity of its people, horses and wagons is at least as great as the weight of all its other items… Otherwise the unit cannot issue a MOVE order.” (neworigins-rules.html:1101). The engine refuses the whole order and reports it a week later: MOVE: Unit is overloaded and cannot move.

Decided, 2026-08-17, over two rounds. (1) The check weighs the unit as it will be when it moves — this month's gifts, takes, purchases and sales priced in, exactly as ah-eet does for fleets and for the same reason. (2) The message names the numbers, both of which are in the report. (3) The sentence ends “so it will not move” rather than “so the MOVE will be refused”, because ADVANCE reads as the same order and would otherwise be told it was a MOVE — and because the fleet warning already ends “so it will not sail”. (4) The toggle is Overloaded units in the existing Orders group, on by default.

Almost everything this needs already exists. The report prints both numbers on every own unit — Weight: 600. Capacity: 0/0/75/0. — and movement::mode::mobility() already turns them into Moves / Overloaded / Unstated. The route planner already refuses to plan a route for an overloaded unit and says so. The gap is the hand-written MOVE: type one yourself and nothing says a word.

The two real cases neworigins-3.0.0-g3-f42-t40.rep

:60 Builders (12054): MOVE: Unit is overloaded and cannot move. * Builders (12054), ... 10 humans [HUMN], 34 stone [STON]. Weight: 1800. Capacity: 0/0/150/0. 1800 carried, 150 is the most it can move with. :61 Builders (11619): MOVE: Unit is overloaded and cannot move. * Builders (11619), ... 5 hill dwarves [HDWA], 11 stone [STON]. Weight: 600. Capacity: 0/0/75/0. 600 carried, 75 is the most it can move with.

Both are caught by the report's own two numbers; no ruleset arithmetic is needed. And here is what the player did next. In the following turn 11619 gave the stone away and moved in the same month, and both worked — t41:131 Gives 11 stone [STON] to Taxers (11992), t41:264 Walks from mountain (38,4) to mountain (38,6). It works because GIVE is phase 4 and movement is phase 9. Dropping the ballast and walking is the standard fix for this exact mistake — and it is the case that decides question 1.

  • Nags precisely the player who is fixing the overload, which is the one shown above.

OPTION C — chosen Weigh it as it will be when it moves

Price this month's gifts, takes, purchases and sales into the weight — the same thing ah-eet is doing right now for fleets, for the same reason and with the same code.

nothing — 600 less 550 of stone is 50, under 75

  • Right in both directions — quiet when the player unloads, loud when a gift is what overloads them.
  • One rule across the client: the fleet check and this one answer “what does it weigh when it moves” the same way.
  • Waits for ah-eet to land, and needs its weight function reused rather than copied.
  • A unit that gives away its horses loses ride capacity as well as weight. Capacity is not repriced — that direction errs towards saying nothing, never towards a false warning.

Problems

4501⚠this unit is overloaded: it carries 300 and can only carry 70 riding, so it will not move
Orders
Overloaded units
A unit ordered to move carrying more than it can move with.
  • Tells the player which of the three allowances the 150 actually is — the report prints all four as 0/0/150/0 and never says which is which.
  • Three wordings to keep in step (on foot, riding, flying), and the mode named is the best one available, which may not be the one the player had in mind.
