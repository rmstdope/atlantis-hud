Option A — The second line says nothing

The case that was wrong

Tundra (35,9). The market sells 16 silk at $85. City Guard writes BUY ALL SILK twice; Scout writes it once. The Guard gets 12, the Scout 4. Deleting the Guard’s second line would drop it to 8 — so that line does buy.

AThe second line says nothing

The hover keeps one sentence for the unit’s share; the extra line adds no sentence of its own — matching the order editor’s silence.

UnitSilverItems

  • Nothing new to word or keep right.
  • Doesn’t tell the player that the second line is what pushed the share up to 12.
