  • Recommended. The spreadsheet rule: a number is shown whole or not at all — a spreadsheet shows ##### rather than part of a number.
  • No figure can ever be misread: any digit you see is the real month-end figure.
  • A four- or five-digit figure becomes … sooner (1440 is … from 51 px), so a narrow column shows less.
