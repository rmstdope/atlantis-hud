ah-vtwn — A unit's PRODUCE is counted at its skill level and its tools

The preview computed what a PRODUCE order makes as men ÷ man-months. The rules (rules/tableiteminfo) say the man-months a unit brings are men × level: "five men at skill level one are exactly equivalent to one man at skill level 5 in terms of base output", with a worked example that six level-1 men make two of a three-man-month item and six level-3 men make six. The same page gives eight items a production bonus. Neither entered the arithmetic, so every producing unit's ITEMS cell, both hovers, the SILVER cost and the two shortfall warnings were too small by a factor of the unit's level — 3 to 5 for a mature faction.

Decisions (recorded from the planning discussion, navigator, 2026-08-29): the rate is men × level + min(men, tools) × bonus — one tool to one man, spare tools idle; a unit below the recipe's minimum level makes none, and the hover says nothing about it because the Problems panel's produce-without-skill already carries that sentence; the cap sentence reads "…not the 46 its skill and tools could make."; tools are done in this bead rather than a later one. Rejected, and why, below each choice.

Decision 1 — how tools combine with men — CHOSEN: one tool, one man

The rules page states the bonus and stops; it never says how many men one tool serves, so this is the navigator's reading of the game and not a lookup. Worked on MinersA (5105) — 8 orcs, mining 5, PRODUCE iron, iron being 1 man-month.

Decision 2 — the cap sentence — CHOSEN: "its skill and tools"

It appears in both the SILVER hover and the ITEMS hover wherever the unit could have made more than its silver or materials allow. Shown for 8 men, weaponsmith 5, 6 hammers, holding 5 iron, ordered PRODUCE sword — 8×5 + 6 = 46 man-months, capped at 5 by the iron.

CHOSEN — "its skill and tools"

ITEMS hover

was: 5 IRON, 350 SILV Includes 5 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month. This unit has materials for 5 swords, not the 46 its skill and tools could make.

SILVER hover — 10 men, carpenter 5, silver for one catapult

This unit has silver for 1 catapult, not the 12 its skill and tools could make.

Accurate in every case. Says "and tools" to units carrying none, which was weighed and accepted.

Rejected — "its skill"

This unit has materials for 5 swords, not the 46 its skill could make.

Chosen in the first round, when the figure was men × level alone; withdrawn once tools came into this bead, because the hammers are inside that 46 and "skill" does not cover them.

Rejected — "its men" (the shipped wording)

This unit has materials for 5 swords, not the 46 its men could make.

Covers both without naming either and moves no test string, but says nothing about where 46 came from — which is the number a player would otherwise recompute by hand.

Decision 3 — the unit that cannot make it at all — CHOSEN: silent

9 men at armorer 1, ordered PRODUCE plate armor, which needs armorer 3. The ITEMS cell showed 3 plate armor appearing; it now shows no change at all.

CHOSEN — silent; the Problems panel carries it

ITEMS hover

was: 9 HUMN, 40 IRON (no production line at all)

Problems panel, unchanged

cannot produce plate armor: needs armorer 3, has armorer 1

One statement of the fact, in the panel that exists for it. A unit with no level in the skill at all reads the same way.

Rejected — the hover says why

was: 9 HUMN, 40 IRON This unit makes no plate armor: it needs armorer 3 and has armorer 1.

Answers the question where it is asked, but duplicates the panel's sentence — two things to keep in step — and would still say it when that warning is switched off in settings.

Deliberately not in this bead

The region's resource pool. A hex states how much of each resource it holds and every unit producing there shares it. The real report says MinersA (5105): Produces 20 iron for a unit whose men could make 40 — the pool is what took it down, and the app models none. Named here so this bead's verification is not confused by it: the preview will say 40 where the report said 20, and that is correct for this bead.
