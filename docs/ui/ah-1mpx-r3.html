W3 — what a multi-row selection does

The table is single-select today, and its one selected row drives the Unit panel and the map. A second, wider selection has to sit beside that without the two being confused.

Picked rows take a 15%-alpha wash; the cursor row keeps today's 25% and is the one that fills the Unit panel. The cursor is always one of the picked rows when anything is picked.

The gestures
ClickPicks that row alone, as today.
Shift+clickExtends from the cursor row to this one.
Ctrl/Cmd+clickAdds or removes one row.
Shift+↑/↓Extends by one row from the keyboard.
Ctrl/Cmd+APicks every row the filter is currently showing.
EscNarrows the pick back to the cursor row.
Right-clickAdd to army ▸ then an Army, or New Army…
DragOnto an Army in the rail.

Changing the source clears the pick. A selection that survived a jump from one hex to another would act on rows nobody can see.
