Today every skill-reading warning judges a unit on the skills the report gave it, and ignores men its own orders hand it this month. This page is only about the half of that which needs your decision: what the warnings should do when the merged skill cannot be worked out at all. The decision — when the merge cannot be computed

Three situations where the application cannot honestly say what the receiving unit will know. In each, the warnings can either go quiet for that unit or carry on judging it on the report's skills, exactly as today.

1. The report only estimates the headcount. A report writes approximately 50 men for a unit it cannot count exactly. The merge is a headcount-weighted average, so an estimated headcount makes every merged figure a guess.
2. No ruleset has been fetched. Without the catalogue there is no telling which item tags are people, so the application cannot even see that men moved.
3. The men arrive by TAKE FROM rather than GIVE. The application models a GIVE of men and does not model a TAKE of them — neither here nor in the units table.
A — the unit goes unjudged: every skill-reading warning is silent for it 
unit 2200                    ; report says "approximately 50 men", lumberjack 1
  PRODUCE LUMB
unit 1010
  GIVE 2200 40 HUMN          ; 1010's men know no lumberjack at all
40 unskilled men into ~50 who are LUMB 1 (30): the merged level is somewhere between 0 and 1, and which one depends on a headcount the report would not commit to.
Problems
Nothing to report in this hex.
  • Follows the module's own stated rule — a warning that is wrong is worse than one that is missing — and the sailing check already does exactly this: men arriving into a unit aboard silence the crew warning for the whole fleet.
  • The affected warnings are advisory. Nothing is blocked by their absence.
  • A real mistake in one of these units goes unmentioned. An estimated headcount is not rare on a large faction.
  • The panel gives no sign that it has gone quiet — the unit simply has no entries.
B — carry on judging it on the report's skills, as today
Problems
⚠ 2200 Woodsmen — cannot produce lumber: needs lumberjack 1, has lumberjack 1.
  • Nothing that warns today stops warning. No warning is ever lost.
  • It is the behaviour this bead exists to remove, kept for a subset of cases — and the subset is invisible to the player, so the panel is right about one unit and wrong about the next with nothing to tell them apart.

Which warnings this covers, either way

Every check that reads a unit's skills through its orders: producing without the skill, building without the skill, teaching (both the teacher's level and the pupil's), studying at maximum, and magic study outside a building.

Not the SILVER column, and not pillage-without-men. Those read the report's headcount as well as its skills, so putting them right is a change to the silver forecast rather than to the warnings — a separate bead, which I will file. And not the fleet crew warning, which is ah-z73s.3 and already waiting.

The other side — arriving men can make a warning appear

This follows from the same change and needs no decision, but it is worth seeing before you choose above.

unit 3400                    ; a mage, magic level 1, standing in no structure
  STUDY FORC
unit 3401
  GIVE 3400 1 HUMN           ; a man who knows FORC 3
The merged mage reaches force 2, and "above level 2" magic study needs a building.
Problems
⚠ 3400 Adept — studying force above level 2 outside a building that houses mages.
  • A warning that is right and that the player would otherwise meet only when the turn came back.
