A move that walks into a wall

The same underground piece in the app's default map. A unit in Ciestucshire (9,3) has the order MOVE N NW. North of Ciestucshire is a wall, because the report lists no exit that way. The route planner already never plans through a wall, so the only question here is what the map and the Problems list show for an order someone typed by hand.

The game's rules do not say what happens when a unit moves toward a side with no exit. They only say that a move that fails stops the rest of that unit's moves. Neither option claims more than that.

Option R1: the route becomes uncertain at the wall

The route is drawn solid up to the wall, a small red cross sits on the wall where it hits, and the rest of the route is dashed. That dashing is what the map already does when the sea stops a route, so it needs nothing new to learn. You still see where the order was heading.

Option R2: the route ends at the wall

The route is drawn to the wall and stops with a red bar across its tip. Nothing is drawn past it. It is the clearest picture of "you can't go this way", but you no longer see where the rest of the order was heading.
