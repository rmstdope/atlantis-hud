ah-1mpx.5 — where other factions' units live

The epic's round 4 asked whether the faction dossier folds into the source rail, and you said later — this bead. That drawing showed a Factions seen group with two factions in it. Before building it I counted what the fixtures actually contain, and the number changes the shape of the answer.

A mid-game report sees 17–29 other factions at once. A turn-0 report sees 102. A 172 px rail with one entry per faction is a scroll, not a list — so the drawing from round 4 cannot be built as drawn.

Counted over tests/fixtures/reports/: turn 40 → 17 factions / 112 foreign units; turn 42 → 18 / 126; turn 82 → 29 factions / 254 units / 61 units whose faction is hidden; turn 0 → 102 factions. Hiding is the rules working as written — rules/stealthobs: you see the faction only when your Observation beats its Stealth, so equal Observation shows you the unit and not who owns it.

Open this page before answering. All three options for each question are side by side, drawn at the real 172 px rail width with real fixture data — the difference is in the scrolling and does not survive being described.

R2 — what becomes of the faction dossier popover

The dossier (ah-bu2c) opens two ways: floating beside a faction name in the units table, and inline in the right-hand Faction panel from the attitudes list. It shows three things — Attitude, Seen in, and Known units — and hovering a row rings that hex on the map, which was its own bead (ah-mwqa). Only the third of those three is what the rail replaces.

Where this sits. ah-1mpx.3 (the export, PR #758) already ships the notice that a foreign member exports with no skills, and ah-1mpx.4 already lets any row be picked and dragged into an Army without an ownership test. So a foreign unit can already join an Army through This hex. What is missing, and what this bead is really about, is a way to reach a foreign unit without walking the map hex by hex.

Verified, not recalled: rules/stealthobs (the faction is shown only when Observation beats Stealth); rules/reportformat (“if you can see a unit, you can see any large items it is carrying”, silver and herbs excepted). Counts are from tests/fixtures/reports/.
