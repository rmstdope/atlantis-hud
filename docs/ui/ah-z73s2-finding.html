The states the two questions above do not show

These follow from whichever you choose and need no separate decision, but they are what the wording will actually have to cover. Say if any of them reads wrong.

Problems
⚠ 3100 Timber crew — giving 1 man to this unit lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 2.   one man: "man", not "men"
⚠ 3100 Timber crew — giving 99 men to this unit lowers its lumberjack from 1 to 0.   the skill is diluted away entirely and leaves the Skills cell; "to 0" is what is left
  • A gift that raises or holds the level says nothing at all. The Problems panel gets no entry, and the Skills cell keeps the ordinary italic-brass marking it already has.
  • A unit formed this month can never trigger it — it starts with no skills, so nothing can fall.
  • A unit whose merged skills cannot be worked out — an estimated headcount, no ruleset, men arriving by TAKE — says nothing either. That is the other page's decision.
  • The giving unit is never mentioned. Its own skills do not change: dividing evenly among people leaves its points per man exactly as they were.

Question 4 — on or off when the application is first opened

Every check ships on except Unguarded hexes, which was turned off because it fires across the whole map and trains the player to ignore the panel.

On — the player is told the first time it happens
It fires only when a gift actually costs a level, which is uncommon, so it will not flood the panel. But it warns about something perfectly legal that the player may have meant.
Off — the player turns it on if they want it
Nobody is warned about a turn they wrote deliberately. But a player who does not know the rule is exactly the one who will not go looking for the switch.
