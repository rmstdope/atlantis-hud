ah-z73s.4 — when one route helped and the other hurt

The last thing I cannot decide for you. Everything else is settled and this page re-opens none of it.

The case

A unit of ten at lumberjack 2 is given five skilled lumberjacks — which takes it up to lumberjack 3 — and then buys nine peasants, which takes it down to lumberjack 1. The report said 2; the month ends at 1, so the advisory fires. But the gift is the half that helped.

unit 1010
GIVE 3100 5 LUMB        five skilled men: 2 → 3

unit 3100
BUY 9 PEAS          nine unskilled: 3 → 1
PRODUCE LUMB

The rules fix the order: Give orders run before Market orders (rules/sequenceofevents), so the gift is always first and the buy always second.

D1 — name both routes whenever men arrived by both
Problems
⚠ 3100 Timber crew — giving 5 men to this unit and buying 9 more lowers its lumberjack from 2 to 1.
  • One rule, stated in one line, with nothing to compute per route: men came by both, so both are named.
  • Truthful about what the player would have to undo — deleting the BUY alone leaves the unit at 3, but deleting the GIVE alone leaves it at 1 as well, so both lines are load-bearing.
  • Blames a gift that raised the level. A player who reads it as “the gift cost me a level” has been misled about which order to look at.
D2 — name only the routes that lowered it
Problems
⚠ 3100 Timber crew — buying 9 men into this unit lowers its lumberjack from 2 to 1.
  • Names only the order actually worth changing, which is the whole point of the advisory.
  • The two levels in the sentence are 2 and 1, but the buy alone took it from 3 to 1. The message's own numbers no longer describe the step it names.
  • Needs a per-route judgement the merge does not currently produce, and a rule for the case where the drop is caused by the two together and by neither alone.
D3 — name only the routes that lowered it, and give that route's own step
Problems
⚠ 3100 Timber crew — buying 9 men into this unit lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 1.
  • Internally consistent: the order named, the men named, and the two levels that order moved it between.
  • The 3 appears nowhere the player can see. The report says 2 and the Skills cell will say 1; the intermediate level exists only inside the check.
  • The advisory stops being “what the report said, and what you will have” — which is the shape every sentence here was built on.
How rare is it? It needs a gift of men who are better at the skill than the receiver, in the same month as a recruit into that same unit, with the net still falling. My recommendation is D1: it is the only option whose numbers are the report's figure and the month's figure, which is what every other sentence in this advisory promises.
