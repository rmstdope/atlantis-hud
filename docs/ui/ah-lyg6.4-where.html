ah-lyg6.4 — where the study-order export lives

Three placements for the same thing: turning next turn's plan into STUDY and TEACH lines, grouped by faction, for the clipboard or a file. The text itself is the other mockup (ah-lyg6.4-format.html); ignore its exact wording here and look only at where the control is and how much you see before you send it.

A — a third view inside the study planner

F4 opens the planner; a third tab beside All mages and Schedule shows the generated text, one section per faction, each with its own Copy button and a Save… beside it.

A Orders tab

You read what you are about to send, per faction, and copy one ally's block at a time straight into the mail you are writing them.
