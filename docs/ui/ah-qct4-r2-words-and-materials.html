Q5 Q1 and Q2 disagree at the boundary, and only you can settle it

You chose silence when the number is nought (Q1 A) and a sentence when it is three (Q2 C). The same sentence writes itself perfectly well at nought. So: does it appear there, or not?

What the machinery does on its own. To say “3 rather than 8” the arithmetic has to carry both figures — what the men who actually work this month make, and what the report’s own headcount would have made. Once it does, the test for the sentence is simply “fewer men work this month than the report showed”, and that is true at three and equally true at nought. Q5 B is what falls out; Q5 A costs one extra condition whose only job is to go quiet in the case that surprised you most. It also keeps the gaining direction silent by construction, which is Q3 A as you answered it.

Q6 The exact words

Whichever of Q5 you pick, this is the string that ships. It is read beside the two sentences already in that hover, quoted here for the fit.

Q6·A — as drafted in round 1
Items hoverwas: 8 ORC, 20 IRON, 100 SILV
Includes 3 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month.
Its men leave before production resolves: 3 swords rather than the 8 the report’s men would have made.
  • Names the cause, the sequencing and both numbers.
  • “the report’s men” is our vocabulary, not a player’s.
Q6·B — shorter, no jargon   recommended
Items hoverwas: 8 ORC, 20 IRON, 100 SILV
Includes 3 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month.
This unit has men for 3 swords: GIVE resolves before production, so the men it gives away this month do not work for it.
  • Opens with the shipped cap sentence’s own shape — “This unit has … for N …” — so the hover reads as one voice.
  • Says the rule once, which is the thing that was actually surprising.
  • Longer.
Q6·C — two clauses, no rule
Items hoverwas: 8 ORC, 20 IRON, 100 SILV
Includes 3 SWOR this unit will produce. Production resolves last, so they cannot be spent this month.
It gives away 5 of its 8 men before production resolves, so it makes 3 swords and not 8.
  • Concrete: names how many left and when.
  • Has to be assembled from four numbers rather than two, and reads badly for a TAKE.
