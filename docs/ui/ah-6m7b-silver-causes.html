ah-6m7b — where the silver causes live

You chose balance walk plus the change list: UnitSilver.changes should stop being a second derivation and come from the ledger's own movements. There is a catch worth one minute of your time before I plan it.

The ledger's movement list has 17 causes (ItemChangeCause, orders/effects.rs:443). The SILVER column's list has seven the ledger has never heard of: Taxed, Pillaged, Claimed, Worked, Entertained, Studied, CastEarned. The ledger applies all seven to its balances — it just never records them as movements.

And the ITEMS popup draws a line for every tag the unit holds, silver included, with no filter (packages/shared/src/unitCellPopup.ts:288). So teaching the ledger those seven causes puts new sentences in a second surface — which is the exact failure docs/retrospectives/ah-728m.2.2.md records.

The silver line is already there, and it is already half a story. It names the market and the gift because those are movements; it says nothing about the 250 taxed, the 190 worked or the 100 studied, yet the balance it shows moved by all of them. A player reading 1000 → 1216 against bought 24, gave 100 cannot make the arithmetic come out.

Option A One list — the ITEMS popup gains the seven sentences

The seven causes join ItemChangeCause, the ledger records them, and both surfaces read one list. itemCauseClause gets seven new arms, so the silver line becomes whole.

The cost is that the ITEMS popup says more than it does today about a tag some players only ever read in the SILVER column — and the clauses are in the turn's order, so the gift comes first and the wage last, which reads oddly beside a running total nobody sees.

The cost is a filter that has to be justified in code and kept true — a list of seven causes the ITEMS popup deliberately drops, which is a small new thing to keep in step.

Option C Leave the change list alone

Unify the balance walk only. UnitSilver.changes stays silver.rs's own derivation with its own seven causes, and neither popup changes.

This is the answer you moved away from a moment ago, and it is here only so the three sit side by side. It leaves the smaller half of the duplication in place: the balances stop drifting, the two change lists can still.
