ah-bumi — a unit's CLAIM counts as income

The Silver column prices eight kinds of order and CLAIM is not one of them. forecast_unit has arms for Tax, Work, Entertain, Sell, Study, Cast, Buy, Give and Withdraw. No Claim. So a unit funded by claiming from the faction's purse reads as ending the month short when the claim covers it — and that is the ordinary shape of a new or idle unit.

Most of this needs no decision. The hover has generic In and Out rows, so a claim simply makes In bigger: no new row, no new string, no new colour. The existing order-check ledger already credits CLAIM (semantics.rs:701), so the column is catching up with the warning rather than diverging from it.

One thing does need deciding, because the epic set two different precedents for a shared pool and this is the third case.

Chosen cap at the purse, and warn separately when the total cannot be met

Decided 2026-08-23: A — and the navigator added the half that makes it safe: "file a new bead to introduce a warning if the total CLAIM in all hexes exceeds the unclaimed silver." Filed as ah-wur4. So the column stays simple and honest per unit, and a warning carries the case where the faction cannot meet every claim — exactly the shape ah-1wcw.2 took, where an unsellable sale earns 0 and the shipped not-traded-here check is what tells you why.

CLAIM draws on one faction-wide pool, which the report states outright (Unclaimed silver: $4935). Several units can claim from it in one turn. The epic has already met a shared pool twice and answered differently each time:

So which precedent CLAIM follows is a real choice, not a lookup.

A Cap at the purse, no contentionChosen

Each claiming unit gets the lesser of what it claimed and what the faction has. Two units claiming 4000 each from a purse of 4935 both read 4000.

C Uncapped

Count the claim as written, whatever the purse holds — exactly what the shipped order-check ledger does today.
  • Simplest, and the column and the not-enough-silver warning agree by construction.
  • Rejected.
  • A unit claiming 9000 from a purse of 4935 reads as though it got 9000, which the game will refuse.
  • Overstating income is the direction that makes a player commit orders that fail.
