Round 4 — what an emptied FORM leaves behind

Bead ah-ty3s.1. Rounds 1–3 are settled and I am not reopening them: A1, B1, D1, E1, F1. Earlier pages: docs/ui/ah-ty3s-formed-unit-orders.html, docs/ui/ah-ty3s-round2.html.

Writing the plan turned up one thing E1 promises that the game's own rules take away again, and I do not think I should settle it alone. The hex is the one from round 1: 1922 forms NEW 1, gives it 100, and new 1 buys five Plainsmen.

Question 7 yours · You empty new 1's editor. What is still on screen?

E1 says — “emptying the editor leaves an empty form 1/end standing and the row selected”. The first half is entirely in my hands and holds. The second half is not: rules/form says “if no recruits are gained at all, the empty unit will be dissolved”, and the units table draws the forecast, so a form 1 with nothing in it produces no unit and therefore no row. That is ah-dhga's existing rule, not anything this bead does — but it is what makes the row vanish about a third of a second after the last keystroke.

K1 The editor stays on the block; the row goes

The orders pane keeps editing form 1 because the document still has it — the pane stops asking the forecast whether new 1 exists. The units table drops the row, because a dissolved unit is not a unit. Type buy 5 plainsmen back and the row returns.
