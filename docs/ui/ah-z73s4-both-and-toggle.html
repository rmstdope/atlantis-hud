ah-z73s.4 — a unit given men and recruiting, and the Settings toggle

Two things the widening to recruiting opened. First: you decided one entry per skill for the whole month — but a unit can be given men and recruit in the same month, and both dilute the same skill. Second: the toggle's agreed title and description are both written about a GIVE, and are now not true.

1 — both routes in one month

A unit of ten lumberjacks is given five unskilled men and recruits four more. The gift takes it from lumberjack 3 to 2; the recruits take it from 2 to 1. The rules run gifts first — Give orders before Market orders in rules/sequenceofevents — so the order of the two steps is fixed, not arbitrary.

unit 1010
GIVE 3100 5 PEAS

unit 3100
BUY 4 PEAS
PRODUCE LUMB
M1 — one entry per route, in the game's order
Problems
⚠ 3100 Timber crew — giving 5 men to this unit lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 2.
⚠ 3100 Timber crew — recruiting 4 men into this unit lowers its lumberjack from 2 to 1.
  • Both agreed sentences are used exactly as written. Nothing new to word, nothing new to test.
  • Each step is separately true, and the two read as a chain: 3 → 2 → 1.
  • Two entries about one unit ending at one level — the very thing you rejected when you chose one entry per skill for the month.
  • A player who fixes only the gift is still left at 2, and has to read both lines to see that.
M2 — one entry, naming both routes
Problems
⚠ 3100 Timber crew — giving 5 men to this unit and recruiting 4 more lowers its lumberjack from 3 to 1.
  • Keeps one entry per skill for the month, exactly as you chose, and states the month's real drop.
  • Names both causes, so the player knows fixing one is not enough.
  • A third sentence shape, with its own singular cases on two counts (“1 man… 1 more”).
M3 — one entry, route-neutral when both happened
Problems
⚠ 3100 Timber crew — 9 men joining this unit lower its lumberjack from 3 to 1.
  • Uses joining, the word you chose for the crew clause in ah-z73s.3 precisely because it is true of a gift and a recruit alike.
  • One entry, one number for the arrivals, one drop. Shortest of the three.
  • Says the least about why. A player who gave men and did not think of the BUY is told nine men arrived and left to work out which orders those were.
  • A third shape all the same, and it needs its own count agreement (lower / lowers).
Only the same skill is at stake. If the gift lowers lumberjack and the recruits lower mining, there is no clash — that is already two entries by the one-per-skill rule, each in its own sentence, and every option above agrees.

2 — the Settings toggle

Agreed while planning ah-z73s.2: title Transfers that lower a skill, description A GIVE of men that leaves the receiving unit at a lower skill level than the report gave it. It sits in the Orders group after Gifts to units that are not here, and is ON by default. A recruit is neither a transfer nor a GIVE, so both strings are now wrong.

T1 — one toggle, retitled to cover both
Orders
Gifts to units that are not hereA GIVE naming a unit that is not in the same region.
Arrivals that lower a skillMen joining a unit, by gift or by recruiting, that leave it at a lower skill level than the report gave it.
  • One switch for one idea: unskilled men arriving cost you a level. The player does not have to know the two routes are implemented separately.
  • A player who only ever wanted to be warned about gifts cannot have that.
T2 — one toggle, keep the agreed title, widen only the description
Orders
Gifts to units that are not hereA GIVE naming a unit that is not in the same region.
Transfers that lower a skillA GIVE of men, or a recruit, that leaves the unit at a lower skill level than the report gave it.
  • The title you already approved does not move.
  • Transfers still does not cover recruiting, and the title is what a player scanning the list reads — the description is the part they do not read.
T3 — two toggles, one per route
Orders
Gifts to units that are not hereA GIVE naming a unit that is not in the same region.
Gifts that lower a skillA GIVE of men that leaves the receiving unit at a lower skill level than the report gave it.
Recruits that lower a skillA purchase of men that leaves the buying unit at a lower skill level than the report gave it.
  • Each can be silenced alone. A player who recruits constantly and knows what it costs can turn only that off.
  • The two sentences are two codes anyway, which is how every other pair of messages in the panel is switched.
  • Two entries in a group that already has several, for one idea.
  • If you choose M2 or M3 above, the both-at-once entry belongs to neither toggle and a third rule is needed to say which switch hides it.
