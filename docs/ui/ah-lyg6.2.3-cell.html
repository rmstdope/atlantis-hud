ah-lyg6.2.3 — round two: inside G2

You chose G2 — the grid is the planner, a mage's plan is a queue of goals, and clicking a cell says what he studies from that turn on. Six turn columns, no money column. One bead. Round one is still at ah-lyg6.2.3-schedule.html.

Four things that choice leaves open. Everything else I am deciding myself and writing into the plan where you can overrule it in a sentence — including these two, which follow the habits the application already has: a cell menu commits on Set and nothing is stored until you press it, and the free-text note commits like a hex note does — a Save button, ⌘↵, and Esc to abandon.

Decided by me unless you say otherwise

  • A cell menu writes nothing until Set; Cancel and Escape close it and change nothing; Clear from here drops that goal and everything after it.
  • The note commits the way a hex note does — a Save button, ⌘↵ to save, Esc to abandon — because that is the only habit the application has for stored free text.
  • Arrow keys walk the grid cell by cell, Enter opens the cell's menu, and the hover card is what Enter-less focus shows; the list's keyboard from ah-lyg6.2.2 is unchanged in All mages.
  • The view you were last in is remembered while the game is open and forgotten when it closes.
  • No cell offers TEACH in this bead — ah-lyg6.3 adds that — but the cell already knows how to draw one, so nothing is reshaped when it lands.
  • An ally's mage from a stale sheet is planned exactly like your own, from his sheet's projection, and his row is tinted as it is in All mages.
