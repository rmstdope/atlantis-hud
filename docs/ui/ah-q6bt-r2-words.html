PILLAGE — the warning, the silence and the column

Settled in round 1: G1 — only the units that issue PILLAGE count towards the threshold. D1 — the take is divided proportionally between the combat ready men that participate. Both are your reading over the rules text, recorded as such.
Why no warning fired at all — found in the code, not guessed. combat_ready_in (semantics.rs:824) sums the hex with combat_ready(&facts, ruleset)? — the ? is inside the loop, so one uncountable unit anywhere in the hex makes the whole total None, and check_pillage_men returns at its let Some(ready) = … else { return } with no warning for any unit. A unit is uncountable when its headcount is estimated or when a transfer this month cannot be followed — and City Guards gave 2418 a sword. Your hex has ninety-odd units; it takes one.

G1 shrinks that blast radius on its own, because only PILLAGE-issuing units get counted. Three things it leaves open, and all three are yours.

P The warning's sentence, now that the count is not the region's

It reads today: “cannot pillage here: needs 227 combat ready men, this region has 0”. Under G1 “this region has” is simply false — the region has 445; the units that ordered it have 0.

  • Keeps the existing “this unit has…” tail, which is already built and already tested.
  • Longer than today's line.

  PILLAGE cannot pillage here: needs 227 combat ready men, has 1 — this unit has 1 man with no weapon it can use
  • Shortest of the three.
  • “has 1” with no subject reads as this unit has 1, which is right by accident here and wrong the moment two units order PILLAGE together.
P3 — spell the rule out
unit 2418
  PILLAGE cannot pillage here: the units ordering PILLAGE have 1 combat ready man between them and this region needs 227 — this unit has 1 man with no weapon it can use
  • Closest to how you described the rule.
  • Two clauses before the number that matters; the existing warnings all lead with the shortfall.

U When the pillaging unit's men cannot be counted

Exactly your case: a unit receives a sword this month through a GIVE the checker cannot follow, so whether it is combat ready is genuinely unknown. Today that produces silence, which is the bug you filed.
