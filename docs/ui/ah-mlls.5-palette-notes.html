Your notes in the search box — agreed

Typing a word from a note you wrote — on a hex, or on a mage in the study planner — finds the note in the search box (Ctrl/Cmd+K). Example notes: on hill (3,29) in Grey Reach, turn 61: “Scouted turn 61. / A seam of iron under the ridge — come back with miners once the road is safe.” On the mage Ulla the Grey (2214): “Needs FIRE 3 before the siege, then switch her to iron-working? Ask the alliance.”

After choosing

A hex note (Enter or click)

The search box closes and the map goes to the hex as choosing the hex does. Its notes are scrolled so the chosen note is in view, outlined in gold for about two seconds, then back to normal. Nothing is edited.
