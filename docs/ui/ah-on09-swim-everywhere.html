Giant turtles swim in every world — the agreed shape

Swimming, agreed for Trident in docs/ui/ah-g9sf.7-swimmers.html, now applies in every world whose data gives anything a swimming capacity. In New Origins every unit shows a Swim tile and a swim popup line; a swimmer crosses any sea, coastal or deep, because New Origins' rules carry no coastal-water limit; and swimming that comes from items such as giant turtles reads exactly like swimming by nature. Every sentence, mark and refusal agreed for Trident is reused word for word; Trident and Arcanum keep their coastal rule.

1 every unit, as in Trident

New Origins becomes a game with swimming. Every unit has four tiles; one that cannot swim reads 0, exactly as it already does in Trident.
