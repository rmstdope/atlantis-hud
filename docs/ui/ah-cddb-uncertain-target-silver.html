Shipping to a quartermaster that may refuse — what the sender's silver says

A shipment to another faction's quartermaster whose position is in the report has a price that can be worked out. What the report cannot show is whether that faction accepts goods from ours, or — for a Caravanserai owner whose skills are hidden — whether it can receive them at all. The goods already stay with the sender and carry + ?. Until now the month's silver was added up as though shipping were free. Instead, the silver shows both ways the month can end.

unit 900 ; owns a Caravanserai, Quartermaster 5, silver 1000
  TRANSPORT 7003 9 FUR ; another faction's quartermaster, 3 hexes: 45 silver

3 · Accepted, but the sender could not pay

unit 900 ; silver 20
  TRANSPORT 7003 9 FUR ; 45 if accepted
short $25 if unit 7003 accepts: this unit can have $20 and its orders spend $45, so it ships none of the 9 furs ordered

Refused or accepted, the furs and the silver stay, so the month is certain and reads plain 20. The player is still told the shipment could never have gone: the existing shortfall sentence with the condition after its amount, counted, marked and walked like any other shortfall.

4 · The same rules, everywhere else

The other uncertain target

9 FUR + ?
was: 9 FURCould not count 9 FUR for unit 7004 because your report does not show whether it is an eligible transport target.

A Caravanserai owner whose skills the report does not show. Its silver behaves exactly as sections 1–3.

Nothing changes when the shipment is free
TRANSPORT 7003 9 FUR ; Trident, two hexes: free
Silver 1000

Accepted or refused it costs nothing, so the silver stays certain: no or, no note. In New Origins a shipment between quartermasters is charged at any distance, so there it is uncertain.

An unknown price means no total: ? wins over or, and only the existing notes remain.

And the rest

A shipment that is certainly refused for another reason — out of reach, not a quartermaster — is never uncertain and keeps its own warning. While the forecast is loading or in error, none of this is shown. On a narrow window the notes wrap inside the panel. Nothing new is remembered: a reload or new report works it out again. The Transport warning switch never takes away or or to, because they describe the figure rather than warn about an order; the conditional shortfall follows the silver warning, like every shortfall.

5 · Shipments each affordable alone, but not together

unit 900 ; silver 520
  TRANSPORT 7003 9 FUR ; 45 if accepted
  TRANSPORT 7004 20 GRAI ; 500 if accepted

Either can be paid for alone. If both are accepted the furs go first and 475 is left, so the grain stays. The endings are 520, 475 and 20, so the silver reads 20 to 520.

Warned, naming every acceptance it needs
short $25 if units 7003 and 7004 both accept: this unit can have $520 and its orders spend $545, so it ships none of the 20 grain ordered

The player is warned about the ending in which a shipment fails, the same way section 3 warns about a single one. With three it reads if units 7003, 7004 and 7005 all accept.
