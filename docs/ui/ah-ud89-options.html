ah-ud89 — a contended taxer's CAST and BUY ALL caps

When two of your own units tax one region whose pool cannot pay them both, the app already knows how the money splits — the Silver column's income row shows the split figure, and the Problems panel says the pool is oversubscribed. But the cap on what that unit can then CAST or BUY ALL is still sized against the whole pool. So the app tells the unit it makes an amulet, and in the same breath tells you it ends the month a hundred short.

This is a deliberate convention, not an oversight. ah-omn7 Q2 decided that a cap reads the hopeful purse while the money columns keep the settled figure — you accepted then that “where a tax really is split, the projected inventory can show goods the unit will not get”. The narrow question here is whether that should still hold when the hopefulness comes from your own other units, which the app can see and has already counted. Nothing outside that is being re-opened.

The contradiction, in one screen. The Silver hover says the unit cannot pay for its cast; the ITEMS column projects the amulet anyway, with nothing qualifying it, and neither surface says the two are the same fact or which of them to believe. The cast count is the half that is wrong, and it is the half nothing speaks about.

Option A nothing changes — the cap stays hopeful

Exactly the screen above. ah-omn7 Q2's rule holds everywhere without exception: one rule for caps, no new plumbing, no test churn. You close this bead.

  • One rule, stated once, that a player can learn: a cap assumes your income arrives in full.
  • A player who is about to cancel 901's TAX — a normal thing to do once the Problems panel tells them — sees the amulet they are about to secure, not a zero that would vanish a second later.
  • The projected inventory keeps an amulet the game will refuse, and no sentence anywhere says why the month ends short.
  • It is the case the app is best placed to be certain about — both taxers are yours, both headcounts are exact, the split is arithmetic.

Option B the cap reads the settled share, where the app settled it

When the contention is between your own units and every headcount is exact, the app already computes each unit's share. The CAST and BUY ALL caps read that share instead of the pool. The money columns are untouched; only the count and its sentence move.

  • The two surfaces and the Problems panel finally tell one story, and the month no longer ends red for a cast the app itself said was affordable.
  • The projected inventory stops showing an item the game will refuse.
  • Two rules where there was one: a cap is hopeful about a foreign taxer and settled about your own. A player has to know which.
  • A player who intends to cancel 901's TAX sees a discouraging 0 until they do — and cancelling it is the fix the panel is pointing at.
  • Real plumbing, and it is not the cheap version: making only the column read the share re-opens exactly the SILVER/ITEMS drift ah-6m7b was built to close. Done properly the settlement reaches the ledger, and the ledger’s deliberate accept-on-doubt policy then needs a second, optimistic reading of its own or a false not-enough-silver fires in every contended tax hex.

Option C keep the hopeful count, and say that it is hopeful

  • One rule for caps survives, and the screen stops contradicting itself.
  • A player about to cancel the other TAX still sees the amulet they are securing.
  • Cheapest of the three by a wide margin — two sentences, no change to any number.
  • The projected inventory still shows an amulet the game will refuse; the player has to read a sentence to know it is provisional.
  • One more line on a hover that is already dense, on every contended taxer that casts or buys.
