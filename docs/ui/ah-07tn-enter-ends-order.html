Enter on a last word finishes the order

Agreed: when a suggestion is accepted with Enter and the game allows no further word after it, the word is written with no trailing space and a new line opens, exactly as Enter would open it. STUDY is the one exception: it finishes after the skill although a level may follow. Words after which more may follow keep today's space. The suggestions list itself looks as it does today.
