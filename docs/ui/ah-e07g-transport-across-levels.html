Shipping to a quartermaster on another level — B · treated as out of reach

Unit 900 stands on the surface. Unit 901, the quartermaster it ships to, stands in the underworld. In this variant the application assumes the game will not carry goods between levels, and reports it exactly like a shipment that is too far away: an ordinary problem, the goods stay with the sender, nothing is charged. Chosen knowing the game’s rules do not say this either way — this variant is a bet that they refuse it.
