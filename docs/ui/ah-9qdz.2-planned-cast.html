5 · Keyboard, closing, and saving

  • ↓ from a turn's study moves to its cast half when it has one, else to the next mage's study; ↑ the reverse. ← → move along the same half; when the neighbouring turn has no cast half, they land on its study.
  • ↵ or a click opens the list; focus starts on what is ticked; Esc or a click outside closes it with nothing changed, and focus returns to the half.
  • A pick is saved with the plan straight away, like a study, and comes back after a reload, a switch of game, a backup restore and when a new turn's report arrives.
  • In a narrow window the grid scrolls sideways as it does today; the cells keep their width.
