What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (0.13.0)
===========================================

## What your units will hold and earn

- What a unit will produce now counts its skill level and its tools, instead of assuming every man works at level one — for a trained unit this was often three to five times too low
- A unit is no longer shown producing more than the hex yields, or more than its share when several of your units work the same resource
- A unit that gives its men or materials away this month no longer shows a month's production it can no longer make
- The Items column now follows what a PRODUCE makes and consumes, what a BUILD spends, and what a CAST creates — including spells that only sometimes succeed
- The Items column now follows goods you BUY, SELL, WITHDRAW and TAKE, and goods transported between hexes
- The Men column now follows men bought, taken, or given away as a whole class, so a unit's headcount matches the orders you wrote
- A unit that buys or sells the same goods twice is no longer charged or paid twice
- BUY ALL now brings the goods it paid for, instead of spending the silver and delivering nothing
- Giving a whole class of items now moves what the class covers, and anything the game will not hand over stays put
- The same item written a different legal way — the singular, the plural, in capitals — now moves the same goods in every column

## Writing orders

- Men who arrive this month bring their skills with them, so a unit given trained men is no longer warned that it lacks the skill they brought
- The Problems panel now explains a skill level that fell because unskilled men joined the unit, rather than only reporting the level
- A crew given sailors is no longer told it is short of them
- A unit given two orders for one month is now told that only one of them will run
- Pillaging is now checked properly: you are warned when your units cannot carry it out, and the silver is shared between the units that actually order it
- The Silver column and the pillage warning now account for what this month's orders move, instead of pricing a unit as the report printed it
- A unit you create with FORM now gets a Silver figure and has its orders checked, like any other unit

## Armies

- Group units into a named Army and keep it between turns, then point the units list at it instead of at a hex
- Pick several units at once and drag them into an Army together
- Send an Army straight to the battle simulator, with each unit's combat skills read from the turn's battle reports — including other factions' units
- See every other faction's units in one list, and narrow it to a single faction
- Clicking a unit in All my units, Other factions or an Army now takes the map to the hex it stands in
- All my units now shows the coming month, the same as This hex, instead of the figures the report arrived with
- Switching to another Army or hex clears the units filter with it, so a new list is never hidden behind an old search
- The faction you have pinned reads as one chip, with your declared attitude beside it

## Magic

- A new study tree shows every magic skill and what it stands on, grouped by branch of study
- The tree says what each of your mages already knows, what they may begin, and what they can never raise further
- See the whole graph in one pannable picture, and click any skill to light its full lineage without it running off the screen

## Maps

- A map you export can now be imported again, and it only ever adds to what you know instead of replacing your turn

## Layout

- The header keeps to one row as a turn's problems are counted, and gathers everything the turn wants checked into a single chip
- Planning a move no longer hides the orders editor behind the units pane
