What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.11.1)
============================================

Almost all of this release is the Silver column, which arrived in v0.11.0 and got
several things wrong. If you have been reading that column and distrusting it,
this is the release that earns it back.

## What your units will have at month end

Money that was never counted, and now is:

- A unit that taxes because its taxing flag is set, without a TAX order this
  turn, earns its tax in the column instead of showing nothing
- A unit with no month-long order works automatically and earns the region's
  wage — it was being shown as idle, earning nothing, and sometimes as starving
  when the wages would have fed it
- A pillaging unit earns twice the region's takings, as it always did in the
  warnings and never did in the column

Money that was counted against the wrong purse, or not at all:

- A withdrawal is paid for out of your faction's unclaimed fund, not out of the
  withdrawing unit's own silver
- Producing something that costs silver — a catapult, a steel defender — now
  shows that cost

Units that were shown starving when they were not:

- A unit with food eats it rather than starving, whether or not you have set
  CONSUME. The flag decides whether food is used *before* silver, not whether it
  is used at all
- A faction-mate in the same hex lends its silver for upkeep, with or without the
  SHARE flag — the game has always done this automatically
- Your faction's unclaimed silver feeds a unit that would otherwise starve,
  without needing a CLAIM order

Money that was counted twice:

- Your own units in one hex now split that hex's tax base, its wages and
  entertainment, and what its market will buy and sell between them, instead of
  each being promised the whole thing
- A pillage in a hex leaves your own taxers in it with nothing, as it does in the
  game

## New warnings

- You are warned when your units between them promise more of a hex's tax base,
  wages, entertainment or market than the hex actually holds
- You are warned when you order TAX or PILLAGE in a hex another faction guards,
  which will simply be refused
- You are warned when a unit is ordered to produce something it lacks the skill
  or level to make, so the wasted month is caught before you send the orders
- A PILLAGE earns nothing unless you have the combat-ready men in the hex to take
  it, and the figure now says so

## Wording

- Counts and item names agree in number — "buys 200 horses", not "buys 200 horse"
