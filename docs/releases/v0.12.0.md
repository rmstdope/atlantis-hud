What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (0.12.0)
===========================================

## Taxing and pillaging

- A unit told it cannot pillage now says why its men do not count, so you know whether to give them weapons, teach them combat or hand them mounts
- A unit is told the cheapest thing it is missing, rather than only being told it has no weapons
- A combat mage's men now count toward the number of men a hex needs before you can pillage it
- A unit that taxes by its taxing flag is now warned when a foreign unit guards the hex, so you are not counting on silver that will never arrive
- A unit carrying the taxing flag that you also give a month-long order is no longer treated as taxing, because the order it was given takes its whole month

## Silver

- Silver taken from another unit now shows up in that unit's Silver figure
- A unit paid for by a neighbour in the same hex is no longer reported as unable to afford its orders
- The Silver hover can now tell you several things about a unit's money at once, instead of only ever showing the first one that applied

## Writing orders

- Producing from a boat is now judged against the region the boat is sailing to, so a unit fishing on its way out to sea is no longer told it cannot fish here
- A unit fishing from a boat whose captain sails away is no longer flagged with an error
- An order that overdraws what the units in a hex hold between them is now marked on the order line you wrote it on, not only in the region panel
- A unit that gains pack animals this month is no longer wrongly reported as overloaded and unable to move

## Map

- Picking a unit from the Problems list now brings the map to it, so the selection ring is never somewhere you cannot see
- Structures with a description after their name — a closed lair, a shipyard listing its hulls — are now drawn as what they are instead of as a generic building
