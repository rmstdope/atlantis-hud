What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.21.0)
=============================================

## Your faction's allowances

- The Regions, Quartermasters, Mages and Apprentices counts now follow the orders you are writing, instead of repeating what your last report happened to say — a game you have just imported and not yet given orders shows 0 regions in use, not last month's figure
- Writing a FACTION order changes the faction type and every limit that depends on it, straight away, so you can see the extra regions a switch to more Martial buys you while you write the orders that spend them
- The faction type line shows the change as `Martial 1, Magic 1 → Martial 3, Magic 2`, with what your report said dimmed
- A FACTION order the game will refuse is called out both in your orders and under the faction type, naming exactly what is in the way — "5 mages, MAGIC 2 allows 3"
- The warning about taxing and trading in too many regions now counts your TAX and PILLAGE orders as well as PRODUCE, so a faction that taxes and pillages past its allowance is warned instead of finding out from the turn report
- On older reports, which give taxing and trading separate limits, taxing is now checked against its own limit for the first time

## The Production window

- A new **Production…** button in the faction panel opens a window listing every hex your orders tax, pillage or produce in — also reachable from the command palette
- Each hex says whether you are collecting all the tax it offers, and whether every resource there is being produced up to what is available, so a hex where the iron is worked and the wood is left is easy to spot
- A bar at the top shows how many of your region slots the orders use against your limit

## Map

- Picking a hex by its id now takes the map to the level that hex is actually on, instead of leaving you looking at the level you were already on

## Comparing turns

- Clicking a turn opens the Changes dialog directly, and dismissing the dialog ends the comparison

## Fetching and sending

- When Fetch or Send is greyed out because you have not typed your password, the app now says so and outlines the empty field in red, instead of leaving you to guess
- The two fetch choices are now named "Latest report" and "All reports not yet fetched", which says what each one will actually do
- Import and Fetch now use the same colours as Export and Send in the header

## Web version

- Having several Atlantis HUD tabs open no longer leaves the application greyed out with nothing said — including Create game, which left nothing to be done at all. It now tells you what is in the way
- The site installs for offline use again: some of its icons were missing, and that quietly stopped the site from installing and from updating itself
