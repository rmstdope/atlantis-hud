What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.23.0)
============================================

## Your units' silver

- When nothing you can see will pay a unit's upkeep, its Silver popup now says it may still avoid starvation if an allied unit in the same region is sharing silver or food
- The Silver popup no longer claims shared silver covers a unit's shortfall when a unit sharing silver in that hex has an uncertain month; it now says the hex's shared silver could not be added up
- Grain left behind by a TRANSPORT ... EXCEPT order now counts towards feeding your units set to CONSUME FACTION, as it will when the turn runs
- A STUDY of a skill that does not exist now gets a warning naming it, and the unit's silver is forecast as if it had no month-long order instead of showing ?

## Study planner

- Tick "Assume every mage is sheltered" to see your mages' study plan as if every one of them were in a suitable building; the planner remembers your choice
