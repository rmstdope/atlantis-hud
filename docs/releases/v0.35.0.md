What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.34.0 -> v0.35.0)
========================================================

## Writing orders

- The orders check now warns when a unit's MOVE would walk into an ocean or lake hex, which the game refuses without a ship
- The orders check now warns when a flying unit's MOVE ends over an ocean or lake, where it would drown
- A TRANSPORT or DISTRIBUTE of livestock, or of anything else the game will not ship, is now flagged before you send your orders
- In New Origins games, lakes now count as water for movement, as they do in the game: a lake shows no walking cost, and a walk into one is flagged
- Fixed New Origins reports with lakes in them warning that the ruleset does not define lake (reported in [#1961](https://github.com/rmstdope/atlantis-hud/issues/1961))
- Undo in the study planner, after "Put into my orders", now takes back only the study orders it wrote, and keeps anything else written to your orders since then

## Map

- A unit whose MOVE the game will refuse because of water now stays where it is in the month-end view, instead of appearing in the water hex and leaving its neighbours looking short of silver
- Fixed one of your own units that died in battle staying on the map as a stale unit in the hex where it fell, and games you already have are cleaned up when you open them
- The moving water on the map now uses only a small share of your computer's processor instead of a whole core; it moves at the same speed, in slightly coarser steps

## Fetching your report

- On a New Age game, fetching a newer turn now asks whether to keep the turn on screen or open the new one, instead of replacing it straight away
- The questions asked when a fetch brings the same turn again, or a newer one, now say exactly what is replaced and that the orders you typed are kept

## Battle simulator

- Armies you export now also open in the New Age battle simulator at [atlantis-newage.com/simulator](https://atlantis-newage.com/simulator), and the same file still works in atlantis.pekle.lv
