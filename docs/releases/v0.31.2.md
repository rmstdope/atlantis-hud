What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.31.1 -> v0.31.2)
========================================================

## Gates

- With the Gates badge on, the map now marks every hex where your report shows a gate (reported on Discord)
- A gate you have seen once stays on the map on later turns, after your Gate Lore mage has moved on (reported on Discord)
- Selecting a hex with a gate shows a Gate section in the Region pane, with the gate's number, just above Exits
- Find any gate you know of from the command palette: type "gate" for all of them, or part of a gate's number, and choose one to jump the map to it

## TRANSPORT and DISTRIBUTE in Trident and Arcanum

- A quartermaster's TRANSPORT to a unit within 2 hexes that is not a quartermaster is no longer warned about and refused; it is shown as delivered, as the rules allow (reported on Discord)
- A TRANSPORT to a nearby quartermaster who owns no Caravanserai is no longer wrongly refused
- DISTRIBUTE now works exactly like TRANSPORT, with the same warnings and the same forecast
