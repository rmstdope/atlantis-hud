What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.31.0 -> v0.31.1)
========================================================

## Magic

- Fixed a mage who CASTs and STUDYs a skill in the same turn, such as Summon Wind, being wrongly warned that the study is blocked by the skill it depends on (reported in [#1847](https://github.com/rmstdope/atlantis-hud/issues/1847))

## TRANSPORT and DISTRIBUTE

- The Silver and Items hover now says the same as Problems for every refused TRANSPORT or DISTRIBUTE, including one of goods an earlier GIVE already handed away
- A refused TRANSPORT of an exact amount the unit no longer holds now says it moves nothing, instead of naming silver that would stay with a unit that has none
