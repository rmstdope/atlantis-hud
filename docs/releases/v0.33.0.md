What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.32.0 -> v0.33.0)
========================================================

## TRANSPORT and DISTRIBUTE

- A quartermaster's TRANSPORT of an item it holds none of now draws on sharing faction-mates in the hex, as the game does: the Items hover shows the goods leaving the sharer, passing through the quartermaster and arriving at the receiver
- Goods a quartermaster ships from a sharer are now charged their shipping fee in its Silver forecast, and when the hex cannot pay the fee the goods are shown staying unsent with the quartermaster
- A sharer's Items hover now says when the goods it lent were not sent because the shipping fee could not be paid, and why
- A quartermaster's Items hover shows goods it only passed through as "none → none" instead of "gone"
- A TRANSPORT ALL of an item the sender holds none of now warns you when its sharers hold some, since only an exact amount draws on them
- A TRANSPORT or DISTRIBUTE of an item the game will not ship, such as AMTS, now warns you, and the forecast keeps the item with the sender
- When a unit is short of silver, the Silver hover now names each order that is short with its own amount, instead of blaming the unit's study for a TRANSPORT's shipping fee

## Exporting orders

- Fixed exported orders still holding orders for a unit that has died since those orders were written
- Exported orders now put every unit under the hex it is in this turn, even when the orders you loaded were written before it moved

## Writing orders

- Fixed selecting a unit changing its orders when you had typed nothing, e.g. "@study OBSE" becoming "@STUDY OBSE", and marking them as unsaved

## Study planner

- Put into my orders now also writes every later turn of a mage's planned studies as TURN blocks, so your mages keep studying by the plan even if you miss sending orders for a turn
- Fixed the Put into my orders confirmation quoting an earlier write's comment in the order it replaces
