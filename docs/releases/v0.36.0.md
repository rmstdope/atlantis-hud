What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.35.0 -> v0.36.0)
========================================================

## Writing orders

- When your orders hold an ATTACK, an "attacks" button appears beside the battles one and opens an Attacks window showing which of your units would join each attack, and whether in the Frontline or the Backline (reported in [#2031](https://github.com/rmstdope/atlantis-hud/issues/2031))
- The Attacks window lists the units an AVOID, HOLD or NOAID keeps out of the fight, with the reason for each, including units in neighbouring hexes that could come to help
- The Attacks window warns you about a unit in the wrong place: melee standing behind, or archers, mages, tacticians and unarmed units in the Frontline
- The Attacks window follows your orders as you type, and clicking a unit in it takes you to that unit
- Frontline, Backline and Will not fight each sit in a card of their own, and every unit is one row with its warnings and reasons kept beside it
- Tick "Compact view" in the Attacks window to fit every unit on one line, with the full text shown when you hover over it; the setting is remembered

## Web version

- The web version is now only at [atlantis-hud.rmstdope.eu](https://atlantis-hud.rmstdope.eu); atlantis-hud.kurelid.se stays at v0.35.0 and gets no further updates
