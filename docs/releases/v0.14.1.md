What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.14.1)
============================================

## Your units list

- The new Move column shows whether each unit is overloaded, walking, riding or
  flying, while the unit panel explains its load and each available capacity
- Interface size can now be reduced to 50%, with additional choices at 75% and
  the existing range through 200%

## Planning movement

- Goods acquired with WITHDRAW now count toward a unit's movement load, so an
  order that makes the unit too heavy is warned before an impossible MOVE
- A fleet that is overloaded when it sails stays warned even when later
  manufacturing would consume enough heavy materials to lighten it

## Writing orders

- BUILD warnings now use the materials and workers left after transfers,
  purchases and recruiting instead of what the unit held at the start of the turn
- Recruiting before STUDY now applies the new race's skill ceiling, so mixed units
  are warned when the skill they ordered cannot advance
- GUARD 1 after the earlier AVOID phase now leaves the unit guarding regardless
  of which line appears first, avoiding false warnings that the guard was dropped

## Maintenance

- Grain, livestock, fish and meals now each cover 50 silver of maintenance, as
  the New Origins rules state, correcting the 30-silver value used in v0.14.0
