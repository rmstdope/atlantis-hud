What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.25.1 -> v0.25.2)
========================================================

## Your faction's silver

- In the faction dropdown, see how much of your unclaimed silver this turn's orders are expected to use, how much will remain, and a breakdown of CLAIM, WITHDRAW and maintenance.
- Fixed a bug where a quartermaster's DISTRIBUTE order could leave the sender's or receiver's final Silver calculation wrong.

## About

- Fixed a bug where About still said a new version was ready after you had refreshed, and Reload to update did nothing.
