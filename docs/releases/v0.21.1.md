What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.21.1)
============================================

## Map

- A hex that guards stopped one of your units from entering is now ringed in red and hatched over, so you can see where you were turned back without rereading the turn report
- The mark carries the guarding unit's number at the foot of the hex, e.g. `7235`, and `7235 +1` when more than one guard turned your units back there
- A hex where guards stopped only your ships is marked the same way and labelled `guards`, because the report never says which unit stopped a ship
- Selecting a marked hex gives you a "Blocked" section in the Region panel, naming each guard and every unit it kept out, with your stopped ships listed last
- The mark belongs to the turn it happened in: loading another turn shows that turn's blocks and nothing is carried forward
- "Blocked" has its own entry in the map's Badges menu, just after "Guard", on by default and remembered like the rest
