What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.27.0 -> v0.28.0)
========================================================

## Silver and SHARE

- Silver a sharing unit lends to a faction-mate's BUY or STUDY now leaves that sharer's SILVER column, even when the borrower receives silver later in the month
- A unit that borrows from its region's shared silver now shows the loan in its own SILVER column, so a STUDY or BUY that sharing pays for ends at 0 instead of a red negative
- A sharing unit's silver now pays for STUDY and other month-long orders in the region where its month ends, not in the region it leaves
- Fixed a bug where a STUDY or BUY that could not be paid when it runs got no warning because silver arriving later in the month, by TRANSPORT or wages, left the unit in credit at month end
- Fixed a bug where giving away more silver than a unit holds got no warning when silver reached it later in the month
- A GIVE or TAKE of more silver than the unit holds now moves only what it has, as the game does, so the receiving unit's silver shows what it will really get
- The region's not-enough-silver message now gives the right "can have" and "spend" figures when silver is given to a sharing unit, or when sharing units walk in or out

## Building and production

- Fixed a bug where builders were warned they had neither wood nor stone although a sharing unit brings wood into their region this turn (reported on Discord)
- Wood, stone and other material a sharing unit brings into a region this turn now counts for PRODUCE there, and leaves the sharing unit's own row once it is used

## Movement and the map

- A unit whose MOVE is replaced by a later month-long order, such as MOVE N then STUDY, or leaders with MOVE then TEACH, now stays where it is on the map, in the month-end preview and when measuring TRANSPORT distances, including units formed this month

## New units

- Fixed a bug where a new unit that moved into the region where another new unit was formed showed that other unit's long order and silver (reported on Discord)
