What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.19.0)
=============================================

## New Age: Trident games

- A game's ruleset is chosen when you create it, and the orders editor and command palette suggest only that ruleset's orders, so a Trident game offers CAPITAL, CREATE, EXPLORE and QUEST
- Hills, tunnels, grottoes, deep forests and chasms now have their own look on the map instead of all showing as unknown terrain
- Text after a semicolon in an order is now treated as a comment, as Trident reads it, instead of as part of the order
- BUILD uses the material you name, and stone before wood when you name none
- Builders now take shared materials before producers do, in the order Trident settles them
- Leaders are charged their real 90 silver of upkeep, and the Count upkeep setting quotes the right fee
- A leader whose own food covers only part of the fee is no longer shown as owing nothing
- Fleets can sail across lakes, and walking routes no longer cross them
- Construction no longer spends materials at a site where Trident forbids the building or already has one
- Call Pirates is no longer offered as a skill to study or teach
- The Study planner shows whether teaching another faction's mage earns the teaching bonus, which depends on that faction declaring you Friendly, and assumes an ally has unless its report says otherwise

## Shipping goods

- A TRANSPORT to a quartermaster too far away is listed with the other problems, and the goods stay with the sender
- Long shipments are priced, and the unit's silver breakdown shows what the shipping cost
- A sender that cannot pay the shipping cost ships nothing, and is told so; a CLAIM that covers the bill clears the warning
- When the distance cannot be worked out, the figures are marked as uncertain instead of guessed
- A shipment to a quartermaster on another level of the map is refused with a message that says why
- Shipments are measured from where both units stand after this month's moves
- Turning off Settings > Warnings > Transport lets the forecast deliver the shipment as ordered

## Movement and the map

- A unit that can swim shows its Swim capacity beside Fly, Ride and Walk, and can cross coastal water without a ship
- Routes through an inner passage stop at the structure and say why, and once you have been through, carry on where the passage comes out
- Hovering a passage ring explains the passage and names where it leads
- Several MOVE lines for one unit now chain into one route instead of the last one replacing the rest
- Fleets are no longer routed straight through a neck of land without a canal
- A fleet can land on a shore you have only seen from the sea
- The route planner no longer proposes sailing a fleet that is already overloaded
- A fleet's route now follows its owner's orders rather than a passenger's
- Fixed a bug where a new unit's path started in the wrong hex when several hexes formed a unit with the same NEW number

## Writing orders

- The order editor completes building and ship names after BUILD
- A BUILD naming a building or ship the game does not have is marked as an error

## Units

- Fixed a bug where a unit that was killed, disbanded or otherwise gone from the latest turn's reports still appeared as if it were there

## Study planning

- You can insert or remove a turn in a mage's study plan, and later turns move along to make room
