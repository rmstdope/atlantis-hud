What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.17.0)
=============================================

## Loading reports

- Desktop: You can fetch this turn's New Origins report directly from the server, without opening the browser first
- Desktop: Fetching or sending now asks for your password when it needs it, then gets out of your way
- A map you export from AtlaClient can now be added to your map

## Units table and reports

- A battle fought in a hex now leaves a badge on that hex in the map
- A unit line that was cut short in your report is clearly marked, and its missing counts are shown as floors rather than facts
- Figures that could not be read from a unit's report are shown as not known instead of guessed
- When another unread unit may share a tax, wage, entertainment, or market pool, the affected earnings are shown as “at most”
- A hex's structure rows are now named from the hex they belong to

## Silver and orders

- Your units' monthly silver now follows the actual order sequence, so sales, gifts, takes, purchases, production, spells, and pillage affect the right balance
- A unit borrowing from its faction-mates now shows which neighbouring money paid for it
- A contested taxer's purchases and spells are limited to the tax share it can actually collect
- Buying from a shared market uses your unit's settled income instead of money it only hopes to receive
- A refused withdrawal, recruitment, sale, or spell no longer changes the unit's forecast as though it succeeded
- Giving to an unavailable unit and taking from yourself now warn you instead of silently forecasting money or items that will not move
- Taking another unit's silver, including `TAKE ... ALL SILV`, is now reflected in both units' month-end figures
- Item lists no longer mix silver in with goods, so the Silver column is the single clear account of your money
- A market order that cannot be named by the market no longer takes a share from units that can use it

## Study planning

- You can set up a teaching mage before there is an eligible pupil, then keep its pupils current as you plan
- The study planner now distinguishes mages from apprentices and shows all of a mage's known skills
- A teacher's doubled study month is shown in the study choices and forecasts
- A mage's note saves as you type and is preserved when you reopen the study planner
- Study forecasts no longer charge or mark progress for a skill already at its maximum level
- A mage moving into a fort is warned when it would actually remain in the open

## Moving fleets

- A sailing step between land hexes, or with an invalid direction, is now explained before you send it
- Flying fleets are no longer incorrectly refused inland hexes
