What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.29.0 -> v0.30.0)
========================================================

## Magic planner

- Each planned turn in the Schedule can now also name the spell your mage casts or readies for battle that turn, picked from a dropdown under his study
- The Schedule now counts the 5 days of practice a planned cast gives, and shows when those days go to a foundation skill instead, or are lost because the spell is already at its highest level
- Put into my orders now also writes each mage's planned CAST or COMBAT line, behind an "Also write the casts" checkbox that starts ticked

## Fleets and passengers

- Fixed a false "only the owner can set its course" warning when a ship's owner LEAVEs and another unit ENTERs and SAILs it; the new owner is now worked out after LEAVE and ENTER, as the game does (reported in [#1491](https://github.com/rmstdope/atlantis-hud/issues/1491))
- A unit that cannot swim or fly now stays aboard when it writes LEAVE at sea, everywhere in the app, instead of being shown going ashore
- A LEAVE written at sea by a unit the game will not let off the ship now gets a warning on that line saying why

## Writing orders

- Fixed a bug where a second TEACH line added below an existing one was ignored by the teaching checks; every TEACH line now adds its students and gets their warnings
