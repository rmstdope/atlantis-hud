What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.31.0 -> v0.32.0)
========================================================

## Writing orders

- Export orders now tells you how many errors your orders still have and asks you to confirm before saving: Export anyway saves them as they are, Show errors takes you to the first one, and orders with no errors save straight away as before
- A NAME or DESCRIBE whose words are not in double quotes is now warned, since the game keeps only the first word, and an Add quotes button in the hover fixes the line for you
- A NAME or DESCRIBE with an opening double quote but no closing one now says what the game will do with it, and a Close the quote button in the hover fixes it
- Fixed the orders editor turning ordinary words in a description or name into capitals, e.g. "of" becoming "OF" in DESCRIBE UNIT Outlaw of Sherwood

## Units

- Fixed a unit whose description ends in "?" showing no skills, and its orders being warned that it lacked skills it has (reported on Discord)
- The Unit panel now shows a unit's description under its faction, for your own units and other factions' alike, and a DESCRIBE order shows the description it will leave

## Gates

- With the Gates badge on, the map now marks every hex where your report shows a gate (reported on Discord)
- A gate you have seen once stays on the map on later turns, after your Gate Lore mage has moved on (reported on Discord)
- Selecting a hex with a gate shows a Gate section in the Region pane, with the gate's number, just above Exits
- Find any gate you know of from the command palette: type "gate" for all of them, or part of a gate's number, and choose one to jump the map to it

## TRANSPORT and DISTRIBUTE

- In Trident and Arcanum, a quartermaster's TRANSPORT to a unit within 2 hexes that is not a quartermaster is no longer warned about and refused; it is shown as delivered, as the rules allow (reported on Discord)
- In Trident and Arcanum, a TRANSPORT to a nearby quartermaster who owns no Caravanserai is no longer wrongly refused, and DISTRIBUTE now works exactly like TRANSPORT
- The Silver and Items hover now says the same as Problems for every refused TRANSPORT or DISTRIBUTE, including one of goods an earlier GIVE already handed away
- A refused TRANSPORT of an exact amount the unit no longer holds now says it moves nothing, instead of naming silver that would stay with a unit that has none

## Magic

- Fixed a mage who CASTs and STUDYs a skill in the same turn, such as Summon Wind, being wrongly warned that the study is blocked by the skill it depends on (reported in [#1847](https://github.com/rmstdope/atlantis-hud/issues/1847))
