What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.26.2 -> v0.26.3)
========================================================

## Magic

- The magic planner now opens straight on its Planning view
- In the magic planner, a turn's selector closes without changes when you click that turn again, click anywhere else or press Escape
- You can walk the magic study tree with the arrow keys and press Enter to pick a skill, just as if you had clicked it
- Fixed a bug where a mage aboard a Galleon in New Origins was estimated to study at half rate above level 2, although a Galleon gives one mage room to study there

## Rulesets

- You can now change a game's ruleset after creating it; if the new ruleset does not know some of the items, skills, buildings, ships, races or terrains in your reports, you are shown which before you switch
- When you open a report that names something your game's ruleset does not know, a warning lists it and lets you switch ruleset, or report the gap on GitHub or the game's Discord

## Game data (F2)

- A new Terrains category lets you look up your world's terrains: what it costs to move in, what can be found there and which races can be recruited
- Game data now opens on a new All category that lists everything at once

## Map and movement

- Lakes now look different from the open sea in every map theme, while still reading as water
- Fixed a bug where a unit given horses in the same turn it handed men to another unit was wrongly warned that it was overloaded and would not move

## Writing orders

- Pressing Enter on a suggestion that finishes the order now ends the line and takes you to a new one, instead of leaving a trailing space
