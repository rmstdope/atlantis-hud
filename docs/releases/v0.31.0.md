What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.30.0 -> v0.31.0)
========================================================

## Writing orders

- The orders editor no longer shrinks to about one line when its warning list appears, and the list's "N more ↓" count no longer covers the message above it
- Warnings on lines inside a FORM block now show on those lines in the forming unit's editor and in Problems, and clicking the FORM line's tag selects exactly the words the problem is about
- A newly formed unit is named once, saying who formed it, e.g. "New unit 1 (formed by 683)", instead of "Unit (new 1) (new-1)"
- You are now warned when you order WORK in a region where work earns nothing, such as an ocean hex (reported in [#1800](https://github.com/rmstdope/atlantis-hud/issues/1800))
- You are now warned when a unit SELLs something it does not hold or more than it holds, ENTERTAINs without the entertainment skill, or QUESTs where there is no Town Hall
- A STUDY the unit cannot afford is now warned on its own order line
- EXCHANGEs now settle in the order the game runs them, and one the game will abort is warned on both units' lines, saying why
- A GIVE 0 that discards a sharing faction-mate's goods is now warned, and the faction-mate is told who discarded them
- A WITHDRAW or CLAIM that leaves faction-mates unable to pay upkeep is now warned on that line, and an over-claim is warned only on the CLAIM lines that go unpaid
- A lone unit's SELL ALL of more than the market wants is no longer warned every turn
- Manipulation is treated as the ordinary skill it is: it stays out of the magic study planner, and studying it past level 2 is flagged as gaining nothing (reported in [#1554](https://github.com/rmstdope/atlantis-hud/issues/1554))
- Ocean hexes are no longer flagged as unguarded, and GUARD 1 at sea is now refused for the right reason
- A BUY that would mix leaders and normal men in one unit is now shown refused, with no silver spent and no men recruited
- When your faction PRODUCEs, TAXes or PILLAGEs in more regions than it may, the forecast drops what the game refuses and each refused unit is told so on its own line
- In an Arcanum game, CREATE VILLAGE is no longer called an unknown order
- Hover and warning sentences that misnamed things - a shortfall called the cost, an apprentice called a mage, "livestocks", a GIVE UNIT treated as goods - now say what is happening

## Your units' silver

- A red Silver figure now shows plainly how much the unit is short, e.g. "8650 short", and a narrow column never cuts it into a figure that reads as a different number
- Faction-mates who SHARE now pay each other's STUDY, BUY, GIVE and TRANSPORT costs the way the game does, so units are no longer called short of silver a sharer covers, or shown spending silver nobody had
- When the shared purse can pay only part of the month's costs, it now pays students one by one and cuts a BUY or GIVE to what it can fund, as the game does
- One sharing unit whose month cannot be counted no longer makes the whole hex's silver uncountable and turns every faction-mate red
- The Silver hover now names who lent silver to whom, and for which order, including loans to and from a fleet that sails
- A unit whose STUDY or CAST is refused is no longer charged the fee, nor shown with all its upkeep paid by a faction-mate
- A hex's not-enough-silver warning now sits on the order the game actually refuses, its figures add up, and a unit that moves away is counted where it ends the month rather than in the hex it leaves
- A GIVE to a unit that is not in the hex no longer takes anything from the giver, and a chain of GIVEs now resolves in the game's order, with a warning where a gift falls short

## Markets, wages and taxes

- When units compete for a market, a tax base, wages, entertainment or production, the shares now add up to the whole, instead of the hex trading one less than the game
- A BUY larger than the market line or the unit's purse no longer squeezes out other buyers, a BUY beside a BUY ALL of the same goods competes for their sum, and the share sentence counts all of a unit's BUY lines
- A unit selling more than it holds into a contested market is no longer shown selling more than it has

## TRANSPORT and DISTRIBUTE

- A TRANSPORT to a unit that is not a quartermaster owning a transport structure is now shown refused and warned at the order
- A DISTRIBUTE from a unit that is not a quartermaster, owns no Caravanserai, or reaches beyond its range is now shown refused, and the warning blames the sender rather than the target
- A quartermaster that leaves, moves out of or hands over its transport structure no longer goes on receiving TRANSPORT and DISTRIBUTE
- A TRANSPORT of more silver than the unit will have is now warned instead of quietly cut, and a refused TRANSPORT or DISTRIBUTE of ALL silver now reads the same in the hover as in Problems

## Ships, buildings and PROMOTE

- When a unit writes several PROMOTEs, the owner is now forecast from the last one, as the game does, and a structure is never handed on twice in one month
- You are now warned when a PROMOTE will not run: written together with LEAVE, naming a unit not in the structure, or PROMOTE FACTION 0 NEW
- EVICT is now followed: the evicted unit is shown outside the structure everywhere
- A unit that will not move, or a fleet that will not sail because it is overloaded or short of sailors, is shown staying put on the map, in the units list and in the silver forecast
- A sailing passenger's STUDY is now paid from the hex its fleet lands in, and the passenger is told on its STUDY line when that hex cannot pay
- A newly formed unit now takes BEHIND, AVOID and REVEAL written above its FORM, and stands ahead of units that ENTER its structure, as the game does

## Spells, production and food

- A CAST the game will refuse now leaves the mage's silver and materials where they were and shows nothing made, and a CAST never uses up material nobody had
- Cooks now show how many meals they make, instead of a Silver "?" that spread to their faction-mates
- BUILD of a new ship now forecasts the wood it uses and the ship it starts
- Food for upkeep is eaten in the game's order - meals, then grain, livestock and fish - instead of fish first
- The Items hover now shows goods a sharing unit passed on and whose shared materials a producer or builder used, and no longer says "Nothing this month changes these." right after listing this month's changes

## Units table

- A studying unit's SKILLS cell now shows its skills as they will be next month, still marked in yellow
- Type an item into the units filter to find the units carrying it, e.g. LIVE for livestock
- The Long order column shows the month-long order that will actually run, knows EXPLORE, and drops a STUDY that a MOVE replaces
- After merging another faction's report of the same turn, that faction's units show what its report says about them, instead of "not disclosed"
- The Unit panel no longer shows a negative weight after the unit gives silver away
- The Add to army list can be reached in full with many armies, opens wherever you click its button, and no longer hides behind a unit's hover card

## Command palette

- Find other factions' units seen this turn, towns and cities by name, factions by name or number, your named Armies, and your hex and mage notes
- Import, Fetch, Send and export a mage sheet straight from the palette
- Look up any order: the game data (F2) has a new Orders tab describing every order of your game's rules

## Map

- Hex coordinates are now printed on every known hex, on by default and switched off under Grid in the Badges menu (reported on Discord)

## Layout and help

- The Getting around guide (Ctrl/Cmd+/) now lists every control the app has
- Links in the game data dialog now look like links
- With many games, the Games menu fits the window, and in a short window the orders panel can still be resized and the Plan move button stays inside the right rail

## Reading your turn report

- Fixed the game header showing a turn number one lower than the game's own, e.g. Turn 83 for a December Year 7 report
- A report whose events mention a hex before your faction's details no longer loses your declared attitudes and unclaimed silver
