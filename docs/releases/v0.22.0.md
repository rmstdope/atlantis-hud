What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.22.0)
============================================

## Map and finding units

- Units remembered from an older report now show their new faction after they have been transferred, rather than their former one
- Underworld hexes no longer appear on the surface map
- Search the command palette for units, buildings, ships, and other map objects from older reports; each result tells you when it was last seen and takes you to its last-known hex

## Fleet movement

- After finishing a ship and having its builder leave, the sailor taking over can sail it without an incorrect warning

## Your units' silver

- When you send silver with TRANSPORT or DISTRIBUTE, the Silver column now moves it correctly between the sending and receiving units (New Origins rules/transport)
