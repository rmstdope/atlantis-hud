What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.18.0)
=============================================

## Map

- Biome textures now vary subtly in rotation and brightness from hex to hex, so the world feels less tiled
- Lake hexes now use the same water colour and texture as oceans
- Ocean and lake textures can flow in the direction of their own texture, and Settings lets you pause the movement or rotation separately

## Study planning

- The study planner now opens on an Overview, where mages are listed in unit-number order with clearer, roomier skill summaries
- The Planner keeps its headings readable while you scroll and clearly highlights the mage and turn you are examining
- A mage planning advanced magic study without shelter now receives a clear yellow warning
- Study and teaching choices now make selected skills, projected outcomes, and available actions easier to read
- Generated study orders are grouped into clearer faction sections, with write, save, copy, and undo results easier to follow

## Dialogs and settings

- The Game Data, Magic Study Tree, Study Planner, and Settings windows have clearer headings, controls, selections, and empty states
- Settings has more room for its tabs and groups the biome texture, rotation, and water-animation controls together
- Starting a new game now has clearer map choices, form fields, errors, and primary actions
