What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.24.0)
============================================

## Your units' food and upkeep

- The Items column now shows the food your units will have left after upkeep, and hovering it tells you the food was eaten for upkeep
- Fish your units catch at sea now counts as food for upkeep under CONSUME FACTION, so fishing crews are no longer warned about upkeep their catch will pay
- When the upkeep shortfall warning appears, it now names the units or regions that are short and by how much, so you can see where the total comes from
- Ships sailing into the same hex now share silver for upkeep there, so a ship with no silver is no longer warned when another ship arriving alongside it carries enough
- Units that walk or sail into another hex now share silver and food with the units they end the month beside, instead of the ones they left behind
- A unit set to CONSUME FACTION that moves away is no longer fed from grain it left behind, and the units that stay are no longer fed from food a mover carried off
- A unit that ships its food away with TRANSPORT now pays its upkeep in silver, instead of being shown eating food it no longer has
