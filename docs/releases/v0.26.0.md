What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.25.0 -> v0.26.0)
========================================================

## Map

- Every one of the 17 biomes now has its own colour, so forest, swamp and jungle are easy to tell apart even when zoomed far out
- Choose how biomes are drawn under Settings → All games → Biome textures: Standard (the default), Shapes, which gives each biome its own large shapes, or Painted, a hand-picked picture for each biome
- Switch on biome symbols from the Badges menu to read the terrain from small shapes in each hex, without relying on colour
- Five new map themes: Collective, Chronicle, Blueprint, Stained Glass and 8-Bit Quest
- A unit's movement line now glows and curves through the hexes it passes, with a comet running along it that you can switch off or speed up and slow down in Settings
- Trade routes are drawn as a silver caravan between towns, with beacons and price tags
- Roads are drawn as a network of milestones in every theme, and unfinished roads are dotted
- A new Unvisited hexes switch, under Staleness in Settings, controls how hexes you have never visited are dimmed, so turning Staleness off no longer seems to do nothing

## Dialogs

- Drag any dialog by its top bar to move it out of the way and see the map behind it
- Game data (F2), Magic tree (F3), Study planner (F4) and Help now open one at a time, close with their own key, Escape or a click outside, and dim the map the same amount

## Your units' silver

- The faction dropdown now shows how much unclaimed silver this turn's orders are expected to use, and what will be left
- Silver a quartermaster sends with DISTRIBUTE, including to a unit formed this turn, is now counted in both units' final silver
- Fixed a bug where the quartermaster's silver hover credited one unit with silver that other units had sent

## Updates

- The app now tells you when a new version is out, with a small mark on the Settings button and the About tab; on desktop it checks at startup and then every hour
- Fixed a bug in the web app where About's Reload to update button did nothing when a new version was ready
