What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.24.2)
=============================================

## Writing orders

- New units formed and moved in the same turn now appear at their destination, including a destination the map already knows about, with their existing orders ready to inspect and edit
- In Trident games, FACTION now correctly refuses an allocation that leaves either Martial or Magic with zero points

## Your units' silver

- The Silver column now divides a short shared food supply between CONSUME FACTION units in the same order as the game, including after food is produced and sent away, instead of leaving their result unknown or counting food that has left

## Your units' items

- An item a unit produces and uses up in the same turn still appears in its hover, with an explanation of the order that leaves none at month end
