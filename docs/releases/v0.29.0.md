What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.28.0 -> v0.29.0)
========================================================

## Units in hex

- Unfinished buildings and unfinished ships now carry a small dashed mark, and the mark drops in the month-end view when this month's BUILD finishes them (reported on Discord)
- Fixed a bug where an ENTER of a structure the hex does not have showed the unit inside it with no warning; the order is now warned and the unit stays where it is

## Silver and SHARE

- Wages now help pay for shipping goods, and silver arriving later in the month is no longer counted toward spends that happen earlier
- A SELL written below a BUY now pays for it, and a sharing unit's sale can now fund a faction-mate's BUY
- GIVE, CAST and SELL now draw on sharing faction-mates' silver the same way every other spend does
- Fixed a bug where an EXCHANGE was shown as short of silver although a sharing faction-mate covers it
- When several CLAIMs together ask for more unclaimed silver than you have, they are now filled in the order your orders list them, as the game does
- Silver you CLAIM and give away in the same turn now actually moves in the preview
- Fixed a bug where silver given to a new unit that forms with nobody vanished instead of going where the game sends it
- A recruiting unit's study fee and entertainment are now priced on its headcount and skill after recruiting
- Two BUY lines for different items are now each priced correctly, instead of each as if the other had not been paid for
- A CAST that creates an artifact now charges its inputs once per cast, not once per item made
- A newly formed unit now spends and lends at the end of its former's structure, as the game does, instead of after every unit in the hex
- A quartermaster can now forward goods it received from another quartermaster in the same month, and its own study is no longer paid from silver that only reaches it later by TRANSPORT

## Items and goods

- EXCHANGE between two of your own units now swaps their goods in the preview
- CREATE VILLAGE now uses up the 1000 people and 100 wagons it founds the village with
- QUEST and EXPLORE now take the tokens and maps they hand in
- A STEAL now shows the stolen item as uncertain on the thief, since the attempt may fail
- A new unit recruited with BUY PEASANTS no longer shows as dissolving, and keeps the goods you give it
- Goods an empty new unit was given or bought now go to your faction's first unit when it dissolves, instead of vanishing
- Fixed a bug where goods given to a unit and then used by another unit's BUILD vanished from the preview without a word

## Building and production

- Several units building the same structure are now priced against the work left between them, not each against all of it
- A BUILD no longer uses output a manufacturer in the same region only produces later in the month
- Resources and wages you share with another faction's producers are no longer forecast as yours alone
- In New Age, a BUILD of a structure you stand inside while it is unfinished is now priced as finishing it, not as founding a new one
- A DESTROY that only damages a structure no longer counts it as gone when pricing BUILD or other month-long orders
- A unit that ENTERs or LEAVEs a structure now has its month-long orders judged from where it moved to

## Villages

- A CREATE VILLAGE that will not found a village now says so in Problems, with the reason: an existing settlement nearby or in the region, the wrong terrain, or too few people or wagons
- A CREATE VILLAGE too close to another settlement no longer uses up its founders in the preview
- Of two CREATE VILLAGEs ordered close to each other in one month, only the one the game runs first is now shown founding

## Fleets and passengers

- A sailing passenger's BUILD and PRODUCE are now judged where its fleet lands, and a refused BUILD names that region rather than saying "here"
- A SAIL that is refused because both hexes are land now leaves the fleet, its crew and its passengers where they are, instead of showing them arriving

## Writing orders

- BUY PEASANT, PEASANTS and PEAS no longer get a "no item" warning, and are offered as completions in BUY
- With Order OCD on, the keyword NEW is now uppercased like other keywords
