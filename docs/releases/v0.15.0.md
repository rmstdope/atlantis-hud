What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.15.0)
============================================

## Sharing mages with your allies

- Export a mage sheet from the Export menu and mail it to an ally: every one of
  your mages, written in the game's own report syntax so it can be read without
  a client
- Opening or dropping a sheet an ally sent you takes their mages in, rather than
  trying to load it as your own turn report, and tells you about any mage it left
  out
- A chip beside your faction shows whose sheets you hold, how many mages each
  has and how many turns old it is, and lets you forget one

## The units pane

- Choose which columns the units table draws, from a Columns tab in Settings —
  Movement, Skills, Items, Structure and long order among them
- A Flags column showing each unit's flags as a compact run of letters
- The pane opens sorted by Id, and falls back to Id when you clear a sort
- Two units in different hexes that you both numbered FORM 1 now get a row each,
  instead of sharing one, and selecting one of them no longer highlights the
  other

## Units you form this month

- Selecting a unit your FORM orders create shows that unit's own orders, and
  editing them writes back inside its FORM block rather than its creator's
- A unit that recruits nobody keeps its row, marked as dissolving, and its silver
  and goods go back to your first unit in the region — the way the game does it
- A new unit inherits its parent's avoiding, behind, revealing and sharing flags,
  but not guard or autotax
- A gift to a unit your sibling forms arrives whichever order the two blocks are
  written in
- BUILD HELP NEW finds the unit you formed this month and helps it
- Units formed this month count towards your trade region and quartermaster
  allowances, so the warnings about exceeding them are no longer too generous
- FORM 0 is refused as an error instead of quietly making a unit that cannot exist
- The orders-import summary names a formed unit the way the rest of the app does,
  as "new 1" rather than "new-1"
- Silver and item figures no longer leak between two new units that share a number

## What your units will hold and spend

- Giving all your silver away is settled at the point in the turn the game settles
  it: a gift no longer spends tax income that arrives later, and no longer looks
  smaller than it will be because of a study charged after it
- A cast or a manufacture is priced against silver a gift has already handed over,
  so the Silver column stops showing you can afford something you cannot
- A BUY you cannot afford buys as many as your silver covers, instead of charging
  you for all of them
- A BUY in a hex where one of your units is sharing is funded from that shared
  silver, so a valid purchase is no longer reported as unaffordable
- Silver claimed from your unclaimed fund is settled in report order, so several
  units claiming at once can no longer be shown drawing more than the fund holds
- A spell is funded by the silver its unit holds when spells are cast — counting
  a claim that came in, and not counting a sale that has not happened yet
- Materials you buy or sell this month count towards what you can manufacture,
  and shared materials in the hex are drawn on before a BUILD takes them
- Study fees, production costs and upkeep are each measured against the silver
  available when they actually happen
- Goods you withdraw count towards what a unit is carrying when it moves; silver
  it spends afterwards does not lighten the load
- When a unit has two month-long orders, only the last one counts — the earlier
  one contributes no cost, no output and no warnings of its own
- A hex holding two units printed with the same number reads and charges each of
  them separately, instead of counting one twice

## Giving, taking and transporting

- A gift to another faction stays uncertain when you cannot tell whether they have
  declared you Friendly, rather than being shown as certain to arrive or certain
  to fail
- Competing GIVE and TAKE orders settle in report order, the way the game resolves
  them, rather than in the order you happened to write them
- TRANSPORT checks that the receiving unit can actually receive before moving
  anything, and says which requirement is missing when it cannot
- A chain of TRANSPORT orders routes through your quartermasters in the game's
  three phases, so goods received in one phase no longer move again in the same one
- Giving silver away happens before you spend at the market, whichever order the
  lines are in
- Unfinished ships can be given by name, and are discarded when their builder
  moves or their fleet sails — as the game discards them
- A mage can no longer be shown giving men away

## The map and your hexes

- The hex pane distinguishes an advanced resource that is definitely absent from
  one nobody has looked for: "0 floater hide" against "mushroom?"
- What one of your units discovered about a hex's advanced resources is remembered
  in later turns, even after that unit has moved on

## Writing and checking orders

- A unit that sets its share flag this turn is counted in the hex's sharing pool
  straight away, instead of only from next turn
- NAME accepts the spellings the game accepts — SHIP, VILLAGE and the other
  synonyms — instead of marking them invalid
- PRODUCE with a number produces that many, and its materials, cost and shortages
  are all counted, rather than the order being treated as opaque
- A unit sailing to another region has its production counted in the region it
  arrives in
- Teaching warnings count people who join the unit this month
- Only a single leader may begin studying a Foundation magic skill, and a unit that
  is already a mage can neither buy men nor be given them
- You can write orders for one of your units that the report's orders template
  never listed

## Playing an Atlantis New Age game

### This is a feature under development. It is not expected to work at this point.

Atlantis HUD now speaks to Atlantis New Age worlds directly, so a turn no longer
means downloading a file from the web site and uploading another one back.

## Planning your mages' studies

### This is a feature under development. It is not expected to work at this point.

A study planner of its own, opened with F4, and a way to see your allies' mages
beside your own.
