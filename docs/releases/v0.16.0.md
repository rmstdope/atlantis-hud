What's new for you in Atlantis HUD (v0.16.0)
============================================

## Hover any cell in the units table

Every column of the units table now explains itself. Rest the pointer on a cell
and a popup opens about that cell alone, saying what the figure was in your
report, what it will be once your orders run, and what moved it.

- A changed figure is written as the pair, `8 → 12`, coloured by direction, so
  you can see both ends of the month at once
- **Items** names every movement behind the change — bought, sold, withdrawn,
  given away, given to it, taken, produced, consumed as a production's material,
  spent on a build, cast, or transported in or out
- **Men** names every man who joined or left the unit and what moved them
- **Silver** says what the month does to the unit's silver and why the cell is
  red, with each amount attributed rather than lumped into one total
- **Skills** draws each skill's month as a chain and shows where this month's
  study lands, teaching included
- **Movement** shows the unit's load against each capacity it has, and what put
  it there
- **Flags** lists every flag, its state, and the order that changed it
- **Structure** names the structure the unit entered or left this month
- **Long order** says how this month's long order differs from the one in your
  report
- A cell nothing touched says so plainly, rather than leaving you wondering
  whether it was checked
- The popup replaces the old whole-unit tooltip, so what opens is always about
  the thing you are pointing at
- Hovering works again on a filtered list in **All my units** — resting on a row
  there previously opened nothing at all

## Flag orders

Seven flag orders were accepted, reported as valid, and then quietly ignored.
They now do what they say.

- `AUTOTAX`, `NOAID`, `HOLD`, `NOCROSS`, `REVEAL`, `CONSUME` and `SPOILS` all
  change the unit they are written on, and the Flags column shows it
- `REVEAL` and battle spoils are single settings rather than separate switches,
  so choosing one clears the others
- `GUARD 0` now actually clears a unit the report showed as on guard, instead of
  leaving the flag in place while the rest of the app believed it gone
- Setting Avoid Combat cancels Guard, and setting Guard cancels Avoid Combat, as
  the rules require
- Your silver forecast and the order warnings follow this month's flag orders too,
  so a unit that stops taxing is no longer credited with the tax

## Moving units

- A unit you create with `FORM` and then order to move now shows its start hex,
  its destination and its path on the map, like any other unit
- `MOVE OUT` and `MOVE <structure>` are followed, so the Structure column shows a
  unit boarding or leaving a building
- A unit that walks to another hex no longer claims to still be in the fort it
  left behind

## Your units

- A unit of centaurs that knows riding counts as combat ready — it can tax, it
  can guard, and it counts towards the men a `PILLAGE` needs
- Each hex remembers which unit you had selected in it, so moving between two or
  three hexes no longer throws your choice away every time you come back
- The unit pane lists that unit's events from the turn report, instead of sending
  you to the report popover to hunt for them
- A market purchase of men reads as "recruited 6" wherever it appears, rather than
  "recruited" in one place and "bought" in another
