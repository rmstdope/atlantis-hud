# ah-19l2.2 — retrospective

- **Implementer:** Storm
- **Date:** 2026-08-23
- **PR:** #615

## The plan's Validation section asserted a fixture fact that was not true, and it was the bead's whole point

**What happened.** The plan's *Validation* section required, by eye, that unit **12881
`Carpenters`** in `neworigins-3.0.0-g3-f42-t82.rep` "shows a non-zero **Expense** where it showed
none", and its *User-facing decisions* interview was conducted on the stated figure "Carpenters
shows **3000**, not 6000: materials allow 2 catapults, its 3000 silver pays for 1." Probing the real
fixture once the column was built gave `expense=Some(0) produced=0 wanted=2 cap=Some(Silver)
held=0`: the unit holds **no silver of its own** — it is a `sharing` unit and its faction's silver
sits elsewhere — so under the plan's own chosen rule (cap the run against what the unit *holds*) it
makes zero catapults and spends zero. The bead's headline claim, that "the last invisible
expenditure in the corpus becomes visible", was on course to ship with the corpus's own motivating
unit showing nothing at all.

**Why.** The interview figure and the fixture were never checked against each other. The plan
derives 3000 from the recipe and from the unit's men, both of which it read correctly from the
report; the unit's *silver holding* is the one input it did not read, and it is the input the chosen
rule turns on.

**Cost.** About fifteen minutes: a scratch probe test against the fixture, then a judgement about
`produced_name`, which the plan documented as `None` "when the unit produces nothing". Read as "when
a cap left it making none" that nulls the name exactly where the hover most needs it and the case
stays silent; read as "when there is no PRODUCE order" the hover says *"This unit has silver for 0
catapults, not the 2 its men could make."* and the expenditure does become visible. I took the
second reading and said so in the PR, since the alternative defeats the bead. No CI cycle was lost —
the probe was deleted before the gate.

**Prevent by.** A plan whose *Validation* names a specific unit in a specific fixture should state
the inputs its expected figure depends on, read off that fixture — here the unit's `SILV` holding
beside its headcount — rather than only the derived figure. `plan-bead`'s validation section is
where that belongs. The cheap version: any figure quoted in a *User-facing decisions* interview that
comes from a fixture is worth one `grep` of the fixture before it is put to the navigator, because
that figure is what the navigator is choosing between.

**Seen before.** `ah-cg1` (a plan asserted two queries were equivalent; two of six were not) and
`ah-ywj7` (the plan's own `jq` snippet was inert against the live database). Same shape each time: a
plan states a fact about real data that nobody ran before the implementer did.
