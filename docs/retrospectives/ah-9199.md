## A fix for "unknown skill" first shipped as "any skill the catalogue lacks"

**What happened.** The first version of the fix took "a STUDY naming a skill `Ruleset::find_skill`
cannot find" as a typo. It warned about the skill, dropped the STUDY, and showed a confident silver
figure. The cold-read review found that the committed corpus's one such STUDY, `@study BRTL` in
g3-f42-t82, is a real skill. Unit 683 holds blasphemous ritual [BRTL] 1, lists it under Can Study,
and the game ran the order. `pnpm run atlantis data BRTL` finds nothing: the data page, and so the
shipped catalogue, does not list every skill the game has. The one sign in the gate was the
corpus test `silver_agrees_with_the_warning`, whose "at least one Doubted unit" floor went red. I
read that as the floor being out of date and moved it into a synthetic test, instead of asking which
unit had stopped being doubted and why.

**Why.** The bead's wording ("a skill name that does not exist") was read as "not in the catalogue".
The bead did say to confirm against a game report, but the corpus was only searched after the fix
was built, and then to relocate a test rather than to check the premise.

**Cost.** One full rebuild of the approach after review. The navigator was asked to define
"unknown" (catalogue or report). Two more review rounds and two more CI cycles followed.

**Prevent by.** In `skills/fix-bug` step 1 ("Start from the bead's bug claim"): before choosing the
test, grep `tests/fixtures/reports/` for the claimed case in real orders, and read what the report
says about it. Separately, when a corpus floor in `crates/core/tests/` goes red under a fix, name
the fixture unit that moved before touching the floor.

**Seen before.** None found.
