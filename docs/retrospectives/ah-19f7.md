## The hover's own item list and the ledger disagree about what a unit holds as TRANSPORT runs

**What happened.** The plan had the hover keep deciding "holds none" from its own item list
(`apply_transport_phase`'s allowance) while Problems read the semantics ledger. The first parity
test on the committed G3 turn-42 fixture split them: after `GIVE 1162 ALL SILV`, the hover said
"moves nothing" and Problems named 100 SILV. Unit 683 has no month-long order, so it works by
default (`ah-gjq4`) for 12 silver, and WORK runs before TRANSPORT (`rules/sequenceofevents`); the
ledger counts that wage, the effects walk's item list does not. The hover was rewritten to read the
ledger's answer alone, and the plan's own human check (written from the synthetic fixture's
behaviour) had named the wrong sentence for that input, which the review caught.

**Why.** Established. The unit preview's item list is not the ledger's stock: it lacks what the
month adds before TRANSPORT that is silver-only bookkeeping in `semantics.rs` (wages here; the
`unshipped_silver` handover of `ah-7jsv` exists for the same reason). The synthetic
`shipping_quartermaster` fixture pays no wage, so tests there could not show it.

**Cost.** About twenty minutes and one rework of the hover's design inside the build; a wrong
validation line in the bead until the review.

**Prevent by.** A plan that makes the hover and Problems "say the same thing" about stock reads
the stock from one place, the semantics ledger, handed to `effects.rs` through `UnitItemEffects`
(as `unshipped_silver` and `nothing_to_ship` now are) — never the effects walk's own item list for
one and the ledger for the other. And a parity test for such a plan runs on a committed real
fixture (`crates/core/tests/the_hover_and_the_problem_say_the_same_thing.rs`), where idle units
earn wages, not only on a synthetic region.

**Seen before.** No.
