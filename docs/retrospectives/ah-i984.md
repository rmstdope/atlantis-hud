## The acceptance promised a Structure fit the real app never had

**What happened.** The acceptance says "Caravanserai [12] keeps the room it has today" in Structure, and rejected option B because B would cut that name. A planned smoke test measuring it in the real app (1400 px window, g7f95t71) failed on main's own widths: the name needs 133.1 px of content and Structure gave it 108.6 px before this bead. It was already cut with an ellipsis.

**Why.** The mockup `docs/ui/ah-i984-silver-hundreds.html` sets its columns in its own approximate fonts, and the acceptance's fit claims were read off it. The acceptance even says the real-app measurement decides the Silver width, but nothing measured the column the width was taken from.

**Cost.** One smoke run and a dropped test. A disclosure on the bead and PR. The rejected option's stated cost was already being paid, so the choice between A and B rested on a false premise.

**Prevent by.** In `agree-experience`, when the acceptance says a named string fits or is cut in a column at a given window width, take the claim from the real app (a measuring smoke run, as `tests/smoke/workspace.spec.ts`'s "has room for 999 beside 9999 short" does for Silver), not from the mockup.

**Seen before.** ah-ottr ("The default Silver column cannot hold the agreed long form"), ah-ykac ("The agreed mockup drew a cell the application does not draw").

## CI went red on a hover test that also fails on main

**What happened.** The `smoke (desktop-shell, 2, 2)` shard failed on "resting on a unit row summarises it" (panel bottom 805.6 px of a 720 px window). Main's own CI run on a docs-only commit (#1786) failed the same test with the same number. Locally it failed 8 of 15 runs with this bead's widths and 9 of 15 with main's.

**Why.** `TooltipPortal` measured and placed the panel once. Its content can grow after that first measure (most likely the forecast the core's worker now answers asynchronously since ah-iauy, though that link was not proven), and nothing placed it again. Fixed here with a ResizeObserver that re-runs placement: 20 of 20 runs passed afterwards.

**Cost.** One CI cycle, about 25 minutes of reproduction and fixing, and a follow-up review for a fix outside the bead.

**Prevent by.** A smoke test that grows a placed hover panel near the window's bottom edge and checks it stays on screen. Today the only cover is a test that hits the race about half the time.

**Seen before.** none found.
